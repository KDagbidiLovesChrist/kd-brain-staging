## CORE FRAMEWORK BANKS (grouped by framework, ready to ask, use in any consult before the per-type bank below)
The per-type banks below already weave these frameworks into project questions. This section is the
raw, standalone version of each framework, so the order and logic is visible on its own, not just
folded into a project type. Run SPIN as the spine of the session, cross-check with the Mom Test so
answers are real (not polite), and use JTBD to get the honest "why now". Sandler's Pain Funnel and
the 5 Whys (already threaded into STEP 2 of `consult.md`) are the deep-drill tools you reach for the
moment any of these surfaces a pain or a stated solution.

**SPIN bank** (Neil Rackham, built from 35,000 sales calls. Spend under 15% of the session on
Situation and over half on Implication + Need-payoff, most people over-ask Situation and never
build urgency):
- **Situation** (ask sparingly, 3-5 max, do the homework first so these aren't wasted): "Walk me
  through how you currently [sell the course / handle sign-ups / run this]." "What are you using
  right now to do this?"
- **Problem**: "What's not working about your current setup?" "Where does it break down?" "Is [X]
  difficult, slow, or costing you?"
- **Implication** (the urgency engine, ask MORE of these than any other type): "If this stays as it
  is, what does that do to your [launch revenue / time / reputation / next quarter]?" "How does that
  knock on to [X]?"
- **Need-payoff** (let the client sell the value to themselves): "How would it help if that were
  solved?" "What would a [site/system] that does X actually mean for you?"

**The Mom Test bank** (Rob Fitzpatrick. People lie to be nice, especially about your idea, so stop
talking about your solution and talk about their life and their past behaviour):
- Three rules underneath every question: talk about their life, not your idea; ask about specifics
  in the past, not generics or hypotheticals about the future; talk less, listen more.
- Instead of "Would you pay €X for a site that does Y?" ask: "What are you using now, and what did
  it cost you to set up?"
- Instead of "Is price an issue?" ask: "Walk me through the last time this was a problem." "What
  have you already tried to fix it?"
- Good anchors: "Why do you bother doing it that way?" "Talk me through your workflow." "When was
  the last time this happened?"
- The close (commitment, not compliments): know your ask before the call ends. Commitment escalates
  in three currencies, Time, then Reputation, then Money. "I'd definitely buy that" is worthless;
  "here's a deposit, tell me when it's ready" is everything. Anchor every compliment with a specific:
  "When did that last happen?"

**JTBD bank** (Bob Moesta and Chris Spiek's Switch Interview. Reconstruct the timeline of the
client's last "switch" and map the Four Forces of Progress, Push and Pull have to beat Anxiety and
Habit before change happens):
- "When did you first start thinking you needed a [course site / new system]?" (first thought)
- "What was going on that made the old way stop working?" (push, what's wrong with today)
- "What did you imagine the new solution would let you do?" (pull, the appeal of the new)
- "What worried you about switching, or about spending the money?" (anxiety, fear of the unproven)
- "What nearly made you just stick with what you had?" (habit, the comfort of staying put)
- "Walk me through the day you decided. Who else was involved? What did you do first?" (the full
  switch timeline, in their own words, not your agenda)

**Sandler Pain Funnel** (the canonical 8, verbatim, each one deepens the previous answer so it can't
be fully pre-scripted, run it the moment any pain surfaces): 1. "Tell me more about that." 2. "Can
you be more specific? Give me an example." 3. "How long has that been a problem?" 4. "What have you
tried to do about it?" 5. "Did that work?" 6. "How much do you think this has cost you?" 7. "How do
you feel about that?" 8. "Have you given up trying to fix it?" Stop when they name a specific cost
they can't tolerate.

## Question Banks (per project type)

Save as `workflow/consult_question_banks.md` (LAPTOP-ONLY: lives outside the synced brain, saved per client project, not in the repo). Each question below carries its **probe** (the follow-up that forces a specific answer).

**(a) WEBSITE / COURSE SITE**

*Vision/why*, "What's the single most important thing this site must do?" → *probe (5 Whys):* "Why does that matter? …and why's that?" until you hit the real need. · "Why now, what made you decide you need this site this month?" → *probe (JTBD):* "What was going on that made the old way stop working?"

*Who*, "Describe one real person who buys this, name, age, what they do." → *probe:* "Where do they find you, ads, YouTube, search, a referral?" · "What do they already know, and where do they get stuck?"

*Offer (course)*, "Finish this sentence: after this course they will be able to ___ that they couldn't before." → *probe:* "What proof do we have they'll get that, results, testimonials, your own story?" · "Price and model, one-off, payment plan, or tiers?" → *probe (Mom Test):* "What are similar courses charging, and what have you already sold anything for?"

*Look*, "Send me 2-3 course sites you love, and tell me the one thing you love about each." → *probe (anchor):* "When you say 'premium', point me at a page that nails that exact feeling." · "Send me 2-3 you can't stand." · "Logo, colours, fonts already, or building from scratch?"

*Scope/pages*, "List every page: home/sales, checkout, login, modules, about, FAQ." → *probe:* "What must each page DO, pay, play video, gate content, capture an email?" · "What's explicitly OUT for version 1?" (the most undervalued question)

*Content*, "Who writes the sales copy and supplies the videos, you or me?" → *probe:* "Is the course content already filmed and ready, or still being made?" (biggest hidden delay)

*Tech*, "Hosted platform (Whop/Skool/Kajabi) or a custom site?" → *probe:* "Who's the payment processor, what's the domain, and who needs to be able to edit it later, you alone?" · "Drip the modules or all-at-once? Login/membership area?"

*Funnel*, "Walk the path: how does a stranger become a buyer, lead magnet → landing → email → sales page → buy?" → *probe:* "What happens the second after they pay, onboarding, login email, first lesson?"

*Constraints/metrics*, "Budget range for the build, and a separate budget for upkeep?" · "Hard launch date or campaign driving it?" · "What number tells us it worked, sign-ups, sales in month one?"

**(b) FACELESS AI CONTENT CHANNEL**

*Vision/why*, "What's this channel FOR, money, audience, authority?" → *probe:* "What does success look like 90 days in, subs, views, or first revenue?" · "Why this niche?" → *probe (5 Whys):* drill the niche to a specific audience and emotion.

*Who/niche*, "Exactly who watches this, one person, what they feel when they watch?" → *probe:* "Which channels already win in this niche? Send me 3." · "What's the hook in the first 3 seconds of those?"

*Format/look*, "Show me 2-3 videos whose style we're matching, and the one thing that makes each work." → *probe (anchor):* "Pace, voice, captions, music, point at the exact reference for each." · "Vertical Shorts, long-form, or both?"

*Scope/cadence*, "How many videos a week, and how long each?" → *probe:* "Who approves before it posts, you, and how fast?" · "What's OUT, no faces, no copyrighted clips, no certain topics?" (faith-values filter applies)

*Content/pipeline*, "Where do topics come from, trends, a list, my research?" → *probe:* "Who writes the script, who does the voice, who picks the visuals?" · "What's the one thing only you can provide?"

*Tech*, "Which platforms, YouTube, TikTok, Insta?" → *probe:* "Auto-post (Postiz/Upload-Post) or you post manually?" · "Whose accounts, and who holds the logins?"

*Growth/money*, "How does this make money, ads, affiliates, a product, sponsors?" → *probe (Mom Test):* "Has anything in this niche been monetised before, show me." · "What's the call to action on each video?"

*Constraints/metrics*, "Budget per month for tools?" · "The number that matters, views, watch-time, subs, or revenue?" → *probe:* "By when?"

**(c) SOCIAL / CONTENT MANAGEMENT**

*Vision/why*, "What does this person/brand want from their social, sales, fame, bookings?" → *probe:* "Why now, what's not happening that should be?" (Pain Funnel if they name a frustration)

*Who*, "Who's the audience and who's the customer, same or different?" → *probe:* "Where do they buy/DM/book from now?"

*Brand/look*, "Send me 3 accounts in this space you'd want to look as good as." → *probe (anchor):* "What specifically, the editing, the captions, the consistency?" · "Existing brand colours, logo, fonts?"

*Scope*, "Which platforms, how many posts a week, what types, clips, carousels, stories?" → *probe:* "Who films/supplies raw material, them or us?" (e.g. Aunty snaps a clip, we edit) · "What's OUT, no certain topics, no certain platforms?"

*Workflow*, "What's the loop: they send raw → we edit/caption → who approves → we post?" → *probe:* "How fast must approval happen so we don't miss the trend window?" · "What's the call to action, 'DM to order', link in bio?"

*Tech*, "Whose accounts, who holds logins, scheduler or manual?" → *probe:* "Any tools they already pay for?"

*Constraints/metrics*, "Monthly retainer budget?" · "The win number, followers, DMs/orders, reach?" → *probe:* "Measured how, by when?"

**(d) AI AUTOMATION BUILD**

*Vision/why (the pain)*, "Walk me through how you do this today, step by step." → *probe (Pain Funnel):* "Where does it break? How long's that been a problem? What have you tried? Did it work? What's it costing you in hours/money?", stop when they name a cost they can't tolerate.

*Implication/urgency*, "If nothing changes, what does this cost you over the next quarter?" → *probe (SPIN need-payoff):* "If this were solved, what would that free you up to do?"

*Who/what*, "Who touches this process and who's the output for?" → *probe:* "How many times a day/week does it run? Volume at peak?"

*Scope*, "What exactly should the automation do, start to finish?" → *probe:* "What are the edge cases, the weird inputs, the days it goes wrong?" · "What's explicitly OUT for v1?"

*Tech/constraints*, "What systems does it have to plug into, email, sheets, CRM, calendar?" → *probe:* "Who has the logins/API keys? Any rules about where data can live (compliance)?" · "What happens on failure, retry, alert you, roll back?"

*Acceptance*, "How will we know it works, what's the test it has to pass?" → *probe:* write it as 'Given/When/Then' or a plain checklist. · "Who must be able to run/edit it without me?"

*Constraints/metrics*, "Budget for the build, and for keeping it running?" · "The number, hours saved, errors gone, money made?" → *probe:* "Measured how?"

## The No-Guessing Probe Cheat-Sheet

13 highest-leverage patterns. Pin this above the desk.

1. **"Walk me through the last time this happened."**, *use when* a client is enthusiastic but vague; kills hypotheticals by forcing real past behaviour.
2. **"What have you already tried, and what did it cost you?"**, *use when* you need the real budget, the real effort, and what already failed.
3. **"Why? …and why does that matter? …and why's that?" (×5)**, *use when* they hand you a SOLUTION ("I want animation") instead of a problem; drills to the real requirement.
4. **"If nothing changes, what does this cost you over the next quarter/launch?"**, *use when* you need urgency and a number attached to the pain.
5. **"Can you be more specific, give me one concrete example?"**, *use when* any vague word appears; the universal anchor.
6. **"Show me one site/account that nails what you mean by [premium/clean/modern]."**, *use when* a non-technical client describes a look in adjectives; turn words into a reference.
7. **"After this, they'll be able to ___ that they couldn't before."**, *use when* scoping a course or any offer; the transformation IS the product.
8. **"What would solving this actually let you do?"**, *use when* you want the client to state the value themselves instead of you arguing it.
9. **"When did you first start thinking you needed this, and what triggered it?"**, *use when* you need the real 'why now' and the push behind the project.
10. **"What's worried you about going ahead or spending this?"**, *use when* you sense a hidden blocker; surfaces the anxiety that derails launches before it bites.
11. **"What does *done* look like, how will you know this worked?"**, *use when* you need the acceptance test / pass-fail; nothing is LOCKED without this.
12. **"What do you NOT want, anything that's a deal-breaker?"**, *use when* scoping; negative space catches wrong assumptions before they're built.
13. **"Let me read this back to make sure I've got it exactly: [A, B, C; not D; budget E; deadline F]. What did I get wrong?"**, *use at* the end of every topic and every session; THE lock-the-spec move that feeds the WAT build prompt.

**The one rule that ties them together:** after every session, sort every answer into LOCKED (specific + confirmed by playback) vs OPEN. Open items become the next session's questions. Only LOCKED items go into the build. That sorting is the "zero guessing" guarantee, and the whole reason this system can run over hours or days without ever losing the thread.
