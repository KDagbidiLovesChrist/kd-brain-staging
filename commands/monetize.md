### /monetize qualify: [client description]
Qualify whether a prospect is worth pursuing.

Ask these questions:
1. **Problem:** What's the specific problem they have? (Not "they need AI", what is the actual pain?)
2. **Cost of problem:** How much time/money does it cost them per month?
3. **Decision maker:** Are they the one who can say yes and pay?
4. **Budget:** Can they afford a real solution? (Rough gauge: turnover, team size)
5. **Urgency:** Why now? What's driving them to solve this?

**Scoring:**
- Problem worth solving? (yes/no)
- Cost > €500/month? (is the value there for a real engagement?)
- Decision maker? (yes/no)
- Verdict: Pursue / Discovery call needed / Pass

### /monetize roi: [their situation]
Calculate the ROI to justify value-based pricing.

**ROI Calculator:**
```
1. How many hours/week does the current problem waste them?
   Hours wasted per week: ___

2. What is their time worth? (rough hourly rate based on role/business)
   Hourly value: €___

3. Calculate monthly cost of the problem:
   Hours × hourly rate × 4 weeks = €___ per month lost

4. Annual cost:
   Monthly loss × 12 = €___ per year

5. King David's price = 15, 25% of annual value delivered
   (i.e., if the system saves them €24,000/year, charge €3,600, €6,000)

6. Present it as: "This system pays for itself in [X weeks]."
```

Turn a completed project into a long-term relationship.

**The expansion playbook (Nate's $3K to $50K/year framework):**
1. **Track the metrics.** After delivery, document the actual results:
