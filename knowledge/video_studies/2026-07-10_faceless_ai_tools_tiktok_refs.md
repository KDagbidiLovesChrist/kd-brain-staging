## The shared DNA (the formula they ALL use)
1. **Hook in first 3s**, one of three proven types:
   - **Unexpected analogy** ("the Sims for AI agents")
   - **Leak / secret / exclusive** ("they leaked the entire prompt")
   - **Speed-value promise** ("X big outcome in 30 seconds")
   - (plus **pain-point** "it stops you mid-build")
2. **Fast cuts, 1-2s**, fastest during the how-to steps. Constant visual change = retention.
3. **Captions:** white bold sans-serif, centered, **key words highlighted** (yellow/orange gradient, red, teal). Readable with sound off.
4. **Show-don't-tell:** screen-records dominate; text overlays label every action ("Click here", "Paste this").
5. **Upbeat music** matched to the vibe (Sims music for the Sims analogy = extra points).
6. **Structure:** HOOK -> BUILD (2-3 steps) -> PAYOFF (the outcome/benefit visual) -> CTA.
7. **CTA = keyword comment/DM** ("OPEN", "Fable 5", "BRAIN") to get the resource. This is a LEAD-CAPTURE mechanic, not just engagement, 3 of 4 use it.
