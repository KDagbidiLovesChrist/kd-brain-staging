    *   **Accent Color for Text**: Use the primary purple (`#6A25FF`) or a vibrant accent color for specific words to highlight.

3.  **Line Animations (Crucial Detail)**:
    *   **Connecting Lines (Dashed)**:
        *   **Stroke**: 2-4px white or light purple.
        *   **Dashes**: Set dash to 10-15px, gap to 5-10px.
        *   **Animation**: Animate "Offset" of the dashes over time to create a "marching ants" or flowing effect along a path. Or animate "Trim Paths" with **Ease Out** and a slight delay between lines if multiple.
        *   **Path**: Use custom bezier paths to connect elements dynamically.
    *   **Waveforms/Ribbons**: Use multiple layers of thick (10-20px) lines with slightly different wave frequencies and amplitudes, moving horizontally. Use primary purple, yellow, cyan, pink. Apply `Trim Paths` or `Wave Warp` effects for organic motion.

    *   **Internal UI Elements**: Individual UI components (chat bubbles, buttons, videos, data cards) should animate into place *within* the app screen.

        *   **Buttons/Icons**: Scale up slightly with a quick bounce, or fade in with a subtle parallax.
