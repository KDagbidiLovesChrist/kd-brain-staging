## 9. WHAT LOOKS DATED OR INEFFECTIVE FOR 2026
-   **Static, Unvaried Shots:** For purely visual content, long holds on a single, static shot, even if well-lit, can feel slow and unengaging for a 2026 audience accustomed to dynamic, fast-paced edits. The video itself, while informative, relies heavily on a static frame of the speaker which might need more visual variation if it were purely entertainment/branding.
-   **Random or Irrelevant Visual "Junk" (15:39):** Adding emojis or GIFs that don't directly enhance the message or relate to the coffee topic (e.g., a dancing clown in a coffee brewing tutorial) will likely be perceived as low-effort or clickbait. All visual elements should serve a clear purpose to avoid looking unprofessional.
-   **Overly Aggressive "Spilling the Tea" (20:07):** While hot takes can work, giving away *all* the solutions or "secrets" right in the hook removes any reason for the viewer to watch the rest of the video, leading to low retention which algorithms penalize.
