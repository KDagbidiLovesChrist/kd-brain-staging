# The objective lesson

A TikTok creator (@structurewebworks) demos a four-stage automated lead system for local service businesses, branded "Claude AI Agent," offered free in exchange for a comment. Watched in full via Gemini, not summary-only.

## The four stages shown
1. **Lead capture and instant response.** Website form, SMS/chat, or missed call all funnel into an AI intake. Valid leads get an SMS reply in under 10 seconds, which books the appointment directly in the same thread.
2. **14-day automated follow-up.** If a lead doesn't respond: Day 1 SMS, Day 4 email, Day 9 SMS+email, Day 14 final message, then flagged for manual review. Any response at any point moves them straight to booking.
3. **Owner brief delivery.** Once an appointment's confirmed, the agent reads the full conversation and auto-generates a brief: lead name, phone, source, service requested, appointment time, and a suggested quote range.
4. **Review request and win-back.** On job completion, auto-sends a review request, chases after 3 days if none left. Separately scans past customers for 90+ day inactivity and sends a personalised win-back message.

The dashboard shown ("Command Center") tracks: leads today, average response time, booking rate, a conversion funnel (new lead → AI responded → engaged → booked), and a live activity feed.

## What's substantiated vs hype
Substantiated: the flow logic, the example outputs (a real-looking SMS exchange, a real-looking brief pop-up), and that this category of automation is well-established generally. Hype: the "$30,000 value" claim has no backing, the dashboard's specific numbers (47 leads, 8s response, 9% booking rate) are shown with no methodology or timeframe, and "free" doesn't specify what's actually being handed over (a template, a consult, or a working system).
