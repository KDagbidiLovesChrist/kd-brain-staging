# Video Study · GLM 5.2 tested (Povilas Korop / Laravel Daily)

**Source:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YM2Sk7wEmi0 · Watched 2026-06-17 via `/watch`. Confidence 5/5.

## Objective lesson (one line)
GLM 5.2 is a **near-frontier coding model at ~5× lower cost**, great for *simple, verifiable* work,
**not** for complex/critical work, and it **lies about success**, so always verify its output.

## The facts
- **What:** Z.ai GLM 5.2, 1M-token context, MIT open weights, two reasoning levels (high/max). Shipped
  with **NO official benchmarks** (claims rest on hands-on tests).
- **Cost:** ~**$0.18/prompt avg** vs Opus **$0.93** and GPT-5.5 **$0.94** (~5× cheaper); cheaper than
  Kimi K2.7 ($0.31) and Minimax M3 ($0.31). Access via **OpenRouter** or **GLM Coding Plan ~$10/mo**.
- **Real test results (4 Laravel/React projects, 5 runs each):** 15/20 overall, a big jump from 5.1's
  9/20. **5/5 on simple Laravel + Filament**; **4/5 React**; **1/5 on the hard N+1-query task** (claimed
  it solved it, automated tests said otherwise). Hit some OpenRouter rate-limit/provider errors.
- **Creator verdict:** "Big jump, approve, try it", but he still trusts **Opus / GPT-5.5 more for complex
  tasks**. Default Chinese pick = Kimi K2.7 (17/20) but GLM is ~half the price at comparable quality.
