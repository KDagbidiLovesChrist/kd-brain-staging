# 2 · Cloudware · what the three become assembled

**Cloudware is not a fourth thing sitting beside hardware, software and coldware. It is what they
become when they are put together and switched on.**

```
        HW  +  SW  +  CW   =   CLOUDWARE
```

---

## 2.1 Why this is a real claim and not a slogan

Take any one away and nothing happens.

**Hardware alone** is a warm box. Your laptop with no brain on it is a laptop.

**Software alone** is instructions nobody is executing. `drive_mirror.py` on a USB stick is a text
file.

**Coldware alone** is a shelf. The drive holds 8.3 GB of models and 5,003 files and does nothing
at all.

**Together they are an estate that runs**: it reads, it decides, it writes, it remembers, and it
keeps doing so while you sleep.

## 2.3 The test that makes it more than a picture

**The claim is falsifiable, and it has been tested twice by accident, in opposite directions.**

**Oracle, from the top.** It has hardware and it has software and it has almost no files it may
read: **7 files, 52 KB**, against 8,524 on the laptop. Two of the three. The formula says such a
box cannot correct and build. **Three of its four agents refuse.**

**The drive, from the bottom.** It has more knowledge than Oracle and 8.3 GB of models, and no
machine. **It does nothing at all.**

Two out of three fails in both directions. The formula survived a test it did not ask for.

---

## 2.4 What this buys you in practice

Once you can see the three states, three things stop being mysterious.

**Why Oracle refuses.** It is not broken and it is not misconfigured. It is missing an input, on
purpose, because letting it read the whole brain would put your faith files and cap tables on
someone else's computer.

**Why the drive is safe to carry.** It cannot run, so it cannot leak. The keys on it are
encrypted anyway, but the inertness is the first line, not the second.

**Why a restore needs both cold stores.** GitHub has the history and no keys. The drive has the
keys and is a copy. **Either alone leaves you rebuilding something by hand.**

---

## 2.5 The word, said carefully

Inside this estate, "cloudware" means the assembled whole and it is a useful word.

**Outside, it means almost the opposite.** To a buyer, cloudware is software delivered from
someone else's cloud. Your estate is deliberately local, sealed and floor-first: the exact
opposite of what they will picture.

That is not a reason to drop your word. It is a reason to gloss it once in any room where money
is involved: *"by cloudware I mean the whole estate assembled, most of it running on hardware I
control."*
