# 3 · The equation · knowledge to wisdom to logos to power

**This is your own formula. It is the most useful thing in the whole estate, because it explains
what every machine can and cannot do without having to inspect any of them.**

```
   KNOWLEDGE  ──+ MACHINE ──►  WISDOM  ──+ LOGIC ──►  LOGOS  =  POWER
```

---

## 3.1 In plain words

**KNOWLEDGE** is what you gathered. Files. Notes, transcripts, decisions, code. It cannot act and
it cannot reason. A library with the lights off.

**Add a MACHINE** and it becomes **WISDOM**: knowledge alive. Now it can be read, searched, served,
indexed. The lights are on. Still nobody in the building.

**Add LOGIC** and it becomes **LOGOS**. Logic is the part that reasons: **the language model**. It
reads the workflow, takes the role, picks up the tool. Now there is a librarian who understands
the shelves and can rewrite them.

**That is POWER**, and power here means one specific thing: **it acts without you at the keys.**

---

## 3.2 The naming, which needed a ruling and got one

On 9 September you said: *"Logic is a llm its a smart system"*, and *"if you connected it to a
machine with files and folders it will correct and build it"*.

That contradicted `CLAUDE.md`, which said Logic is the `/command` skills layer. On 10 September you
settled it, and the settlement is better than either half:

| | claim | verdict |
|---|---|---|
| **the function** | the LLM is what reasons; skills are instructions and cannot | **true, as engineering** |
| **the name** | "Logic", rooted in the Logos, belongs to the skills, your own written word | **true, as naming** |

**They were never about the same thing.** So the sacred name stays where it was, the machines get
a plain engineering label (**LOGIC-A**, **LOGIC-B**), and `_ops/CREED.md` is untouched. No machine
gets named after Christ the Word as a side effect of an engineering correction.

## 3.3 The prediction it made, before it was written down

A formula that only describes is decoration. This one **predicts**, and the prediction was tested
twice by accident.

**Oracle.** Machine, yes. Logic, yes. Knowledge: **7 files, 52 KB.** Two of three. The formula
says it cannot correct and build. **Three of its four agents refuse.**

**The drive.** Knowledge, more than Oracle. Logic, 8.3 GB of models sitting right there. Machine:
none. **It does nothing.**

**Two out of three fails in both directions, and it fails differently each way.** Oracle refuses
loudly, with an audit line. The drive refuses silently, by being a drawer.

## 3.5 What follows from it, immediately

Once you hold the equation, several questions answer themselves without investigation.

**"Why can't Oracle do X?"** Count its three. If knowledge is missing for X, it cannot.

**"Is it safe to put the drive in my bag?"** It has no machine. It cannot run, so it cannot leak.

**"Will a second cheap box make things faster?"** No. It adds a machine, and you are not short of
machines. Speed comes from RAM, retrieval and routing.

**"What does a client actually buy?"** Not the model. They can get a model anywhere. They buy the
assembly, and the governance around it. Part 9 and part 12.
