## 0.3 Two rules this map runs on

**One: every part says what is real, what is half built, and what is zero.**

Not for tidiness. An asset register written at commissioning still says the generator is available
long after it went on manual bypass, because nobody goes back to edit a document that already says
something reassuring. **Documents drift toward the version you wish were true**, not by lying but
by not being updated, and by the writer quietly choosing the kinder sentence.

So each part is forced to name its own gaps, and **a part that cannot say "this bit is zero" is not
finished.**

**Two: anything that can go stale is measured, not written.**

A whiteboard reading "all systems normal" was true when someone wrote it and is connected to
nothing. The BMS screen beside it cannot be out of date, because it is not a statement, it is a
measurement.

So the prose here is written by hand, because it has to be good, and every count is generated by a
script, because it has to be true. You see that state on the **P&P board**, as stage `SB`, fourteen
rows filling in as the work happens.

```
knowledge/KD_STORYBOARD.md          the spine. the equation, the 14 parts, the halls.
knowledge/storyboard/00 .. 13.md    one file per part. this is 00.
tools/progress_board_iotas_storyboard.py    the STATE of each part
```

## 0.5 The five words you need before anything else

You already invented all of these. They are collected here so the rest reads smoothly.

**HW, hardware.** Powered, connected, able to act right now. Your laptop, Oracle's box, your phone.

**SW, software.** The instructions. The engine, the tools, the hooks, the scheduled jobs.

**CW, coldware.** Real, and inert. **GitHub and the hard drive.** Both hold everything. Neither
reasons or acts. A drive in a drawer is a complete component with one input missing on purpose,
which is exactly why it cannot leak.

**Cloudware.** Not a fourth thing beside the other three. **What they become when assembled and
switched on.**

**Logic.** The part that reasons: the language model. Not the skills. The skills are instructions,
and instructions do not reason; Logic reads them and reasons. *(The NAME "Logic", rooted in the
Logos, stays with the skills. Your ruling, 10 September. Part 3 explains why that is not a
contradiction.)*
