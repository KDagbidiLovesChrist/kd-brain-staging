## TRACK C · Cash Cow SEO

### What "cash cow SEO" actually means
Tag research · title optimization · thumbnail A/B · description with timestamps · hashtags · end screen · cards · channel keywords + recurring publishing rhythm.

### Fiverr SEO providers (bundled with cash cow editing)
Most cash-cow Fiverr gigs already **include** SEO in the package, separate SEO-only gigs are weaker value. Editors with bundled SEO:
- **mubashirfolk** ($165), explicit "thumbnails and SEO" in gig title
- **abuzarghaffarii** ($25), bundles script + VO + edit + thumbnail + SEO
- **robertmarzan** ($15), bundled SEO + thumbnail
- **mr_sheff** ($10), "high CPM niche selection" + SEO in package

**Recommendation:** Don't buy a stand-alone SEO gig. Use the bundled SEO from whichever editor you hire, then verify titles/tags yourself with TubeBuddy Free.

### Paid SEO tools · what each tier does

**TubeBuddy** (winner on price + A/B testing, only tool of the two with thumbnail A/B):
- Free: keyword explorer + basic optimization
- **Pro $3.50/mo (annual), $1.75/mo if channel <1k subs**, bulk tools, advanced optimization
- Legend ~$27/mo (annual), A/B testing thumbnails/titles/descriptions, bulk processing

**VidIQ** (winner on keyword research depth + AI ideation):
- Free: basic keyword score + volume estimates
- Boost **$16.58/mo (annual)**, Keyword Inspector with search volume, competition, trending predictions
- Coaching $99/mo (annual) or $199/mo monthly, structured creator coaching

**Cash cow SEO checklist (free first, paid later):**

1. **Keyword research:** YouTube Autocomplete (type your topic, screenshot suggestions) + Google Trends (free) + VidIQ Free score check + TubeBuddy Free keyword difficulty.
2. **Title:** Front-load primary keyword, include curiosity hook ("How I Made $10k With AI in 30 Days"), <70 chars.
3. **Thumbnail:** Use TubeBuddy free thumbnail analyzer (AI prediction). A/B test once on Pro ($3.50/mo).
4. **Description:** 200+ words, primary keyword in first 25 words, **timestamps every 30, 60 sec**, 3, 5 related links, channel CTA.
5. **Tags:** 8, 15 tags, mix broad ("AI side hustle") + specific ("ChatGPT prompts for beginners") + brand ("Built with AI"). Pull from VidIQ Free competitor tag inspector.
6. **Hashtags:** 3 max in description (#aisidehustle #builtwithai #passiveincome).
7. **End screen:** Always point to the next video (algorithmic gold).
8. **Cards:** 2, 4 cards mid-video pointing to playlists.
9. **Channel keywords:** Set in YouTube Studio → Settings → Channel → Basic info. 8, 12 words/phrases describing the niche.
10. **Cadence:** Algorithm rewards consistency, same day, same time, weekly minimum.

**Best free SEO method for a new channel:**
YouTube Autocomplete + Google Trends + VidIQ Free Chrome extension + TubeBuddy Free Chrome extension. Total cost: $0. Will carry you to ~1k subs before paid tools become worth it.
