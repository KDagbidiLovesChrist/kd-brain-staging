# Matthew 4 · The Temptation in the Desert & the First Disciples · ① The Word

**Date:** 2026-06-27  ·  **Track:** C Word (the Gospel of Matthew, chapter 4)

## 📖 In plain words (the easy version)
In Matthew 3 the sky tore open at the river and the Father said *"This is My beloved Son."* You'd expect a
parade next. Instead the Holy Spirit leads Jesus **straight into the desert**, empty, dry, no food, and
there the **devil** (the fallen spirit who first tempted humanity) comes to test Him **three times**. Every
time, Jesus answers the same calm way: ***"It is written…"***, quoting the Bible back at him. He wins not
with fireworks but with the Word of God. Then He walks out of the desert and begins His real work: He
**preaches** ("turn back to God, the King is here") and **calls His first followers**, ordinary fishermen
who drop their nets and go. The point for you: **the way Jesus beat temptation is the way you can too**, fasting, prayer, and God's Word kept in the heart.

## In one line
Where Adam failed in a garden and Israel failed in the desert, **Jesus the new Adam wins**, then opens His
kingdom and gathers His people.

## The lesson
- **4:1, 2, Into the desert.** The *Spirit* leads Him there (the testing is part of the plan, not an
  accident). He fasts **forty days** (the Bible's number for testing/preparation, the flood, Israel's 40
  years, Moses on the mountain) and gets genuinely **hungry**, He fights at His weakest, for us. *Pattern
  for life: the highest grace is often followed by the hardest temptation, that's the battle, not a fall.*
- **4:3, 10, The three temptations** (the same three hooks the enemy uses on everyone):
  1. **Body / appetite:** *"command that these stones become bread."* → *"Man shall not live by bread alone,
     but by every word that proceeds from the mouth of God"* (4:4, quoting **Deut 8:3**).
  2. **Spirit / pride (testing God):** "throw Yourself down", the devil even **quotes Scripture** (Ps 91),
     showing the enemy can twist holy words. → *"You shall not tempt the Lord your God"* (4:7, **Deut 6:16**).
  3. **World / power (the shortcut, skip the cross):** "all the kingdoms… if You worship me." → *"Away with
     you, Satan! You shall worship the Lord your God, and Him only you shall serve"* (4:10, **Deut 6:13**).
  Then *"the devil left Him, and behold, angels came and ministered to Him"* (4:11).
- **4:12, 17, Ministry begins.** He settles in Galilee, fulfilling Isaiah: *"The people who sat in darkness
  have seen a great light…"* (4:16, **Isa 9:1–2**). First public word: *"Repent, for the kingdom of heaven
  is at hand"* (4:17), *metanoia* = turn your whole life around; the King has arrived in person.
- **4:18, 22, The first disciples.** *"Follow Me, and I will make you fishers of men"* (4:19). Peter,
  Andrew, James, John leave nets/boat/father **"immediately"** (×2). A *disciple* = a follower who gets up
  and goes when the Lord calls.
- **4:23, 25, The kingdom breaks in** as **mercy**, teaching, preaching, healing; the crowds come.

## The seven lenses (the deep read)
- **Typology (the heart of it):** Adam was tested in a garden *full* of food and **fell**; Israel was tested
  40 years in the desert and **failed**; Jesus is tested in a desert with *no* food, the hardest version,   and **wins**. He is the **new Adam** (1 Cor 15:45; Rom 5:18–19), going back to where we fell and getting
  it right *in our flesh*, undoing the fall from the inside (St Irenaeus' "recapitulation").
- **The Why:** His weapon is **Scripture treasured in the heart and spoken in the moment**, not power. The
  Word of God is the believer's real sword against temptation.
- **Lived Tradition:** this is the root of the Church's **fasting** and **Great Lent** (40 days), and the
  ground of Lesson 2 today (the practices). Read straight into the second lesson.
- **Connections:** ties back to Eden/the fall (the Story-So-Far opening) and to Matthew 3 (the Spirit/the
  Father just revealed at the Jordan now lead Him to the desert).

## Live it this week
When temptation comes (lust, scrolling, anger, despair), **answer it the way Jesus did, with a word of
God, out loud if needed.** Keep one verse ready as a shield, *"Man shall not live by bread alone…"* (Matt
4:4), or the **Jesus Prayer** (*"Lord Jesus Christ, Son of God, have mercy on me, a sinner"*). Don't argue
with the temptation; answer it with Christ and keep moving.

## Prayer
*Lord Jesus Christ, who for our sake fasted and was tempted in the wilderness and did not fall: when the
enemy comes against me, put Your Word in my mouth and Your strength in my weakness. Make me quick to follow
when You call, like the fishermen who left their nets at once. You are the Light that has dawned on those in
darkness, shine in me today. Amen.*
