# Theology · Knowing the Truth, and the Truth is a Person

**Date:** 2026-07-01 (late night, after King prayed) · **Lesson #33**

**Born from King's words:** *"I just want to know Truth and spread Truth."*

---

## 📖 In plain words (easy version)
"Theology" sounds like a huge school word. It is only two Greek words joined: **theos** (God) and
**logos** (word), "knowing God, and speaking of Him." But here is the Orthodox surprise, and it is
exactly your heart: theology is **not** knowing *about* God like facts in a book. It is **knowing God**
the way you know a person you love. Because the Truth you want is not a thing. It is **Him**.

---

## The teaching

### 1. The Truth is a Person, not just facts
Jesus said, **"I am the way, the truth, and the life. No one comes to the Father except through Me"**
(John 14:6, NKJV). And when He promised, **"you shall know the truth, and the truth shall make you free"**
(John 8:32), He had said the key thing one verse before: **"If you abide in My word, you are My disciples
indeed"** (John 8:31). So to *know the Truth* = to **stay close to Christ**. Truth is not information you
collect; it is a Person you cling to.

### 2. What "theology" really means in the Church
The word **theologia** = *theos* (God) + *logos* (word). But in the Orthodox tradition it was never
academic. It means **knowing God personally**, through prayer and a pure heart. That is why the Church is
so careful with the title **"the Theologian."** In two thousand years only **three** saints carry it:
**St John the Theologian** (the Apostle John), **St Gregory the Theologian** (Gregory of Nazianzus,
4th c.), and **St Symeon the New Theologian** (11th c.). Not the cleverest, the ones **closest to God**.
As the desert father **Evagrius** wrote: *"If you are a theologian, you will pray truly; and if you pray
truly, you are a theologian"* (**On Prayer**, ch. 60; transmitted in the Philokalia). You already stepped
through that door tonight, King. **You prayed.** That is where real theology begins.

### 3. Spreading the Truth is the SAME road as knowing it
Your second wish, to spread it, is not a second task. Because the Truth is a **Person**, you do not
spread Him mainly by winning arguments. You spread Him by **becoming light**:
- **"Let your light so shine before men, that they may see your good works and glorify your Father in
  heaven"** (Matthew 5:16). The strongest sermon is a changed life.
- Be ready to explain your hope, but **gently**: *"always be ready to give a defense (**apologia**) to
  everyone who asks you a reason for the hope that is in you, with meekness and fear"* (1 Peter 3:15).
- **St Seraphim of Sarov:** *"Acquire a peaceful spirit, and thousands around you will be saved."* Become
  holy, and people are drawn to Christ through you without a word.

So your two desires are **one road**. To **know** the Truth is to draw close to Christ; and the closer you
get, the more your life **shines Him** onto everyone near you. **Knowing IS spreading.** You never have to
choose between them.

## ✍️ God the Author (the craft + the key word)
- The Greek for truth, **alētheia** (ἀλήθεια), literally means **"the un-hidden / un-concealed"** (*a-*
  "not" + *lēthē* "hiding/forgetting"). Truth is what has been **pulled out of hiding**. So when Christ
  "declares" the unseen Father, *"No one has seen God at any time. The only begotten Son... has declared
  Him"* (John 1:18) — He is literally God **un-hidden**. To know the Truth is to see the God who was hidden.
- **apologia** (1 Pet 3:15) = a reasoned "word back," a gentle account — the root of "apologetics." Ready,
  never rude.
