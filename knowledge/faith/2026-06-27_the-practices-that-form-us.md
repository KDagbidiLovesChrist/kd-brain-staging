# The Practices That Form Us · ② The Tradition

**Date:** 2026-06-27  ·  **Track:** D Praxis (the Orthodox way, how the faith is lived)

## 📖 In plain words (the easy version)
Jesus beat the devil by **fasting, praying, and using God's Word** (Lesson 1 today). The Orthodox faith
isn't only things to *believe* in your head, it's a whole **way of living** that slowly re-shapes you into
the likeness of Christ, the way training re-shapes an athlete. These are the **practices** (*ascesis* =
"training"). Five big ones: **fasting** (saying no to the body so you can say yes to God), the **feasts &
calendar** (living the year as Christ's life), the **prayer rule** (a small steady daily prayer), **Confession**
(bringing sins to God before a priest, and being healed), and **icons & a prayer corner** (a holy place at
home). None of them *earn* God's love, He already loves you. They're the **gym** where that love makes you
new. *How much / how fast* you take them on is **Fr Bogdan's** call, not the study aid's.

## In one line
The faith is not just believed, it's **practised**, and the practices are how grace turns a believer into a saint.

## The lesson · the five rhythms (why before how)
- **🍞 Fasting.** Not hating food or self-punishment, **training the will** so appetites stop being master.
  It frees the heart for God, joins us to Christ in the desert, and is a weapon against the passions
  (Chrysostom: "a most powerful shield against the devil"). Rhythm (FYI, *rule is Fr Bogdan's*): most
  **Wednesdays** (the betrayal) and **Fridays** (the crucifixion), plus four seasons, **Great Lent**,
  **Nativity**, **Apostles'**, **Dormition**. Always paired with more prayer + almsgiving, never alone.
- **📅 Feasts & the Church calendar.** The year is a **wheel** through the whole salvation story, the
  **Twelve Great Feasts**, with **Pascha (the Resurrection) at the centre**, the Feast of Feasts. We don't
  just remember; we **re-enter** (e.g. bless water at Theophany, Jan 6). **Time itself becomes holy.**
- **🙏 The prayer rule.** A set, repeatable **morning & evening** prayer (from an Orthodox prayer book) +
  the **Jesus Prayer** through the day. Feelings come and go; a rule keeps the fire lit. **Small and steady
  beats big and heroic.** (King has already asked Fr Bogdan to set his rule, let him.)
- **✝️ Confession.** Naming sins out loud to God **before a priest**, who pronounces God's forgiveness. A
  **hospital, not a courtroom**; the priest is witness + physician, *with* you, not a judge over you. You
  leave clean, and clean is the soil change grows in. *Pastoral → Fr Bogdan* (King wants to come; bring the
  real things honestly — lust, Jess, family). **"No condemnation"** (Rom 8:1); bring it, don't bury it.
- **🖼️ Icons & the prayer corner.** We do **not** worship icons (worship = God alone); we **venerate** them
  (honour, a bow, a kiss), like kissing a photo of someone loved. **St Basil:** *"the honour shown to the
  image passes to the prototype"*, the honour goes *through* the icon to the real person. Images are
  possible because **God became visible in Christ** (defended at the **7th Ecumenical Council, Nicaea, 787**).
  A home prayer corner (icon + lamp/candle + prayer book) is a **doorway**, a fixed place that says "here I meet God."

## The backing (verified)
- Scripture: Matt 4:2 (Christ fasts); Matt 6:16–18 (*"when you fast"* — Jesus assumes we will); 1 Thess 5:17
  (*"pray without ceasing"*); James 5:16 (confess to one another); John 20:23 (apostles given authority to
  forgive sins); Rom 8:1 (no condemnation).
- Father / Council: St John Chrysostom (fasting as a shield); **St Basil the Great** ("honour passes to the
  prototype"), affirmed at the **7th Ecumenical Council (Nicaea II, 787)**.
- Lived Tradition: the Twelve Great Feasts with Pascha at the centre; Wed/Fri fasts + four seasons;
  morning/evening prayers + the Jesus Prayer; Confession; the home prayer corner.

## Live it this week
**Set up (or tidy) a small prayer corner**, even one icon and a candle on a shelf, facing east if you can, and stand there for morning and evening prayer instead of praying "on the move." Give your body a *place*
that means "now I'm with God." The simplest of the five; it quietly anchors the rest.

## Prayer
*O Lord, You have given me not only truth to believe but a way to walk. Teach me to fast without pride, to
pray without ceasing, to keep Your feasts with joy, to confess without fear, and to honour Your holy ones.
Make my home a little church and my heart a quiet altar. Form me, Lord, make me Yours. Through the prayers
of the Theotokos and all the saints. Amen.*
