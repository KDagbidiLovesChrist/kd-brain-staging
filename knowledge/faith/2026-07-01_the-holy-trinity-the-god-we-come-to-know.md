# The Holy Trinity · the God We Come to Know

**Date:** 2026-07-01 (late, continuing from lesson #33) · **Lesson #34**
**Track:** 🧠 Theology (the doctrine of God) + 🕊️ Formation
**Flows from #33:** theology = knowing God → so **who is the God we know?** The Holy Trinity.

---

## 📖 In plain words (easy version)
The whole Church stakes everything on this: **one God, in three Persons, Father, Son, and Holy Spirit.**
Not three gods. Not one God wearing three masks (that is the heresy of *modalism*). One God, three Persons,
forever. He is not a lonely One; He is a **communion of Love**, and you are invited in.

---

## The teaching

### 1. One God
Not polytheism. **"Hear, O Israel: The LORD our God, the LORD is one"** (Deuteronomy 6:4, the Shema).

### 2. Three Persons · shown at the Jordan
The clearest picture is the **Theophany** (which King already studied, Matthew 3): at Christ's Baptism all
Three appear at once, the **Son** in the water, the **Spirit** as a dove, the **Father's** voice, *"This is
My beloved Son"* (Matthew 3:16-17). And Christ's own command: baptize *"in the **name** of the Father and of
the Son and of the Holy Spirit"* (Matthew 28:19) — one **name** (one God), three Persons.

### 3. One essence, three Persons
The Cappadocian Fathers (St Basil the Great, St Gregory the Theologian / of Nazianzus, St Gregory of Nyssa)
gave the language: **one essence** (*ousia*, the "what") and **three Persons** (*hypostaseis*, the "whos").
Analogies all limp (water/ice/steam = modalism; shamrock = tritheism); the reality is greater than any picture.

### 4. The relationships (the eternal life of God)
- The **Father** is the source, unbegotten (the "Monarchy of the Father").
- The **Son** is **eternally begotten** of the Father, *"begotten, not made"* (Nicene Creed); the Word who
  *"was with God, and was God"* (John 1:1); *"the only begotten"* (John 1:18).
- The **Holy Spirit** **eternally proceeds from the Father** (John 15:26).
- **Not a timeline.** No Person is before or greater than another; all three co-equal, co-eternal, of one
  essence, undivided. They indwell one another in love, *perichoresis* (mutual indwelling; St John of
  Damascus) — *"I am in the Father, and the Father in Me"* (John 14:11).

### 5. Why it is the most beautiful truth (the heart)
**"God is love"** (1 John 4:8). But love needs a beloved. A single, solitary Person could not be love *in
Himself*, He would need to create someone first. But God is **already** a communion: the Father loving the
Son in the Spirit, before anything existed. **So God did not create out of loneliness or need. He is a
fullness of love, and He made us to SHARE it.** And **theosis** (the goal of everything, #33) = being drawn
**into** that circle of love. What we "spread" is not a doctrine but an **invitation into Love Himself.**

### 6. Guarded by the Councils
- **Nicaea I (325):** against Arius (who said the Son was created), the Son is *homoousios*, **of one
  essence** with the Father.
- **Constantinople I (381):** confirmed the full divinity of the Holy Spirit; completed the
  **Nicene-Constantinopolitan Creed** we still confess.

### 7. A mystery we enter, not a puzzle we solve (apophatic)
St Gregory the Theologian: *"No sooner do I conceive of the One than I am illumined by the splendour of the
Three; no sooner do I distinguish Them than I am carried back to the One"* (Oration 40, On Holy Baptism).
Ties to tonight's brain-truth: **the limit is what God allows**, we know Him truly, never exhaustively.

## ✍️ God the Author (the craft + key words)
- **Key words:** *ousia* (essence), *hypostasis* (person), *homoousios* (of one essence, Nicaea),
  *perichoresis* (mutual indwelling).
- **OT shadows → NT un-hiding** (*alētheia*, #33): *"Let **Us** make man in **Our** image"* (Genesis 1:26);
  the three visitors to Abraham at Mamre (Genesis 18, St Andrei Rublev's famous **Trinity icon**); at
  creation God speaks His **Word** while the **Spirit** hovers over the waters (Genesis 1:2-3). All made plain
  at the Jordan (Matthew 3).

## Fair to other traditions (rule #2)
The Orthodox confess the Spirit proceeds **from the Father** (John 15:26). The Catholic/Protestant West says
**from the Father *and the Son*** (the **Filioque**, added later to the Creed in the West). It is the central
East-West Trinitarian difference; the Orthodox guard the **Father as the one source** of the Godhead.
(Deserves its own lesson later. Doctrine questions → Fr Bogdan.)

## 🕊️ Live it this week
When you make the **sign of the Cross** in your prayer rule, make it **slowly**, and as your hand touches
each point say in your heart: *Father... Son... Holy Spirit.* You confess the whole Trinity with your body.

## 🙏 A prayer
*O Holy Trinity, Father, Son, and Holy Spirit, one God, have mercy on me. Draw me into Your life of love,
and let me carry that love to everyone around me. Amen.*

**God is not a lonely One. He is a communion of Love, and you are invited in.**
