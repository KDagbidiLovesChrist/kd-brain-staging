# The Meaning of Everything: Why God Made You (the North Star)
**Date:** 2026-06-21  ·  **Track:** B (Theology). The North Star of the whole study.
*(Re-leveled 2026-06-27 to the new bar: toddler-simple, warm and human, with the one truth to carry on your
heart. Quotes confirmed NKJV / OSB Septuagint.)*

## 📖 In plain words (the easy version)
Let's start with the biggest question there is. Why are you here at all? Why is there anything, instead of
nothing? Here is the answer, and it is good news.

God made you because He wanted to. That is it. Not because He was lonely. Not because He needed help. God was
never missing one single thing.

How can that be? Because God is love, all by Himself, all the time. We have a word for this: the **Trinity**.
Big word, simple meaning. It means God is one God, but three Persons: the **Father**, the **Son**, and the
**Holy Spirit**. They love each other so perfectly that they are not three gods, they are one God. So even
before there was a sky, or the sea, or one single star, or you, there was already love. God was never alone.
God was never sad.

So then, why make us? Picture a mum and a dad who love each other so much that the love kind of spills over,
and they want a little baby to share it with. They do not have a baby because something is missing. They have
a baby because love wants to give. That is exactly why God made you. Love, spilling over.

And He did not make you the way He made a rock or a tree. He made you **"in His image."** That just means He
built you on purpose so you could know Him, talk to Him, and love Him back. Like a child who has their dad's
eyes and their dad's smile, you carry something of God inside you.

Now the deepest part, and do not worry, it has a simple picture. God did not make you just to visit Him now
and then. He made you to be **filled all the way up with His own life**, so that slowly you become like Him.
The old word for this is **theosis** (you say it thee-OH-sis). Here is the picture. Take a cold, grey iron
poker and leave it in a fire. After a while it glows red and hot. It is still iron. But now it is full of
fire. That is you, full of God.

And that is why nothing in this world ever fully fills you. Not money. Not winning. Not even the best day you
have ever had. You were built for God, so only God fits the empty spot inside. Everything else is too small.

This is the most important thing in your whole study, King. Hold it close: **you were made by Love, for love,
to be with God forever.**

## In one line
God, who is love inside Himself, made you out of love, to be loved, and to share His life forever. That is
the meaning of everything.

## ✨ Carry this on your heart

> **"I was made by Love, for love, to be with God forever. Without Him, nothing else matters."**

*Lord, write this on my heart. When I forget it, remind me. You are the reason for everything. Amen.*

## The lesson (slowly, step by step)
**1. God needed nothing. He made you out of pure love.** God is not lonely or empty. He is already full of
love, because He is three Persons loving each other as one God. So He did not make you to fix a problem. He
made you as a gift, for no reason except love. (That is a very different God from the angry or far-away "god"
a lot of people picture.)

**2. He made you "in His image."** The Bible says: *"Then God said, 'Let Us make man in Our image, according
to Our likeness'… So God created man in His own image"* (Genesis 1:26-27). Out of everything God made, only
people are built to know Him and love Him back. (Tiny detail, big deal: God says *"Let **Us**."* Even on the
first page of the Bible, God is speaking as more than one Person. The Trinity is there from the very start.)

**3. Your one job in life: to be filled with God.** The Bible calls this becoming *"partakers of the divine
nature"* (2 Peter 1:4). That is the poker-in-the-fire again: you stay you, but you get filled with God. And
Jesus told us that this is what "forever life" actually is. He said: *"And this is eternal life, that they may
know You, the only true God, and Jesus Christ whom You have sent"* (John 17:3). Notice He did not say eternal
life is a place. He said it is *knowing* God. Like really knowing a person you love, not just knowing a fact.

**4. Why you feel restless.** Because you were made for God, nothing smaller will ever settle you. A wise old
man named Augustine prayed it perfectly: *"You have made us for Yourself, O Lord, and our heart is restless
until it rests in You"* (Confessions 1.1). That restless feeling is not a fault. It is a compass, and it
points home.

## The deepest part: where is Jesus in all of this?
This is the beautiful bit, and it is the key to the whole Bible, so go slow.

God made the first man, Adam, "in His image." But then people turned away from God. The Church calls that
"the fall." And it damaged the image of God in all of us, like a coin that has been rubbed so much you can
barely see the king's face on it anymore. The image is still there, but it is worn and faded.

So what did God do about it? He sent the perfect picture of Himself, in person. The Bible says Jesus is *"the
image of the invisible God"* (Colossians 1:15). You cannot see God with your eyes, but you can look at Jesus
and see exactly what God is like. And Jesus does not just show us the faded picture from far away. He comes
right inside our worn-out lives and makes the image clear and bright again.

An old teacher of the Church, St Athanasius, said it in one short line: *"He became man that we might become
god."* He means: God became one of us, so that we could be filled with God. That is why Jesus coming as a
baby matters so much. That is how the cold poker finally gets put into the fire.

## How we know this is really true (one voice, many witnesses)
You do not have to just take one person's word for it. Watch how everything says the same thing:
- **The Bible** says it (Genesis, 2 Peter).
- **Jesus Himself** said it (eternal life is knowing God, John 17:3).
- **The Church** prays it every single Sunday in the Creed: Jesus came down *"for us men and for our
  salvation,"* which simply means *to bring us back to God.*
- **The wise saints** said it (Athanasius, Augustine, and Irenaeus, who said *"the glory of God is a living
  man, and the life of man is the vision of God"*).
- **And your own heart** says it, every time you feel that ache for "something more."

When the Bible, and Jesus, and the Church, and the saints, and your own heart all say the exact same thing,
you can stand on it. It is solid ground.

## Live it this week
When you feel restless, or you catch yourself reaching for your phone or anything just to fill a gap, stop
for one second and say quietly: *"My heart is restless until it rests in You."* Let that little ache turn
into one breath toward God. The restlessness is not your enemy. It is a compass. Follow it home.
