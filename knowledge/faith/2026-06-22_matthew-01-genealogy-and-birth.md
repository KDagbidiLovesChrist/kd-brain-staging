# Gospel of Matthew, Chapter 1 · The Genealogy and the Birth of Christ

**Date:** 2026-06-22
**Strand:** ① THE WORD (the Spine), first chapter of the New Testament (OSB → NT → Matthew)
**Lenses used:** theology · the why · typology · connections · literary craft · beauty · lived Tradition
**Tie of the day:** prayed the morning after King resisted a temptation by clinging to the Name of Jesus.

## 📖 In plain words (the easy version)
The New Testament (the part of the Bible about Jesus and what came after) starts with a long list of names, a family tree, called a **genealogy**. That might seem boring, but it is God showing His "receipt" that He keeps His promises: long ago He promised Abraham and King David that someone special would come from their family, and this list proves Jesus really is that someone. The list reads "this person had this child, who had this child," over and over, then it suddenly changes, because Jesus had no human father; God Himself stepped in. The list even includes some women with messy, broken pasts, to show Jesus was not ashamed of His family and came to save broken people on purpose. The name "Jesus" means "the LORD saves," and an angel said He came to "save His people from their sins", that is the **Incarnation** (God becoming a real human while still being God, "God with us"). This matters for King because the very Name he reaches for when he is tempted is the Name that was made to rescue him from exactly that.

> *"And thou shalt call his name JESUS: for he shall save his people from their sins."* — Matthew 1:21

## Why the NT opens with a genealogy (the WHY)
The New Testament opens with a list of names, a **genealogy**, on purpose. It is God's "receipt"
that He keeps His promises. For ~2,000 years He vowed to **Abraham** (*"in thee shall all families of
the earth be blessed,"* Gen 12:3) and to **David** (a son on the throne *forever*, 2 Sam 7:12–13).
The genealogy proves Jesus is the real heir of both, born into a real bloodline at the exact point all
the promises aimed. Matthew 1:1 packs the whole OT into one line: *"Jesus Christ, the son of David, the
son of Abraham."* **Son of David** = the promised King · **Son of Abraham** = the blessing for all nations.

## The artistry (literary craft + connections)
- **The drumbeat that breaks:** *"begot… begot… begot…"* runs as a steady human rhythm, then snaps at
  the end. Matthew does NOT write "Joseph begot Jesus" but *"Joseph the husband of Mary, **of whom was
  born Jesus**"* (1:16). The grammar itself signals: this child has no human father, God stepped in.
- **The four unexpected women:** Matthew names **Tamar, Rahab, Ruth, and the wife of Uriah (Bathsheba)**,   a woman who played the harlot, a Canaanite prostitute, a Moabite foreigner, an adultery. **St John
  Chrysostom** (*Homilies on Matthew*) marvels that Christ was *not ashamed* of a family tree full of
  sinners and outsiders, because He came **for** them. Grace reaches the broken and the foreigner. The
  fifth and final woman is **Mary, the Theotokos** (the new Eve).
- **"Biblos geneseōs"**, Matthew's opening Greek ("the book of genesis/origin") deliberately echoes the
  **Book of Genesis**: this is the **new creation**, Christ the New Adam beginning a new humanity.

## The birth · the Name that is the whole point (1:18-25)
Mary is found with child *"of the Holy Spirit."* Joseph, *"a just man,"* plans to release her quietly; an
angel meets him in a dream: *"thou shalt call his name JESUS: for he shall save his people from their
sins"* (1:21). **"Jesus" (Yeshua) = "the LORD saves,"** and the angel names what He saves us *from*: **our
sins.** It fulfills Isaiah 7:14: *"they shall call his name Emmanuel… God with us"* (1:23).
= **the Incarnation:** fully human (real ancestry, real mother) and fully God (conceived of the Spirit,
"God with us"). Joseph models quiet, wordless, righteous obedience.

## Formation tie (the point of it all)
King resisted sin last night by clinging to the **Name of Jesus**, and the first page of the NT announces
the entire reason that Name exists: *"he shall save his people from their sins."* The Name he reached for
in the dark is the Name that saves from exactly what he fought. The Jesus Prayer puts **Emmanuel, God with
us** on his lips.

## Live it this week
Pray the **Jesus Prayer** at the *first* knock of any temptation, not after, knowing the Name means *He
saves His people from their sins.* Make His Name the reflex.
