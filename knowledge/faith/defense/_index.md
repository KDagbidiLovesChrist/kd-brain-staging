# The Defense · Apologetics & Discernment (Strand ③)

*The "armor shelf" for King David's faith study. Where we collect, clearly and fairly, what we learn
about **heresies, other traditions, and hard challenges to the faith**, so the answer is ready when it's
needed (1 Peter 3:15). This is **not** a daily strand; it is a cross-cutting layer, woven in as the Word
and the Tradition touch it, and collected here so it's findable.*

> *"Be ready always to give an answer [Greek: **apologia**, a defense] to every man that asketh you a
> reason of the hope that is in you **with meekness and fear**." — 1 Peter 3:15*

---

## The rules of this shelf (sacred ground · see `the-truth\CLAUDE.md`)
1. **Salvation first, defense second.** This is armor, not the main meal. The two daily strands (the Word
   + the Tradition) are for formation and salvation. This layer is picked up *as it comes up*, never at
   the cost of the daily bread. (King's own ordering, 2026-06-22, and it is the Orthodox ordering.)
2. **The enemy is the lie, never the person.** Apologetics fights to **win the person to Christ**, not to
   win an argument. Firm as iron on the truth, gentle as a lamb with the human being (1 Peter 3:15–16,
   *"with meekness and fear"*).
3. **Represent others fairly FIRST.** State any other tradition or view as *they* would recognise
   themselves, no strawmen, no cheap shots, *then* give the Orthodox understanding (CLAUDE.md rule #2).
4. **Learn heresy through its cure.** In Orthodoxy we mostly meet the heresies through the **Ecumenical
   Councils and the Fathers who answered them**, you learn the crooked by knowing the straight.
5. **Never fabricate.** Every quote and citation verified; sift with the consensus of the Fathers + the
   councils. When unsure, say so and bring the big questions to Fr Bogdan.

---

## How it connects to the daily strands
- When **② The Tradition** reaches the **Seven Ecumenical Councils**, each council's heresy is studied
  *there, in context* (Arianism → Nicaea 325; Nestorianism → Ephesus 431; Monophysitism → Chalcedon 451;
  Iconoclasm → Nicaea II 787; etc.), and a note is added here.
- When **① The Word** touches a passage commonly twisted, we note the faithful reading here.
- The teaching/apologetics map of how it all connects lives in `the-truth\CONNECTIONS.md`.

## The apologetics toolkit (built 2026-06-27 · draws only from verified lessons; grows as the study deepens)
- **`STRUCTURE.md`**, the apologetics map, two ways: **by audience** (curious / open-minded / on the
  defensive / hostile critics & heretics, each with its posture + doorway) and **by objection** (each
  pointed to a verified lesson, or marked 🔜 to build first).
- **`QUICK_ANSWERS.md`**, ready one-page answers, but only where a verified lesson supports them (God's
  existence · Scripture-within-Tradition · the Cross as Christus Victor · icons). Everything else is listed
  as 🔜 to build.
- **`HERESIES_BY_COUNCIL.md`**, the Seven Ecumenical Councils (Nicaea I → Nicaea II), each with the
  heresy, the truth defended, and the core decision; finer detail marked 🔜 to study as the history spine reaches it.
