# Slave to Sin, Free in Christ · the war within (Romans 6-8)
**Date:** 2026-06-23  ·  **Track:** Theology + Formation (lived) · *born from King's own honest confession*

## 📖 In plain words (the easy version)
Sometimes you want to do good, like pray or spend time with God, but you don't feel like it; and sometimes you want to do wrong even when you know it will hurt you. That tug-of-war is what the Bible calls being "a slave to sin" (so caught by a bad habit that it controls you, the way a chain holds you). Here's the good news: even St Paul (one of the greatest early followers of Jesus) felt this same battle, so your struggle isn't proof that you don't belong to God, it's the normal fight of every Christian. In fact, the very fact that you *want* to belong to Christ and that the sin bothers you is the Holy Spirit (God's own life inside you) already pulling you home. Christ broke that chain on the Cross and set you free, and there is now no condemnation, no being found guilty, for you in Him. Becoming truly free is a slow growing, not a light switch: you will fall, but the rule is simple, when you fall, get up at once, say "Lord, have mercy," and keep walking. The Church gives you real help for this: above all Confession (telling your sins to God with your spiritual father, which breaks their secret hold), plus the short Jesus Prayer to whisper the moment temptation comes. Why this matters for you: you are not alone and not condemned, you are a loved son who is being set free, one step at a time.

## In one line
You remembered it rightly: Paul says we were "sold under sin", but Christ has set us free, and the Christian life is learning, with His grace, to live as the free son you already are, falling and rising, never condemned, never alone.

## You did not mix it up · you remembered it faithfully
That tension, *I know the good, I don't want it; I know the harm, I want it anyway*, is **exactly** Romans 7. And the answer, *Christ loves me and sets me free*, is **exactly** Romans 6 and 8, and John 8. You have the whole Gospel in your own words.

## The lesson

**1. The war is real — and Paul felt it too (Romans 7).** *"For that which I do I allow not: for what I would, that do I not; but what I hate, that do I"* (7:15). *"For the good that I would I do not: but the evil which I would not, that I do"* (7:19). *"O wretched man that I am! who shall deliver me from the body of this death?"* (7:24). If **St Paul** fought this, then your struggle is not proof you don't belong — it is the normal battle of every Christian. The saints fought lust and the passions their whole lives; Paul had a "thorn in the flesh" (2 Cor 12:7). The struggle itself is a sign the Spirit is at work in you — a stone lying still feels no current; only the one swimming upstream feels the pull.

**2. What "slave to sin" means.** *"Whosoever committeth sin is the servant [slave] of sin"* (John 8:34); *"I am carnal, sold under sin"* (Rom 7:14). Sin makes a promise — *this will satisfy you* — and then it owns you; the wanting grows and the freedom shrinks. This is the **wounded nature** we inherited from the fall (your lesson "Why the Fall Changed Everything"): the will weakened, the desires disordered. It is a real chain — but it is **not the final word.**

**3. The turn — Christ sets you free.** Paul's cry doesn't end in despair: *"I thank God through Jesus Christ our Lord"* (7:25). *"If the Son therefore shall make you free, ye shall be free indeed"* (John 8:36). *"Our old man is crucified with him… that henceforth we should not serve sin"* (Rom 6:6); *"being then made free from sin, ye became the servants of righteousness"* (6:18). And the line to write on your heart: *"There is therefore now **no condemnation** to them which are in Christ Jesus… the law of the Spirit of life in Christ Jesus hath made me free from the law of sin and death"* (Rom 8:1–2). The chain was broken at the Cross; in baptism you were joined to that freedom.

**4. Freedom is a process, not a switch (synergy).** You were *set free* and you are *growing into* that freedom, with God's grace, day by day — falling and rising. *"A just man falleth seven times, and riseth up again"* (Proverbs 24:16). The desert fathers' whole wisdom in a line: *if you fall, get up; if you fall again, get up again.* Two traps to avoid: **despair** ("I'll never change" — that's the accuser, your lesson #11) and **presumption** ("it doesn't matter"). The narrow path between them is humble, daily repentance.

**5. The wanting itself is grace.** Hear this: the very fact that you *want* to belong to Christ, that the sin grieves you, that you'd rather want the good, **that desire is the Holy Spirit already in you, drawing you home.** A true slave of sin doesn't long for freedom; you do. That longing is God's hand on your shoulder.

**6. The medicine the Church gives.** Christ doesn't just declare you free and leave you to fight alone, He gives means of grace:
- **Confession**, the sacrament made for exactly this. **Bring this to Fr Bogdan.** Naming it before God with your spiritual father breaks its secret power and pours in grace. Do not carry it alone.
- **The Jesus Prayer** in the *moment* of temptation: *"Lord Jesus Christ, Son of God, have mercy on me, a sinner."* A sword for the war.
- **The Eucharist**, **fasting**, and **vigilance** (watching what you let in). God *"will with the temptation also make a way to escape"* (1 Cor 10:13).
- His promise when you feel weak: *"My grace is sufficient for thee: for my strength is made perfect in weakness"* (2 Cor 12:9).
