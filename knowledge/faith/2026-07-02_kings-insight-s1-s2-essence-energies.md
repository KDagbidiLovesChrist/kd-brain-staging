# King's insight · S1/S2 power feeds and the divine energies (2026-07-02)

> **King's own words (voice, while watching the 24/7 Engine agents work):** "S2 usually means another power,
> not using S1, we will see S2 and gen another energy we receive thru grace... even when not fully using
> EirGrid God gives us energies thru His grace, hence theosis."

## The insight, in plain words
In a data centre, a critical load has two feeds, S1 and S2, from the one grid. If the draw on S1 is low or
lost, S2 still delivers. King saw the spiritual picture: **even when WE are weak on our feed (dry prayer,
scattered attention, low effort), God's grace keeps delivering power. We receive energies we did not
generate. That receiving is theosis.**

## The Church's teaching it matches (verified)
This is the **essence and energies** distinction of **St Gregory Palamas** (14th c., vindicated by the
Councils of Constantinople 1341 to 1351):
- God's **essence** is unknowable and unparticipable, the Grid itself, infinite, never exhausted.
- God's **energies** are God Himself in action, uncreated grace truly reaching us. We participate in the
  energies, never the essence. *"Partakers of the divine nature"* (2 Peter 1:4) is read through the energies.
- **Theosis = participation in the divine energies, by grace.**
- Grace flows even when our draw is weak: *"In Him we live and move and have our being"* (Acts 17:28);
  He *"makes His sun rise on the evil and on the good"* (Matthew 5:45); *"the Spirit also helps in our
  weaknesses"* (Romans 8:26).

## The one refinement (no contradiction to God, Rule #19)
S2 is not a different power or a backup god. **Both feeds come from the ONE Grid.** One Source, many feeds;
one God, many energies (FOUNDATION.md: God is the infinite Source, no backup). The analogy preaches
truly only when both S1 and S2 are His.

## To live it
When prayer feels dry, keep standing on the feed: the power is His, not ours (the Jesus Prayer, the
prayer rule). Weak draw does not mean a dead line.
