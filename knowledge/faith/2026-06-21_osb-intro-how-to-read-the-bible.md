# OSB Intro · How to Read the Bible the Orthodox Way (the lens)
**Date:** 2026-06-21  ·  **Reading:** Orthodox Study Bible, introductory pages (the start of Fr Bogdan's plan)

## 📖 In plain words (the easy version)
Before reading even one verse of the Bible, this lesson teaches the right way to read it. The Orthodox (King's Eastern Christian church) don't treat the Bible like a school textbook to pick apart and judge, they read it humbly, expecting God to speak to them personally. There are four simple habits to hold. First, read it obediently, come to be changed by it, not to sit over it like a judge. Second, read it within the Church, Scripture was written inside the Church, so we read it together with the Fathers (the wise, holy teachers of the early Church), the saints, and the worship, not making up private meanings on our own. Third, read it with Christ at the centre, the whole Bible, from start to finish, points to Jesus, so look for Him on every page. Fourth, read it personally, as God talking to *me* today. This matters for King because Fr Bogdan is teaching him *how* to read before *what* to read, so the whole journey through the Bible starts on solid ground from the very first verse.

## In one line
Before reading a single verse, the OSB intro teaches the most important habit: the Orthodox don't read
the Bible alone as a textbook to analyse, they read it humbly, inside the Church, with Christ at the
centre, as God speaking personally to them.

## What's in the OSB introductory pages (read these first)
- **"Introducing the Orthodox Church"**, a short orientation to the faith.
- **"How to Read the Bible"**, the Orthodox way of reading/interpreting Scripture (the lens below).
- **"Overview of the Books of the Bible"**, the kinds of biblical literature and how the Bible is arranged.
- Plus: each book has its own intro (Author, Date, Major Themes, Background) and there are ~50 study articles.

## The four principles (Met. Kallistos Ware, "How to Read the Bible" · the heart of the intro)
1. **Read it obediently**, with humility. We come to be changed by the Word, not to sit over it as
   judges. Receive first, question second.
2. **Read it within the Church (ecclesially)**, Scripture was written *in* and *for* the Church, so
   we read it with the Church's mind: the Fathers, the saints, the Liturgy, not inventing private
   meanings alone. (This is the big difference from "just me and my Bible.")
3. **Read it with Christ at the centre**, the whole Bible, Old and New, points to Christ. Look for
   Him on every page; He is the key that unlocks it.
4. **Read it personally**, as God speaking to *me*, today, for my salvation, not just ancient history.

## Why Fr Bogdan starts here (the why)
Most people pick up the Bible and start interpreting on their own. Fr Bogdan has King learn *how* to
read before *what* to read, so the whole journey through the NT and OT is grounded the right way from
verse one. This is the Orthodox phronema (mindset) being formed before the content begins.

## Live it this week
As King reads the OSB intro pages, hold the four principles. Then before each reading, pause and pray
one line, "Lord, open my eyes to see You here", so reading becomes prayer, not just study.

open the eyes of our mind to understand Your Gospel teachings. Amen.*
