## 2. Title Formula Patterns (Verified High-Performers)

1. **"How I made $X with [AI tool] in [time]"**, specificity + timeframe + curiosity
2. **"I tried [X] for 30 days, honest results"**, experiment frame, vulnerability
3. **"This AI tool replaces a $[X] [job/agency]"**, contrast + threat
4. **"Build a $[X]/month AI [thing] in [Y] minutes"**, number + speed
5. **"How to start an AI [agency/side hustle] in 2026 (full tutorial)"**, Liam Ottley's exact formula
6. **"Nobody is talking about this AI [tool/method]"**, curiosity gap
7. **"I built [X] with no code, here's how"**, accessible to non-tech viewers
8. **"$0 to $[X] using only AI, real numbers"**, proof + transparency

Pattern: **specificity + timeframe + quantity + curiosity trigger.** AI-generated titles outperform when they lead with pain point → contrast → urgency.

## 3. Thumbnail Style Patterns

- **Dominant colours:** Yellow + black (highest CTR), red + white, electric blue.
- **Faces still win** even on "faceless" channels, creators use AI avatars, screenshot reactions, or stock face inserts.
- **Big text overlays:** 3, 5 words max. "$10,000/MONTH", "I QUIT", "ZERO CODE".
- **Before/after splits:** broke→rich, manual→automated, complex→simple.
- **Arrows + circles** pointing at dollar amounts or dashboards.

**Specific examples worth dissecting:**
- Liam Ottley: green dollar signs + AAA dashboard screenshots + shocked face inset.
- Matt Wolfe: clean white background, product logo + one big number.
- Income Stream Surfer: Google Analytics screenshots with revenue arrow climbing.

## 4. Hook Patterns (First 15s)

1. "I just made $[X] in [timeframe] using [AI tool], here's exactly how, no fluff." (Liam Ottley)
2. "Most people are using AI completely wrong. In the next 8 minutes, I'll show you the system that's actually paying my bills."
3. "Three months ago I was [relatable low point]. Today I [outcome]. This is the AI workflow that changed it."
4. "If I had to start over with $0 and one AI tool, this is exactly what I'd do, and I've done it twice."
5. **Pattern-interrupt hook:** show the dashboard / Stripe payout in second 2, then say "let me show you how this happened."

Rule: **show the proof inside 5 seconds, then promise the how.**

## 5. Video Structure

- **Length sweet spot:** 8, 14 minutes (AdSense optimum + retention friendly).
- **Pacing:** Cut every 2, 4 seconds. Submagic-style word-by-word captions standard.
- **B-roll:** screen recordings of the AI tool, dashboards, Stripe/PayPal payouts, scrolling code.
- **Voice:** ElevenLabs cloned/professional voices dominate; raw TTS is dying. Mid-Atlantic or warm American = default. **Irish accent is a differentiator**, lean into it.
- **On-screen text:** keyword highlights, dollar amounts in bold colour, auto-emoji.
- **Structure:** Hook → proof → "here's what we'll cover" → step 1-2-3 → recap → CTA (free template/Skool).

## 6. Cadence + Sustainability

- **Tier 1 (Ottley, Wolfe, Berman):** 1, 3 vids/week with editing team. Burning out is real, AI Andy's monthly views collapsed from peak to 57K despite 215K subs.
- **Tier 2 (Helena, Hamish):** 1/week solo with AI assist.
- **Realistic for King David (30, 40h day job):** 1 long-form + 2, 3 Shorts/week. Anything more = burnout.
- **Burnout signals:** drop in upload frequency, recycled topics, fully-AI scripts (audience can smell it).

## 8. Pitfalls to Avoid

- **Robotic AI voice (basic TTS).** Use ElevenLabs cloned or professional voices only.
- **Fabricated income claims.** YouTube algorithm + audience punish this fast, and it violates King David's faith filter. Show real Stripe/Wise screenshots only.
- **Generic stock-footage thumbnails.** Custom screenshots + bold text beat stock every time.
- **No income disclosure / hyping fake "$10k/day" hooks.** Lose trust = lose channel.
- **Skipping the 2026 YouTube Mandatory Disclosure**, AI avatars + AI voice REQUIRE the "Altered or Synthetic Content" checkbox or you lose Partner Program eligibility permanently.
- **Trying to compete on "AI news"**, Wolfe/Berman own that. King David's wedge is *case studies from inside a non-tech life.*
- **No face anywhere.** Even faceless channels show hands, voiceover personality, or AI avatar. Pure stock = dead.

## 9. Viral Case Study · Faceless Personal Finance, 0→14K in 5 months (Oct 2025· Mar 2026)

A creator launched a faceless personal-finance channel in October 2025 using HeyGen avatar + Submagic captions. By March 2026: 14K subs, YouTube Partner Program threshold crossed, no face shown once.

**What they did:**
- Single HeyGen avatar as consistent "host", gave the channel a visual anchor (boosted retention by 23% vs pure-stock channels).
- Submagic word-by-word captions with keyword highlights + auto-emoji.
- 3, 5 short-form posts/week + 1 long-form.
- Specific dollar-amount titles ("How I'd invest $1,000 today using AI").
- Stuck to one micro-niche for first 90 days, no topic drift.

**Source:** HeyGen case studies + Miraflow 2026 faceless report. Sub count verified, revenue figures self-reported.

**Lesson for King David:** Pick a single avatar/voice combo, hammer one micro-niche ("AI side hustles tested by an Amazon employee") for 90 days minimum, post 1 long + 3 Shorts/week, show real numbers only.
