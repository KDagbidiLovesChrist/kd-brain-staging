## 1. BRAND IDENTITY (the foundation = the money)
- **You win on a clear, specific idea + an ownable visual motif + ruthless consistency, NOT a prettier logo.** In 2025 great coffee is assumed; differentiation is identity, story, experience.
- **Cohesion test:** bag, cup, website and Instagram side by side should read as ONE brand in under 3 seconds.
- **The system:** logo set (primary + submark + favicon, works 1-colour on a bag) · 2-3 colour palette with assigned roles · max 2 typefaces · a signature motif/illustration · a real-photo style (warm, real people, not stock) · a defined voice.
- **Avoid the generic third-wave clichés** (kraft bag + serif + "single origin" + mountains). Stand out with personality, a specific story, a palette nobody else in the city owns.
- **Packaging:** one strong visual idea; minimalism = premium OR committed maximalism (not half-and-half); tactile finish; sustainable materials (now table stakes); QR to story/brew guide.
- **Brand guidelines doc sections (what they asked for):** story/mission · logo rules (clear space, don'ts) · colour (CMYK/RGB/HEX/Pantone) · typography hierarchy · photography style (approved + avoid) · voice · packaging application · social usage.

## 2. GET NOTICED · SOCIAL (the reach)
- **TikTok = #1 organic reach for a new/small shop** (interest-based FYP, a 50-follower account can hit 50k views). Drink-process + staff-personality videos, 3-5/wk, lead with the pour (no logo/intro), phone footage, geotag every post.
- **Instagram = trust + community.** Reels for reach, carousels for saves (highest-value signal), daily Stories. 4-5 feed/wk. **Location-tag EVERY post to the exact address** (the #1 local discovery tool). **5-8 hashtags max** (Instagram capped it late 2025), 2 local + 2 niche + 1-2 mid.
- **Facebook = local groups + events + trust signal + cheap paid radius targeting.** Organic page reach is dead (2-5%); use groups/events for discovery.
- **Universal:** consistency > frequency · first-hour engagement triggers amplification · saves/shares > likes · authenticity + phone footage > polish · hook in under 2 seconds · video dominates everything.
- **Best times:** IG Tue-Thu 7-9am + 12-2pm (Wed strongest); TikTok 7-9am + 7-11pm, weekends OK.

## 2b. SOCIAL SEO + ALGORITHM · be found INSIDE each app (per platform)
"Social SEO" = ranking in each platform's OWN search (40% of young adults search TikTok/IG before Google for local food; 46% have visited a place they found on TikTok). Distinct from Google SEO.

**What every platform now indexes (optimise all of these):** spoken keywords (auto-transcription/ASR on IG Reels, TikTok, YouTube, FB video), on-screen text (OCR), captions, alt text, hashtags, and the profile text fields. **Say your "[city] coffee" keyword in the first 5 seconds of every video** + put it in on-screen text + caption + location tag (the "layered location" = max local signal). Note: IG (post Jul 2025) + TikTok (early 2026) captions now ALSO rank in Google.

- **Instagram:** the **Name field** (not just handle) is the #1 search lever, e.g. "[City] Coffee · Specialty Café". Geotag every post. Algorithm weights (Mosseri-confirmed): **DM shares = #1**, **saves ≈ 3x likes**, Reel completion 50%+ → Explore, hook in first 3s.
- **TikTok:** dedicated **video title field** (2025) + ASR + on-screen text are top search signals. **TikTok Local Feed (Feb 2026)** + geographic seeding = a small cafe's first views are locals; **completion rate (70%+) is the #1 distribution signal**; 80-follower account can hit 50k local views.
- **YouTube (= #2 search engine):** title + description + transcript + chapters indexed; CTR 4%+ and 50%+ retention drive ranking. **Honest: slow ROI for a local cafe**, use repurposed Shorts (free) + 1-2 "best coffee in [city]" long-form/quarter for Google ranking. Not a priority.
- **Facebook:** Page name + About + check-ins + reviews drive local search; Events have their own index. Algorithm favours native video + conversational posts; organic Page reach only 2-5%.
- **Twitter/X (lowest priority for a cafe):** bio = #1 on-platform SEO lever; open-source algorithm weights **reply-the-author-replies-to = +75x**, repost +20x, **external link = -30-50% reach** (put links in a reply), **X Premium = 2-4x reach**. Only **24% of marketers** rate X high-ROI vs 43% IG / 54% FB; **IG engagement ≈ 84x X**. Use X only for real-time updates, B2B, #CoffeeTwitter community, or a witty owner persona, not local foot traffic.

**PLATFORM PRIORITY for a local coffee shop (cross-agent consensus):** 1) **Google Business** 2) **TikTok** 3) **Instagram** 4) Facebook 5) YouTube 6) X (lowest).

**Universal social-SEO consensus:** spoken keywords are indexed everywhere · saves + DM shares are the top distribution signals · completion rate gates whether anyone sees your engagement · layer location 4 ways · consistency > volume · alt-text/captions/transcripts are ranking signals AND accessibility · profile name/bio are your permanent topic metadata.

## 2c. VIRAL VIDEO PLAYBOOK · Reels/TikTok (the reach weapon, scripted here, edited via /video-edit)
**Completion rate is the master signal (~40-50% of distribution; 85% watched = 4x reach).** Viewers decide in ~2.7s.
- **Hook (0-2s):** motion, never a logo, start mid-pour/steam/reveal. 60%+ 3-sec hold = 5-10x reach.
- **Structure:** Hook → Build (2-3 micro-beats, a visual change every 3-5s = +32% engagement) → Payoff (deliver exactly what the hook promised, make it satisfying) → **Loop** (match last frame to first → rewatches; 10%+ rewatch is excellent).
- **Length:** 15-25s for completion; 60-90s for max TikTok views (16k avg vs 5k for <15s).
- **Captions** (85% watch on mute) = **+38-40% completion**. Word-by-word, white-on-dark, lower third.
- **Saves + shares > likes** (2025 shift); design to be saved/sent.
- **Trending audio** only while under ~5k uses (act in 24-48h); cut the video TO the sound.
- **Visual craft:** 3+ angles (overhead/side/macro), slow-mo pours (120fps+), side/back-light the steam, ASMR natural sound (70% music / 30% ambient), warm LUT at 60-80%, beat-synced cuts.
- **Authenticity > polish:** Coffee Milano 9M views on a no-budget clip; Dutch Bros 48x EMV on casual creator sips. Concept realness beats production (best = both).
- This is the **script/structure brain; your `/video-edit` style library is the execution.** Full template + editor checklist sit in the research.

### UPDATE · from proven 2025-2026 creator videos (transcript-studied: Brock Johnson/Insta Club Hub, HubSpot)
- **CORRECTION to the signal hierarchy:** saves are now DEPRIORITISED (private, no time-on-platform). Current order = **Shares (sends-per-reach) > DM replies > Comments > Likes > Saves.** Optimise for *shares*, not saves. (Confirmed via Mosseri.)
- **DM automation (ManyChat):** "comment [WORD] and I'll DM you the menu" → drives comments AND DM replies (Instagram's top-weighted signal). Documented at 17k sends/month. 5-min setup.
- **Trial Reels:** post up to 20/day to NON-followers only (zero risk to your audience); ~70% can be **upcycled** older posts (re-run winners every 60-90 days). Pure discovery engine.
- **Hook spec (data from 1,300 viral reels):** 5-8 words, below 5th-grade reading level, **negative framing** ("stop/don't/avoid") beats positive; bright first frame ≈ +60% engagement; a face + visible eyes in the first second lifts retention.
- **Diagnostic:** track "**view rate past first 3 seconds**" in IG insights, it tells you if the hook (not the content) is failing.
- **Edutainment rule:** pure educational/tips ≤ 20% of the calendar (tips don't get shared); make even those entertaining.
- **Risk flag:** don't use Instagram's in-app music library on a talking video, if the song is pulled, your video's audio (incl. your voice) is permanently muted.
- **Sharper:** trending audio = use it in the first few hundred-to-thousand uses ("before Dunkin uses it"), not just under 5k. Authenticity quantified: HubSpot's unscripted batch got **2x the reach** of scripted.
- **Honest gap:** these are platform/marketing educators, not cafe owners, so it's solid platform mechanics, not coffee-specific proof. The Gemini "watch real proven coffee videos" step (bridge being built) adds the coffee-specific visual layer. Coffee Milano (9M) + Nitro Bar remain the real coffee case proof.

## 3. GET FOUND · LOCAL SEO (the foot traffic)
- **Google Business Profile, fully optimised + 2 posts/wk + 100+ photos = the single biggest lever** (the local 3-pack drives up to 93% more actions). Exact-match name, precise category, all attributes, products/menu, seeded Q&A, messaging on.
- **Reviews = the tiebreaker:** 3-5/wk, asked in-the-moment via QR on counter/receipt + a post-visit text (98% open). Reply to every review in 24h. Never fake/incentivise (suspension risk).
- **NAP consistency** character-for-character across the top 10-15 directories (Google, Apple Business Connect, Yelp, Facebook, Bing, Foursquare, TripAdvisor, OpenTable). Quality over 150 random ones.
- **Website:** local title/meta + a strong location page + LocalBusiness/`CafeOrCoffeeShop` schema + mobile + under-3s load.
- **Blog:** 2 local-intent posts/mo ("best coffee in [area]", "remote-work cafes in [area]"), slow burn, 3-6 months to rank, ~30-50% foot-traffic lift reported.
- **Waste of time:** keyword stuffing, buying citations/links/reviews, treating social as a direct SEO signal.

## 4. RUN IT MONTHLY · THE ENGINE (the retainer)
- **Content pillars:** Craft 30% · Behind-the-counter 25% · Product 25% · Community 20%. Apply 70-20-10 (planned/flexible/spontaneous).
- **Batch:** one 4-6hr session/month produces the whole calendar. **Repurpose 1 Reel → 5 platform versions** (TikTok, IG feed, FB, Stories, email). Core efficiency lever.
- **Production:** real photos for product/coffee; AI for captions, graphics, repurposing. Brand kit locked in Canva.
- **Scheduling:** Buffer or Metricool (Metricool also schedules Google Business posts) + Meta Business Suite. **n8n** ties sheet → approve → publish → report (ready-made templates exist, adapt not build).
- **Manual-only (platform rules):** trending-audio TikToks/Reels, Stories, comments/DMs, review replies, IG Collabs.
- **Email:** 2x/month (reactivation + loyalty, not acquisition); 35%+ open rate for local; Mailchimp/beehiiv.
- **6 metrics only:** reach · engagement rate · follower growth · GBP views · email open · DMs/enquiries. Add a promo code to prove social → foot traffic.
- **~6-10 human hours/month once running** = why the retainer is high-margin.

## WHERE ALL SOURCES AGREE (the consensus = what we actually do)
1. Short-form video is the universal priority format.
2. Authenticity + phone footage beats polish (great for small budgets).
3. Consistency of cadence beats frequency or perfect timing.
4. Location tags + a fully-optimised GBP = the local discovery engine.
5. A cohesive brand system across every touchpoint builds trust faster than any campaign.
6. Saves/shares are stronger signals than likes; design posts to be saved.
