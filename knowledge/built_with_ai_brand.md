## Two flywheels

### Flywheel 1 · AI UGC (cash now, 30-90 days to first €)
- **Product:** 30-60 sec AI-avatar video ads for e-commerce brands
- **Tools:** HeyGen (free tier 3 vids/mo, paid $24/mo when revenue justifies), ElevenLabs (free 10k chars/mo), CapCut (free), Pexels (free B-roll)
- **Distribution:** Fiverr UGC gig + cold Instagram/Twitter DMs to e-commerce brands + reference: own YouTube samples
- **Pricing:** Basic €50 (1 video, 30 sec, AI avatar, AI voice). Standard €150 (3 videos, your voice cloned). Premium €350 (5 videos + script writing + custom avatar).
- **Realistic income:** 10 sales/mo × €100 avg = €1000/mo within 60-90 days IF Fiverr gig + cold DMs are consistent

### Flywheel 2 · Faceless YouTube + Newsletter (long compound, 6-12 months to real income)
- **Channel:** Built with AI (YouTube + newsletter + Twitter)
- **Format:** Faceless videos, AI avatar, AI voice, B-roll + screen recordings
- **Cadence:** 1 video + 1 newsletter per week minimum
- **Revenue streams (in order of activation):**
  1. AIS+ affiliate (20% recurring), month 1-2
  2. Service work (Fiverr/Cal.com link in description), month 1-3
  3. YouTube ad revenue, month 8-14 (after 1000 subs + 4000 watch hours)
  4. Course launch ("Build AI Side Hustles in 30 Days"), month 6+
- **Realistic income trajectory:** €0-100/mo months 1-3 → €100-500/mo months 4-6 → €500-3000/mo months 7-12 (if it works)

### How they share workflow
- Same AI avatar + voice → both UGC ads and YouTube videos
- YouTube video script → repurposed as newsletter post → repurposed to Twitter thread → 30-sec clips become UGC portfolio samples
- One creator, one toolset, two revenue streams
