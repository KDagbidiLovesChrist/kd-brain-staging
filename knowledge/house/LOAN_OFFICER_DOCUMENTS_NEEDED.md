# Documents the Loan Officer Needs Before She Decides

*Palmerstown Credit Union. Updated 2026-06-23 after her call.*
*She said she needs two more things before making a decision: proof of invoice and a property agreement
from the council. Here is exactly what each one means and how to get it.*

---

## 1. Proof of invoice

**What it means:** a written, itemised quote or invoice that shows what the money will actually be spent
on. She wants to see that the EUR 24,000 for the house and the EUR 2,300 for the car go where you say
they go.

**What to give her:**
- A quote or invoice for the **home fit-out** work, broken down into items (flooring, the handyman
  labour, appliances, furniture, and so on). The full breakdown already exists in your project files
  (`deliverables/cost_tracker.md` and `deliverables/master_shopping_list.md`) so this is just tidying
  that into a clean quote.
- Best version: a quote on the **handyman's / fitter's own letterhead** with their name, the work, and
  the price. That carries the most weight.
- A quote or ad for the **car** you plan to buy (about EUR 2,300), to back up that figure.

**Action:** ask the handyman/fitter for a written quote on their headed paper, and pull together the car
quote. I can turn your cost tracker into a clean one-page itemised quote for you to use alongside it.

## 2. Property agreement from the council

**What it means:** the council's written agreement or contract for your home at 7 Adderig, the paper
that shows you own it or are buying it from them.

**You are not sure of the exact scheme name, so look for any of these (one of them will be yours):**
- An **Affordable Purchase** agreement (the council or LDA keeps a share of the value).
- A **Tenant (Incremental) Purchase** agreement (buying the council home you rented).
- A **shared ownership** or **rent-to-buy** agreement (the council part-owns it for now).
- The **contract of sale / deed / purchase agreement** from when you got the house.

**Action:**
- Check your house paperwork (the folder/file from when you got the keys, or your solicitor's pack).
- If you cannot find it, ring the council and ask for "a copy of my purchase/affordable purchase
  agreement for 7 Adderig." Confirm the exact scheme name while you are on the call so we can name it
  correctly for the loan officer.

## 3. The standard documents (have these ready too)

From your meeting checklist, these are the ones to bring with the two above:

- [ ] Photo ID (passport or driving licence)
- [ ] Proof of address (recent bill or bank statement)
- [ ] Last 3 payslips
- [ ] Last 3 to 6 months of bank statements
- [ ] Existing PCU loan statement (showing the ~EUR 4,494 balance to consolidate)
- [ ] Your budget and loan proposal (already prepared)
- [ ] Credit union membership details
