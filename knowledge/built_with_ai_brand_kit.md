## 1. Logo Concept

### Wordmark direction
A text-based logo. Two stacked words, all caps, tight tracking. The word "AI" sits on the same baseline as "WITH" but is set in the accent colour so the eye lands on it. The word "BUILT" anchors the lockup. This works at thumbnail size, in an email header, on a Fiverr gig card, and as a 32px favicon.

### Font pairings (3 options)
1. **Inter Black (display) + Inter Regular (body).** Clean, modern, free on Google Fonts. The same family for display and body keeps the whole brand cheap to deploy and consistent across the channel, newsletter, and gig. Recommended.
2. **Space Grotesk SemiBold (display) + Inter Regular (body).** Space Grotesk gives a tech-startup edge without being cold. Pairs with Inter for body copy.
3. **Geist Bold (display) + Geist Mono (numbers and code snippets).** Geist is Vercel's font, free, sharp, designed for builders. Use Geist Mono whenever a real number appears (€127, 3 days, 41 leads).

### Three lockup concepts
1. **Stacked Two Line.** BUILT on top, WITH AI underneath. AI in gold. Square-ish footprint. Best for avatar, favicon, banner corner.
2. **Inline One Line.** BUILT WITH AI in a single row. AI in gold, separated by a thin gold rule above and below. Best for newsletter header and banner.
3. **Bracket Mark.** [ BUILT WITH AI ] in square brackets. The brackets signal "code", which matches the niche. Best for end-card and lower-thirds in video.

### Icon / symbol (optional, keep simple)
A single right-pointing chevron `>` in gold, sitting just before the wordmark. Reads as a command prompt and as "forward motion". One stroke. Easy to draw, easy to remember, scales to a single character favicon if needed.

## 2. Color Palette

The existing King David palette stays the parent brand. For Built with AI we keep Navy and Gold (anchors the trust and faith story), drop Forest Green (cooler tones already do the work), and add high-contrast Cash Yellow plus pure black for thumbnail pop. Off-White becomes the newsletter and About surface, not the YouTube surface.

| Role | Name | Hex | Where to use |
|------|------|-----|--------------|
| Primary background (thumbnails) | Cash Yellow | #FFD60A | Thumbnail backgrounds, money callouts, highlight tape under text |
| Primary text (thumbnails) | Pure Black | #000000 | Thumbnail headline text, hard outlines, drop shadows |
| Brand anchor | Deep Navy | #0A1628 | Channel banner background, newsletter header, About card, end-card |
| Accent | Gold | #C9A84C | Logo "AI" word, banner accents, newsletter rules, faith-related copy |
| Secondary surface | Off-White | #F8F6F1 | Newsletter body background, gig description background, slide backgrounds |
| Supporting | Soft Charcoal | #1A1A2E | Body text on Off-White, secondary text on Navy where needed |

**Rule of thumb.** Thumbnails wear Cash Yellow + Black. Long-form brand surfaces (banner, newsletter, About) wear Navy + Gold + Off-White. Never mix all six in the same frame. Two surface colours plus the accent, that is the limit.

## 3. YouTube Channel Banner (2560 x 1440)

### Layout
The 2560 x 1440 canvas is split into three horizontal bands. The mobile-safe zone (centre 1546 x 423) holds the wordmark, the tagline, and the schedule. Everything outside the safe zone is decorative and can be cropped without losing the message.

### Text shown on the banner
- Top line (small caps, gold, letter-spaced): `IRISH BUILDER. ZERO CODING. REAL NUMBERS.`
- Centre line (large, off-white): `BUILT WITH AI`
- Sub line (medium, gold underline): `AI side hustle case studies, every Tuesday.`
- Bottom right tab (off-white pill): `New episodes weekly`

### Visual elements
- Deep Navy background fills the whole 2560 x 1440.
- A thin gold rule above and below the centre wordmark to echo the inline lockup.
- A single gold chevron `>` to the left of BUILT WITH AI for the command-prompt feel.
- No photos. No avatars. The banner is type-led on purpose to match the faceless format.

### Mobile-safe area
Anything outside the central 1546 x 423 box is decorative. On a phone, viewers only see the wordmark, the tagline, and the schedule. On a TV, they see the full Navy field with the chevron and the gold rules. The decorative band on the right shows three small gold tags reading `CASE STUDY`, `NO HYPE`, `REAL EUROS`.

## 4. Thumbnail Template (3 variants)

### Format rules (apply to every thumbnail)
- 1280 x 720 px.
- Background: Cash Yellow #FFD60A.
- Headline: Pure Black, Inter Black, 3 to 5 words maximum, all caps, tight tracking.
- One element only on the right third: AI avatar headshot, a screenshot, or a chunky gold arrow.
- Outline every text block in 8px black so it reads on phone screens.
- Bottom left corner: small `> BUILT WITH AI` tag in black on a white pill.

### Variant A · Avatar Reaction
- Big headline left two thirds. AI avatar bust on the right.
- Avatar's expression is wide-eyed or pointing.
- A chunky red circle around the key number in the headline (e.g. `€127`).
- Example: title `I MADE €127 IN 3 DAYS`. Thumbnail shows that exact text, avatar pointing at the €127.

### Variant B · Screenshot Proof
- Big headline left two thirds. Real screenshot on the right (Stripe payout, Gmail reply, Fiverr order).
- A gold arrow drawn from the headline to the screenshot.
- Black "RECEIPTS" sticker in the top right corner.
- Example: title `THIS COLD EMAIL GOT 3 REPLIES`. Thumbnail shows the headline with the actual Gmail inbox screenshot circled.

### Variant C · Failure Story
- Solid black background for failure videos (variant inversion). Cash Yellow becomes the text colour.
- Headline plus a single red X mark across a workflow diagram.
- Bottom strip in Cash Yellow: `WHAT I LEARNED`.
- Example: title `MY FIRST AI BUILD FAILED`. Black background, yellow headline, red X over a flowchart.

### Thumbnail examples for the first 3 videos
1. `I BUILT 29 AI TOOLS IN 7 DAYS`, Variant A. Avatar wide-eyed pointing at the number 29 circled in red.
2. `MY FIRST €100 ONLINE`, Variant B. Stripe payout screenshot on the right, gold arrow from the headline.
3. `THE COLD EMAIL THAT FLOPPED`, Variant C. Black background, yellow headline, red X over a flowchart of the email funnel.

## Production notes (for the AI tools)

- **AI avatar (HeyGen).** Pick a male avatar, 20s, neutral background. Outfit colour should match the brand. Plain navy crew neck on Off-White background. No tie, no suit. Keep it casual professional.
- **AI voice (ElevenLabs).** Clone King David's real voice. Test on the first script and tune for natural pacing. Keep the Irish accent strong, that is the wedge. Do not let the model flatten it into generic American.
- **Music.** None. Faceless cash cow channels in this niche almost always sound better with no music behind voice. Use light room tone instead.
- **Captions.** Burned-in, Inter Black, Cash Yellow text with black outline. Bottom third, centred. One line at a time.

## Faith filter (light touch, never preachy)

Faith shows up in the brand the way it shows up in real life. Quietly, in the values, not in the captions.

- The About says "done with integrity", not "God told me".
- The welcome email mentions "the honest way", once.
- The voice cheat sheet rules out preachy language.
- Sunday is a rest day. No content posts on Sundays.
- No promotion of gambling, alcohol, adult content, deception, get-rich-quick.

That is enough. The faith is in the behaviour, not the branding.
