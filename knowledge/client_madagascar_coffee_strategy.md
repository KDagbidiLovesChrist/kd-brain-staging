# Client Strategy · Madagascar Coffee Company (adjusted from research, 2026-06-03)
The Behance branding lead is a coffee company IN MADAGASCAR. Our default (Western local-cafe) plan does NOT fit. This is the corrected, evidence-backed approach. Full data: the Madagascar research agent findings (DataReportal, Statcounter, PayAtlas, Sprudge, etc.).

## The 6 facts that change everything
1. **Facebook = 90.5% of social activity** (4.69M users). Instagram only ~276k, TikTok negligible. → **Facebook + Messenger + Groups + WhatsApp is the channel, not TikTok/Reels.**
2. **Data is expensive** (~7% of income for a basic pack) → heavy video (Reels/TikTok) is too costly for most locals → **photos + text outperform video locally.**
3. **Language:** Malagasy (mass market, ~98%) + French (business/diaspora, ~23%); **English only for international specialty buyers.** Not English-first.
4. **Payments:** **Stripe is NOT available to Madagascar businesses.** Local = **mobile money (MVola, Orange Money)**. International/diaspora = PayPal/Wise or a France-registered entity.
5. **Platform monetisation is NOT available** in Madagascar (no TikTok Creator Fund, no YouTube Partner, no IG bonuses, no TikTok Shop). → "Monetise socials" = **direct sales (FB/WhatsApp → mobile money), diaspora orders, B2B export leads, tourism**, NOT platform payouts.
6. **The asset is the story:** Madagascar coffee (rare Bourbon Pointu / world's most genetically diverse wild coffee / biodiversity) is a genuine **"rarest origin"** hook for international specialty buyers, diaspora (France ~150k), and tourists.

## MARKET CONFIRMED (King, 2026-06-03): LOCALS primary, TOURISTS secondary
So: **NOT** a diaspora/export/B2B play. Two layers:
- **LOCAL (primary, where most revenue is):** Facebook Page + Groups + Marketplace + WhatsApp ordering + **MVola/Orange Money**. Content in **Malagasy + French**, **photos + text** (data cost), Facebook-first. This is the daily-customers + repeat-orders engine.
- **TOURIST (secondary, higher margin walk-ins):** **Instagram (French + English)** + **Google Maps + TripAdvisor** (tourists DO search these in EN/FR) + the **rare-origin Madagascar coffee story** + reviews. Tourists discover online, then visit/buy on site (cash/card/mobile money). The IG + viral-video + origin-story research applies HERE.
- Drop the diaspora/export/LinkedIn focus for now (not their market). Keep it as a future option only.
- Assume a **physical location** tourists can visit (confirm on call). Confirm their town + current socials + language preference on the discovery call.

## KEEP / DUMP / ADD vs our default plan
**KEEP:** the branding work (logo/palette/type/packaging/guidelines, a brand is a brand), content-calendar discipline, storytelling, consistency, quality visuals.
**DUMP (for local Madagascar):** TikTok/Reels-first, Google "coffee near me" local SEO, Stripe, platform-payout monetisation, email newsletter (use WhatsApp broadcast instead), heavy video for local.
**ADD:** Facebook Page as the flagship "website" + product catalogue + WhatsApp Business button · Facebook Groups/Marketplace · WhatsApp Business catalogue + order flow · MVola/Orange Money QR everywhere · Instagram (Fr+En) as the OUTBOUND portfolio for diaspora/tourists/buyers · LinkedIn for B2B export leads · WhatsApp broadcast lists as the CRM · (if international) a France/EU payment bridge.

## Adjusted platform priority (vs the Western default)
1. **Facebook** (Page/Messenger/Groups), local discovery + sales
2. **WhatsApp Business**, where sales close + the CRM (broadcast lists)
3. **Instagram (Fr+En)**, diaspora/tourists/international buyers (NOT local sales)
4. **LinkedIn**, B2B green-coffee export
5. **YouTube**, long-form origin story (trust/SEO, not local monetised)
6. **TikTok**, low priority (data cost + no monetisation)

## What this means for the proposal + the EMBER & OAK example
- The **branding half stays** (it's strong and universal).
- The **"presence engine" half must be reframed**: Facebook/WhatsApp/mobile-money-first, Malagasy/French, monetisation = real orders + diaspora/export/tourism, the rare-origin story as the international hook.
- The IG/TikTok viral-video research still applies, but to the **international/diaspora/tourist (Instagram/YouTube) layer**, not local. So that work isn't wasted.
- **Do not pitch Stripe, TikTok foot-traffic, or "coffee near me" to this client.** It would expose that the plan was built for the West.

Confirm their target market (A/B/C), their physical presence (café? farm? online-only?), and their language preference before finalising. The discovery call (call sheet) opener already asks the model question, lean into it.
