### The Core Positioning Shift (Most Important Thing)
Stop selling technology. Sell solutions to specific problems.

- WRONG: "I build AI automation tools"
- RIGHT: "I get Irish tradespeople 50 new leads per week without cold calling"

Every pitch, every Fiverr gig description, every DM must lead with the PROBLEM SOLVED, not the tool used.

### The First Client Strategy (From Lessons 5 + 8)
1. Pick ONE niche, plumbers (already have data from the scraping demo)
2. Build ONE demo, before/after showing a plumber with vs without a lead list
3. Approach 10 specific plumbers directly (not Fiverr first, direct outreach)
4. Offer first client reduced rate in exchange for a testimonial
5. Use that testimonial to validate Fiverr pricing and attract the next client

### Why No One Buys (And How to Fix It · Lesson 9)
If outreach gets no response, the likely reasons are:
- Pitch is too technical, business owner doesn't understand what they're buying
- Offer is too vague, no specific problem named, no specific outcome promised
- No proof, no case study, no demo, no visible result
- Price is wrong, too high for the trust level, or too low to be credible

Fix: one specific niche, one specific problem, one visible demo, one price.

### You Don't Need an Agency (Lessons 5, 8, course title)
Nate repeats this in three separate lessons. It is deliberate. King David can sell professional AI services as one person. No legal structure, no employees, no overhead required to start.
