# Faceless YouTube Playbook (5-agent research, 2026-06-01)

Reference for **Mom's faceless channel** + how we position the **/video-edit gig**. Built by 5 parallel research agents (niche/money · scripts/retention · algorithm/packaging · production/tools · ops/legal). Date-sensitive, re-verify policy before relying on it.

---

## 1. NICHE & MONEY

**RPM by niche (US traffic, ~3, 5× higher than SEA/South-Asia):**
- Finance/investing: **$15, 50** (highest on platform). Saturated overall; open sub-niches: business-collapse stories, scam exposés, credit-card optimization, FI for specific groups.
- AI/Tech (applied): **$8, 20**. Low, med saturation; SaaS affiliates (20, 60% recurring) often beat ad rev. Cheapest to produce (screen-record + VO + B-roll).
- Sleep/meditation/soundscape: **~$11**. Lowest competition (~20k channels).
- Psychology/human-behaviour: **$7, 13**. Low-competition sub-niches (dark psych, cognitive biases, body language); fast-growing; evergreen search.
- History/documentary: **$6, 14**. Long watch-time → more mid-rolls.
- True crime: **$5, 12**; "betrayal/revenge" sub-niche anomalously high (~$12.82), lower competition.
- Self-improvement/motivation: **$5, 12**. Generic is crushed; masculinity/discipline/stoicism still works (this is the Mubashir "Disappear & Transform" lane).

**Real faceless channels:** MagnatesMedia (~3M, business stories), ColdFusion (~5M, tech/business docs), How Money Works (~3M, animated finance), The Infographics Show (15M), Lofi Girl (15M, music). Established channels earn **50, 70% of income from NON-ad sources**.

**Monetization beyond AdSense:** affiliates (SaaS 20, 60% recurring; credit cards $50, 500/approval, one link can beat 100k ad views), sponsorships ($500, 2k/video at 10, 100k subs; faceless earns ~10, 25% less than face channels), digital products/courses (100% margin), memberships (faceless converts ~0.5, 2%).

**Top 3 niches for a NEW faceless channel (RPM × competition × ease):**
1. **AI/Tech (applied)**, exploding demand, cheap production, affiliate income from day 1.
2. **Finance/business documentary (storytelling)**, highest RPM + affiliate compounding; needs excellent scripts.
3. **Psychology/human-behaviour**, low competition, evergreen search, patient compounding.

**Red flags (avoid):** generic "Top 10 facts", compilation/slideshow channels, gaming ($3, 7 RPM), reaction content (copyright risk), Shorts-only (RPM ~$0.01, 0.13), political (advertiser-unfriendly), generic "make money online", undisclosed-AI. These are first targets of YouTube's 2026 inauthentic-content enforcement.

## 2. SCRIPTS & RETENTION

**Hook (first 7, 15s carries 100% of retention, no face to anchor):**
- Problem/curiosity-gap: "Most people who [X] never realise [costly mistake]…"
- Bold claim / expectation violation: "[Counterintuitive stat]. That's not what experts say."
- Cold open: start mid-action (quote/stat/soundbite), context at 0:10, 0:20.
- On-screen text in first 5s = +18% watch time; pattern interrupt in first 5s = +23% retention. Write 3 hook variants/video. Target: hold 70%+ through first 30s.

**Long-form structures:** Documentary (tension→payoff, open loop early) · Listicle/"X Truths" (reverse-order reveal) · Story/case-study (Before→Turn→During→After) · Video essay (thesis→deconstruct→defend).

**Retention mechanics:** open loop closed at 60, 70% (+32% watch time); re-hook every 2, 3 min ("but here's where it gets strange…"); pattern interrupt every 60, 90s (esp. at the 50% drop-off point); one idea per 60, 90s; visuals change every 3, 5s; cut AI pauses >0.5s; on-screen text for big claims (+65% retention). 2026 benchmark: 50% completion = strong signal.

**Length & monetization:** **8:00 minimum to run mid-rolls** (7:59 = none). Sweet spot **8, 10 min = 1 mid-roll (~$13 RPM)**, tight pacing. First mid-roll at 40, 50%; spacing ≥3, 4 min. Shorts: 15, 25s, loop the last frame to first, >70% completion = +30% distribution. A tight 8-min @55% AVD beats a padded 12-min @20%.

**VO writing rules:** ~150 words/min (9-min ≈ 1,350 words). Sentences <15 words, active voice, contractions ("you're"), `[pause]` cues at surprises, two-column script (spoken | visual cue), concrete specifics ("saves €10,000" not "saves money"), rhetorical questions every 2, 3 min. Tools: Claude (structured long-form), ChatGPT (fast hooks/shorts), Perplexity (research).

**Long-form beat template (~8, 10 min):** Cold hook 0:00, 0:10 (~75w) · Curiosity gap + promise 0:10, 0:35 (~100w) · Context/stakes 0:35, 1:30 (~175w) · Section 1 problem 1:30, 3:00 (~225w, re-hook) · Section 2 the turn 3:00, 5:00 (~300w, mid-roll ~4:00) · Section 3 evidence 5:00, 7:00 (~300w) · Payoff/close-loop 7:00, 8:30 (~200w) · Outro+single CTA + tease next 8:30, 9:00 (~75w).

**Shorts hook template (30, 45s):** punchline first 0:00, 0:02 (on-screen text) · curiosity escalation 0:02, 0:08 · fast process/evidence 0:08, 0:25 (cuts every 2, 3s) · re-hook/twist 0:25, 0:35 · loop moment 0:35, 0:45 (last frame blends to first; no outro).

## 3. ALGORITHM & PACKAGING (2026)

**Signal stack (by weight):** 1) Viewer satisfaction (surveys/likes/shares/returns), now outweighs raw watch time; 2) CTR (vs your own channel avg, not absolute); 3) AVD % (platform avg 23.7%; only ~1 in 6 videos >50%); 4) session contribution; 5) new-viewer attraction (2026 addition).

**Traffic sources:** Browse (Feb-2026 overhaul → deep personalization, favors niche), Suggested (session context + retention), Search (keywords + performance + intent), Shorts feed (decoupled from long-form; swipe-through/loop/first-frame). New 0-sub channels get aggressively tested, strong early CTR+retention can trigger broad testing in **days**. First few videos = algorithm training data.

**Title formulas:** specific number ("7 Investing Mistakes That Cost Beginners $10,000"), curiosity gap, compression promise ("30 years of X in 30 min"), warning/loss-aversion, year+action, versus, transformation story. Curiosity-first wins Browse/Suggested first 48, 72h; later swap to SEO-explicit title for Search's second wind.

**Thumbnails:** CTR benchmarks, <2% poor / 2, 4% weak / 4, 6% avg / 7%+ strong / 9%+ exceptional (compare to own avg). Faceless: no face needed <200k subs; use illustration/icon/outcome. Text 3, 5 words MAX, must **complement not repeat** the title. High-contrast (red on black ≈ 7% vs <5% low-contrast). Rule of thirds. Consistent template = +15, 20% subscriber CTR. First 24h ≈ 80% of the algorithmic push.

**Cadence:** VidIQ (5M channels), 12+/mo grew views 53% faster + 66% more subs vs 1, 3/mo. BUT max frequency you can sustain at quality; 1 great > 2 mediocre; daily uploads penalized if quality drops. New-channel plan: M1 = 2 long-form/wk (10 total); M2 = +10 Shorts; M3 = 2 long-form/wk + 1 Short/day.

**Shorts→long-form funnel:** combined channels grow 41% faster; 75% of Shorts views from non-subs; ~60% of fast-growing new channels used Shorts for the initial push. Never optimize Shorts for revenue ($0.01, 0.07/1k), optimize for subs; bridge to long-form ("full breakdown on my channel").

**First 90 days:** Wk1, 4 foundation (publish 8, 10 long-form fast = training data; track CTR/AVD%/sub-per-view only). Wk5, 8 optimize (double down on top 2, 3 by CTR+retention; add 10 Shorts; reply to every comment; target >50% long-form / >70% Shorts). Wk9, 12 monetize (2 long/wk + 1 Short/day; affiliate links top 10 descriptions; apply YPP at 1k subs + 4k hrs). Realistic day-90: 300, 2,000 subs, 2k, 8k watch hrs.

**Channel killers:** title repeated on thumbnail (73% do it), optimizing for wrong traffic source, overcrowded thumbnails, low-contrast colors, misleading packaging (2026 satisfaction surveys penalize), no consistent visual identity, weak first 10s, treating Shorts as income.

## 4. PRODUCTION & TOOLS

**AI voice:** **ElevenLabs Creator $22/mo** = 100k credits (~100 min), pro voice cloning (PVC), 192kbps, commercial, the right narrator tier. Pro $99 only adds volume. Fallbacks: OpenAI TTS ($0.015/1k chars, no cloning), Murf, Google Cloud TTS (cheapest at scale). **PlayHT is dead** (Meta acquired, API off Dec-2025).

**B-roll:** Free, **Pexels** (HD/4K, no attribution, API needs browser UA + Referer) + Pixabay. Paid, **Storyblocks $30/mo Unlimited** (incl. Content-ID protection; best volume value), Artgrid $20, 50/mo (cinematic). **Fair use is a courtroom defense, not a permission slip**, Content ID flags clips instantly; attribution ≠ fair use; avoid movie/TV clips on a monetized channel.

**Editing:** **Remotion + ffmpeg = correct core for an automated pipeline** ($0.05, 0.50/video, scriptable, version-controlled; visual ceiling ~7, 8). ffmpeg = encode/mux layer. CapCut/Premiere/After Effects = no API, manual only; AE = the high-end motion-graphics tier (needs a human). 

**AI visuals:** Midjourney $10, 60/mo (best stills, no clean API), Stable Diffusion (free/local, automatable). AI video: Kling $10 (cheapest ~$0.07/s), Pika $8, Runway $12, Sora $20+. AI video still weak on realistic humans/text → use for short atmospheric B-roll only.

**Music (cleared):** YouTube Audio Library (free, zero-claim), Uppbeat ($9.99 Creator, 3 channels), Epidemic Sound ($16.99/yr Creator, largest catalog + Content-ID protection).

**Captions:** **faster-whisper (local, free, word-level timestamps ~98%)** → ffmpeg burn-in or Remotion animated captions. Best for a code pipeline.

**Per-video cost (10-min, automated):** ~**$0.10, 1.00** marginal. Time: 1, 2 hrs with a mature pipeline (vs 5, 10 hrs manual).

**LEAN STARTER STACK ≈ $72/mo:** ElevenLabs Creator $22 + Pexels free + Uppbeat $9.99 + Storyblocks $30 + Midjourney $10 + faster-whisper free + Remotion/ffmpeg free. (For King's portfolio ad we only need ElevenLabs $22 + free Pexels + free music.)

**Honest ceiling:** Remotion+ffmpeg = indistinguishable from mid-tier human faceless content (~7, 8). Cannot match $500+/video AE motion-graphics (particles, 3D camera, compositing, "feel") without heavy custom work + a human. Pipeline = right for scale; human/AE = right for a flagship cinematic piece.

## 5. OPS, LEGAL & SYSTEMS

**Setup:** lock name + handle before first upload (changing fragments recognition). Logo PFP, 2, 3 consistent brand colors (the "face" replacement). Banner 2560×1440 (safe 1546×423), state promise + cadence. About: niche keyword in first 2 sentences, <150 words. Channel keywords (Studio→Settings→Advanced): 5, 10. Organize homepage by playlist/series.

**Monetization tiers 2026:** Early Access = 500 subs + 3 uploads + 3k watch hrs (or 3M Shorts views/90d) → memberships/Super Thanks/Shopping. Full YPP = 1,000 subs + 4k watch hrs (or 10M Shorts views/90d) → ad revenue. Need 2FA, no active strikes, AdSense linked. Realistic: 6, 18 months.

**#1 demonetization trap, reused/"inauthentic" content:** YouTube ran its largest-ever AI-channel purge in early 2026. Targets: AI videos with minimal human input, VO over reused clips w/o transformation, identical templates across 10+ videos, scripts that just read Wikipedia. **Moat = original script + editorial POV + unique framing + deliberate choices.** Bespoke-per-client is exactly right.

**Copyright/fair-use:** "Royalty-free" ≠ free for monetized YouTube, check the license explicitly. "No copyright infringement intended"/crediting = zero legal weight. Safe: YouTube Audio Library / Epidemic / Artlist for music; Pexels/Pixabay/Storyblocks for B-roll. Never use movie/TV/commercial-music even briefly (Content ID scans in minutes; 3 strikes = termination).

**AI/synthetic disclosure (REQUIRED toggle):** MUST tick "Altered or Synthetic Content" when AI makes a real person say/do something, alters a real event, or generates a realistic fake scene/another person's voice. **NOT needed for:** AI scripts/outlines/thumbnails/titles/captions, aesthetic edits, clearly-unreal animation, **or cloning YOUR OWN voice for YOUR OWN narration** (per YouTube Blog). Non-disclosure on realistic content → YouTube labels it itself + can suspend YPP channel-wide. Disclosure does NOT reduce reach/ad eligibility.

**Solo production system:** batch, research/topic bank (1 session/wk, 4, 6 ideas ahead), script batch (2, 3 at once), record VO batch, edit batch (reusable template), schedule all. ~20, 30 published videos before meaningful algorithmic data; plan 3, 6 months before strategic changes. Keep a 2-week buffer of edited videos. Simple sheet: Topic | Keyword | Script | Record | Publish | Status.

**Analytics loop:** CTR (<2% broken / 4, 6% good / 8%+ excellent), AVD (40%+ strong faceless), impressions (low+high CTR = audience not found yet, keep publishing), returning-viewers %. Publish → wait 48, 72h → if CTR<2% A/B new thumbnail/title → first-30s drop = weak hook, 50% drop = weak middle → 5%+ CTR & 45%+ retention = make more of that. +10pp retention ≈ +25% recommended impressions. Review every 4 weeks for patterns.

**Failure modes:** AI slop (demonetize), copyright clip (Content ID claim/strike), no AI disclosure on realistic content (YPP suspension), burst-then-silent (deprioritized), deleting flagged video pre-appeal (admission), single income stream (one policy change = €0), ignoring early retention.

**Stay-monetizable do/don't:** DO original scripts + own framing, cleared music every video, tick disclosure when realistic-AI, consistent 1, 2/wk, 2-wk buffer, monitor CTR + first-30s, keep flagged videos live while appealing, build an email list from day 1. DON'T mass-produce templated AI, use movie/TV/commercial music, rely on "no infringement intended", upload in bursts, skip disclosure, rely only on AdSense.
