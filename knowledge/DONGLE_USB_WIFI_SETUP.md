## Setup Steps (From Your Manual)

### **Step 1: Insert the SIM**
- **Where:** Small slot on the side or bottom of the device
- **What to use:** A standard mobile SIM (like your Vodafone phone SIM, or a data-only SIM)
- **How:** Push gently, it clicks in; to remove, push it in until it pops out
- **Your device:** ✅ Check if your SIM is fully seated (push it in until you hear a click)

### **Step 2: Plug In (Power)**
- **What you need:** USB cable + USB power adapter (5V, like a phone charger)
- **Where to plug:** USB port on the bottom/side of the device
- **Wait:** Device boots up (~30 seconds), lights turn on
- **Your device:** ✅ You've got the yellow/red cable in the photo — plug that USB end into the device

### **Step 3: Connect (WiFi)**
- **What to see:** Lights on the front of the device (usually green/blue = connected)
- **On your laptop:** Look for new WiFi networks, find one named like "Vodafone-XXXX" or "Vodafone Mobile WiFi"
- **Password:** Usually printed on a sticker (bottom/back of device) or in the manual
- **Connect:** Click the network → enter password → done
- **Your device:** ✅ You should see WiFi network(s) available from it

## "Always Connected Vodafone" Check

### What This Means
"Always connected" = the device stays powered, keeps the SIM active, and broadcasts WiFi 24/7. Mobile data keeps flowing as long as you have:
- ✅ Power (USB plugged in)
- ✅ Active SIM with data credit/plan
- ✅ Vodafone signal in your location

### Verify Yours Is Correct

| Check | What to Do | What You Should See |
|-------|-----------|-------------------|
| **Power** | Look at the device front lights | Green/blue light = powered and connected |
| **SIM inserted** | Hold the device, gently push the SIM slot | Should feel firm, not loose |
| **Vodafone signal** | Check the lights on the front (usually bars or dots) | Should show 2-4 bars = good signal |
| **WiFi broadcasting** | On your laptop, check WiFi networks | Should see "Vodafone-XXXX" or similar |
| **Connected** | Connect your laptop to that WiFi | Should get internet, test by opening a browser |

### If It's NOT Always Connected
- **Power died:** Plug USB cable back in
- **SIM not inserted:** Open the slot, push SIM in firmly, close
- **No Vodafone signal:** Move to a window or higher location (mobile networks need line of sight)
- **WiFi doesn't appear:** Restart the device (power off 30 sec, power back on)

## Common Questions

**Q: Is it the same as the WiFi router in the background?**
No. The router (beige box) is likely your fixed home WiFi. The dongle is portable — take it anywhere with you.

**Q: Can I use both at the same time?**
Yes. Use the router for home, use the dongle for travel or as a backup.

**Q: What if Vodafone credit runs out?**
The device stops pulling data. Recharge your Vodafone plan (or ensure you have monthly credit), and it works again.

**Q: Does it need the USB cable all the time?**
Yes, for power. It's not a battery-powered device; it needs constant USB power to stay on.

**Q: How fast is the internet?**
Depends on your Vodafone mobile plan (4G/5G speeds typically). Usually 10-50 Mbps, good for browsing and video calls.
