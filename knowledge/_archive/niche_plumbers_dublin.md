# Primary Niche: Dublin Plumbers

## The Decision
**Niche locked:** Dublin plumbers, first.
**Reason:** Already tested. 20 leads scraped in one run. Pain point is clear. Market is large. No competition for AI-powered lead gen at this price point.

---

## The One-Line Pitch
"I help plumbers in Dublin stop losing leads by building systems that follow up with every enquiry automatically, so they never lose a job because they were on a call."

**Never say:** "I build AI tools" / "I do scraping" / "I use Claude"
**Always say:** The outcome. The problem solved. The money saved.

---

## Why Plumbers (The Logic)
- A missed call = a missed booking = typically €200, €1,500 per job lost
- They're on-site all day, can't answer every call
- Most have no CRM, no follow-up, no automation, zero competition
- They need leads constantly, recurring service = retainer income
- Golden Pages, Yelp, Google My Business = easy to find + scrape
- Already proven: 20 Dublin plumbers scraped from goldenpages.ie in one run

---

## The Pain Points (Use in Every Pitch)
1. **Missed calls**, they're under a sink, phone rings, job goes to the next plumber
2. **No follow-up**, someone fills a contact form, nothing happens, they book someone else
3. **Slow to grow**, word of mouth only, no system to reach new areas
4. **Time wasted**, chasing quotes, typing emails, ringing old contacts manually

## Geographic Expansion Path (In Order)
1. **Dublin**, primary, tested, proven. Build reputation here first.
2. **Cork**, second city, same pain point, same approach
3. **Limerick / Galway**, after 3+ Dublin clients
4. **UK (London / Manchester)**, after Irish income is stable, higher volume

**Rule:** Don't spread wide before going deep. 10 Dublin clients > 1 each in 10 cities.

---

## Trade Expansion Path (After Plumbers Are Working)
Once the plumber niche is generating income, expand in this order:

1. **Electricians**, same pain (missed calls, no follow-up), slightly higher ticket
2. **Builders / Contractors**, bigger jobs, longer sales cycle, better for website upsell
3. **HVAC / Gas engineers**, seasonal demand spikes, needs active lead gen all year

**Rule:** Don't expand until plumbers are generating €500+/month recurring. One niche well > many niches poorly.
