# Living Room · Room Spec

*Floor: First · Date locked: 2026-06-19 · Subtotal: €1,578 (Phase 1) · Layout = show-home replica*

> **Layout = copied exactly from the Sherry Fitz "Aderig" show home** (same house type) + the furnished
> floor plan. **Finish = fresh** (greige walls · locked pale-oak floor · brass + black · olive-green & tan
> accent). Show-home bones, our own skin.

## 1. Concept
The rear of the open-plan ground floor, opening through double doors onto the terrace and flowing into the
kitchen/dining. A calm, cosy, modern living zone: sofa and a media wall facing each other across a low
table on a soft rug, a tan-leather accent chair catching the terrace light, brass warmth overhead.
Greige + pale oak + a **deep olive-green & warm tan** accent give it character without clutter.

## 6. Layout notes (to the floor plan · replicating the show home)
- The Living Room is the **rear ground-floor zone**; **double doors open onto the terrace** at the far
  (rear) end, and the space is **open-plan into the Kitchen/Dining** at the front end.
- **⚠️ GLAZING = ONLY the terrace double doors (rear wall).** Both side walls (the sofa wall and the
  media/TV wall) are **SOLID, there is NO window behind the sofa.** The street-facing front window belongs
  to the Kitchen/Dining, not here. *(Corrected 2026-06-20 after render QA, v1 wrongly added a side window.)*
- **Media/TV wall = the wall shared with the hall/stairs** (the tall block on the plan). The **BESTÅ TV
  unit + TV sit here.**
- **3-seat sofa sits on the opposite long wall, facing the TV**, exactly as the show home runs it.
- **Coffee table centred on the rug** between sofa and TV.
- **Tan accent armchair angled in the corner by the terrace doors**, catching the light (show-home spot).
- **Large floor plant** in the corner; keep the walkway from hall → terrace → kitchen clear.
- Mind the **radiator on the terrace-door wall** when placing the accent chair/plant.
