## The shared palette (both videos agree · this is the formula)

| Element | Direction |
|---|---|
| **Walls (base)** | Warm greige / soft taupe · exactly the "one shade warmer than builder white" rule. |
| **Feature walls** | A deeper **mocha / mink / taupe-brown** behind the bed in the master; **sage green** in a secondary/kids room. One feature wall per key room, not every wall. |
| **Accent colours** | **Mustard / ochre** + **charcoal/black** · repeated in cushions, throws, bedding, the under-stair nook. This is the signature warm accent. |
| **Flooring (ground)** | **Light· mid oak wood-effect** (LVT/laminate) through hall, living, kitchen/dining · warm, not grey. |
| **Flooring (upstairs)** | Neutral **greige carpet** in bedrooms + stairs (the show home carpets the stairs; the real house currently has bare pine · a decision to make). |
| **Kitchen** | **White handleless gloss units** · *the real house already has these*, so the look is achievable as-is; style with warm worktop + wood + greenery. |
| **Lighting** | **Brass / gold globe + drum pendants and mini-chandeliers**; round gold-framed mirrors. The "touch of luxury". |
| **Soft furnishings** | Upholstered/panelled headboards, textured neutral rugs, dried pampas/grasses, layered cushions, indoor plants. Cosy minimalist. |
| **Sofas** | Grey fabric sofa + a tan/brown leather accent armchair (Ref B). |

**One-line brief:** *Warm greige walls, light-oak floors, white kitchen, mustard + charcoal accents, mocha/sage feature walls, brass globe lighting, upholstered headboards, modern cosy minimalist with a touch of luxury.* This matches King's written brief almost exactly.

## Room-by-room cues lifted from the videos (starting points for `/fitout`)

- **Hall / stairs**, light-oak floor, round gold mirror, slim console + greenery, globe pendant. Stairs carpeted in neutral greige (decision vs the bare pine the house has now). **Under-stair nook idea (Ref A): a built-in bench seat with drawers + mustard/charcoal cushions**, strong, cheap-ish, characterful win.
- **Living room**, grey fabric sofa, tan leather accent chair, textured neutral rug, low coffee table, plant, media wall (Ref B recesses the TV into white cabinetry).
- **Kitchen / dining**, keep the existing white handleless units; warm wood dining table + grey upholstered chairs; dome/drum pendant over the table; styling with dried grasses + ceramics.
- **Master (Bedroom 1)**, **mocha/taupe feature wall** behind an upholstered (panelled or curved) headboard; charcoal + mustard bedding; twin bedside lamps; brass globe chandelier; round mirror. This is the "luxury" hero room.
- **Bedroom 2 / 3 (sons, 20 & 24 = adult)**, calmer: one accent (sage green seen in both refs), upholstered headboard, neutral carpet, simple pendant. Account for **sloped ceilings + Velux** (real house).
- **Bathroom / ensuite / WC**, already tiled + sanitaryware in; finish with sage or warm-neutral paint, round/arch mirror, brass tapware accents, towels + storage. Style, don't re-fit.
- **Terrace**, paved, timber bench seating (both refs) + planting; outdoor-room feel off the living/kitchen.
