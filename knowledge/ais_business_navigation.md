### Lesson 7 - Stop Selling Agents, Sell Solutions Instead
Video: 14:09 | PDF: Stop Selling AI Agents, Sell AI Solutions Instead.pdf (140kb)
Date posted: Sep 2025

The core shift in thinking:
Everyone has jumped on the "AI agent" trend. If you walk into a business and say "I sell AI agents", you sound like everyone else. You've become a commodity.

What to do instead:
Walk in and say "I noticed you're losing 5 hours a week on X. I can fix that."

That's an AI solution. The AI agent is just the tool you use to deliver it.
The business owner doesn't care about the tool, they care about the result.

The formula:
Identify a specific problem → Quantify the pain (time, money, frustration) → Offer the solution → Mention AI only as the mechanism

Example:
DON'T say: "I build AI agents for your customer service."
DO say: "I can reduce your customer response time from 4 hours to 4 minutes, and you'll stop losing customers who don't hear back."

Why nobody is buying AI agents:
Because businesses don't speak "AI agent." They speak "problem" and "money."

For King David: When approaching Irish SMBs (plumbers, electricians, cleaners, etc.), lead with the problem. "I noticed you're spending hours chasing leads. I can automate that." Not: "I do AI automation."

### Lesson 9 - Why No One is Buying (The TRUTH About Selling AI to Businesses)
Video: 12:28 | PDF: Why No One Is Buying Your AI Agents (and How to Fix It).pdf (141kb)
Date posted: Sep 2025

The truth Nate discovered: He used this method to fix his own business when sales were slow.

Why they're not buying, the 3 main reasons:

1. You're speaking tech, not business.
Business owners don't know what "n8n automation" or "AI agent" means. When you use that language, they check out.

2. You haven't shown them the ROI.
Businesses invest when they can see a clear return. "This will save you 10 hours a week" = $X per month in saved labor. That's a yes/no calculation.

3. Trust hasn't been established.
People buy from people they trust. If you're a stranger emailing them with tech jargon, they won't respond.

The fix:
- Speak their language (time saved, money made/saved, stress removed)
- Show the before and after clearly
- Lead with social proof (even a single testimonial changes everything)
- Start small, offer a free trial, a low-cost first project, or a demo

Key community comment: "Even with me being a business owner, I still wasn't approaching every aspect the right way. I needed to hear that."

For King David: This is the biggest unlock. His PT friend gives him a real case study to show future clients. "I built this for a personal trainer in Dublin, it saves him 3 hours a week." That sentence will close deals.

## SECTION 3: PRICING & DELIVERY

### Lesson 10 - Pricing Workflows (Without Losing Clients)
Video: 20:36 | PDF: How to Price AI Workflows.pdf (170kb)
Date posted: Nov 2025 | 343 likes

The core pricing problem:
- Price too high = you miss clients
- Price too low = you miss money (and signal you're not worth it)

Nate's pricing philosophy:
Price based on the VALUE delivered, not the time it took you.

The Value-Based Pricing Formula:
1. Identify what the problem costs the business (time x hourly rate = money lost)
2. Your price should be a fraction of that savings (typically 10-20% of annual savings)
3. Show the math to the client

Example:
- Business loses 20 hours/week on manual data entry
- At EUR 30/hour employee cost = EUR 600/week = EUR 31,200/year
- Your automation price: EUR 3,000, EUR 6,000 (10, 20% of annual savings)
- ROI for client: pays for itself in 5, 10 weeks

Pricing tiers (Nate's rough guide):
- Simple workflow (1, 2 nodes, no AI): $500, $1,500
- Medium workflow (multi-step, with AI): $1,500, $5,000
- Complex agent system (multi-agent, APIs, custom): $5,000, $15,000+
- Monthly maintenance/retainer: $200, $1,000/month per client

Key insight from community: "Pricing workflows is an art. If you don't know the value of what you're building, clients will decide it for you."

For King David, translated to his existing services:
- Lead gen scraper (already built): EUR 25, 90 is too low. Value-based = EUR 200, 500 for 300 leads + automation = ongoing retainer
- Website (already building): EUR 300, 2,500 is on track

## KEY FRAMEWORKS & SCRIPTS

### The Solution-First Sales Formula

Instead of:
"I build AI agents and n8n automations."

Say:
"I help [business type] save [X hours/week] by automating [specific task]. I've done this for [social proof]. Want me to show you how it would work for your business?"

---

### The Value-Based Pricing Calculation
1. Ask: "How much time do you or your team spend on [problem] per week?"
2. Multiply: Time x hourly cost = weekly cost
3. Multiply by 52 = annual cost of the problem
4. Your price = 10-20% of annual cost

Example:
"You spend 15 hours/week on manual lead follow-up. At EUR 25/hour that's EUR 375/week or EUR 19,500/year. I can build a system to automate this for EUR 2,000. It pays for itself in 6 weeks."

---

### The ROI Pitch Template

I noticed [specific problem].
This is costing you roughly [quantified cost] in [time/money/stress].
I've built a solution that solves this in [X hours/days].
Other businesses I've worked with have seen [result].
Would it be worth a 20-minute call to see if it fits?
