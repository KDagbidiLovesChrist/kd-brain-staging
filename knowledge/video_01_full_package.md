## 6. PRODUCTION NOTES FOR KING DAVID

### Voice recording
- **Script length when read at natural Irish pace:** ~10 min 18 sec.
- **ElevenLabs cost estimate:** approximately 1,650 words at the standard 1,000-character credit rate. Around 8,500 to 9,000 characters total. Fits inside one Creator-tier month easily. Budget around $5 for this video.
- **Recommendation:** record into ElevenLabs using your cloned voice, export as one continuous MP3, then chop in CapCut. Do not paste straight into HeyGen as a full block. HeyGen will lip-sync better with shorter chunks (2-3 sentences per chunk).

### HeyGen avatar
- Use the same avatar across every video for the first 90 days. Consistency = retention (per niche research, 23 percent retention bump).
- Render in 1080p. 4K is wasted on YouTube compression and slows export.
- Estimated render time: 25 to 35 minutes for the full 10-minute video.

### CapCut edit time estimate
- First edit: 6 to 8 hours (B-roll, screen recordings, text overlays, music sync).
- After the workflow is dialled in (video 3 onwards): 3 to 4 hours per long-form.
- **Tip:** record all 4 screen demos in one sitting before starting the edit. Saves context-switching.

### Submagic VFX moments
Add a sound effect at each of these timestamps:
- 0:00, soft "ding" when the €100 counter lands.
- 0:05, "whoosh" when the avatar enters frame.
- 0:20, page-flip sound on each numbered channel card (1, 2, 3, 4, 5).
- 5:00, terminal "tap" sound during the /scrape demo.
- 10:30, subtle "ding" on the subscribe button animation.

### Music recommendation
- **Mood:** warm, low-fi hip-hop or acoustic guitar. Slight melancholy, but hopeful. Not corporate, not aggressive.
- **Source:** YouTube Audio Library, filter for `Inspirational > Calm` or `Hip Hop > Chill`.
- **Specific tracks to try:** "Patience" by E's Jammy Jams, "Hopeful Freedom" by Asher Fulero, or any Lakey Inspired track.
- **Volume:** sit at -22 LUFS under voice (very low). Voice should always win.

- Tick the `Altered or Synthetic Content` checkbox when uploading.
- Add to description, top: `This video features an AI-generated avatar and AI-cloned voice of the creator. All numbers, screenshots, and stories are real.`
