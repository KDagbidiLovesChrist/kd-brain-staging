# The product thesis, in King's own words · 10 September 2026

The commercial half of [project_architecture_statement_2026-09-10.md](project_architecture_statement_2026-09-10.md).
That file said what the machine IS. This one says **what it is FOR, who buys it, and how they plug
in.** Recorded the day he said it, close to verbatim in shape. It supersedes nothing; it completes.

---

## 1. His claim, and it is a real one

> *"people have made bots and ides automations but i made the first engine to help you do everything
> in one simple place nice gui interface."*

The distinction is genuine and worth holding to. A bot answers. An IDE assistant edits code. An
automation runs one wired path. **What he built is a smart system that connects to your things,
asks you questions to understand you, and organises the lot.** His own shorthand: *"siri on craic",
"ricks garage level stuff."*

**Careful with the word first.** The category has occupants and a buyer will name them. What is
defensibly his: **the method is recorded once and runs free forever on the client's own machine,
deterministically and auditably** (his CREED ruling, `f5c5a2d8`). That claim survives a
procurement review. "First" does not. Sell the first sentence, never the second.

## 2. The two halves, as he drew them

**FRONT END, and this is the 5D reframe.** Not a page. **An app with the engine installed inside
it**, opening onto his whole infrastructure as a 3D universe with deep immersive scroll. Every
component of the real estate, walkable. His words: *"my vision for this is crazy."*

**BACK END, where he is working right now.** The smart system running on the maths and logic from
the KD master prompt. KD Robot is the orchestra agent; the LOGOI agents are the players; the engine
is what reaches out to data, cloud boxes and every AI.

## 3. The go-to-market, and it is the sharpest thing he said

> *"doing businesses easy first as we just connect to their bedrock main system."*

**This is correct and it is not obvious.** A business already has a cloud account with models
inside it. Logos connects to what they already own rather than introducing a new AI vendor.

**Why it matters more than he may realise:** it dissolves the legal blocker carried since 10 August.
Client traffic through their own Bedrock means **no new processor, no bespoke DPA with Anthropic,
no ZDR problem.** It is his 28 August Option C arriving by a different door, and it is the same
mechanism that makes MEDICAL viable
([project_logos_verticals_2026-09-10.md](project_logos_verticals_2026-09-10.md)).

**Three delivery shapes, all his:** an MCP server (already designed as Stage P), a downloadable app,
or a link. Access governed **per admin request**, with a firewall for data protection.

## 4. His own DCEO use case, and the one hard flag

> *"if i log in as dceo using it i can use logos and connect it to my logos... me as dceo can get kd
> robot to help me do admin and day to day work like investigating."*

The use case is real and it is the best demo he owns: a data centre engineer whose assistant can
read and reason across internal and external components.

**⚠️ THE FLAG, and it is the sharpest open item in the whole product.** Connecting a personally
owned system to Amazon internal systems on a work profile touches **two** things at once: his
employment contract (IP assignment, moonlighting, invention clauses) and Amazon's own security
policy on unapproved tooling. The downside is not a fine. It is **the job, and arguably the
ownership of Logos itself.**

**Ruling that follows from his own constitution, not from caution:** build the DCEO lane as a
capability, demo it on **his own hardware with synthetic data**, and let **a solicitor** clear the
real-estate connection before a single credential is entered. This is the same solicitor item
already open on his Amazon contract; today it went from important to blocking.

---

## 5. The one architectural consequence that changes a build

**The engine will hold admin credentials into a client's cloud estate.** That is what "connect to
everything through correct admin access" means in practice, and it makes the engine the
highest-value target in their infrastructure.

**Three things his existing rulings already answer, which is why this is buildable:**
- **Sealed copy** (8 Sep): their box, their keys, their bill. The credentials never come to him.
- **The floor** (LOGIC-B): private lanes never leave the machine.
- **The audit trail**: every action provable, which is exactly what an enterprise security review
  asks for and what most competitors cannot show.

**What is NOT yet answered:** credential storage and rotation inside a client instance, and
least-privilege scoping per LOGOI agent. Neither is designed. Both are Stage 6D work.

## 7. ⚠️ THE THING TO PROTECT: do not let the vision eat the buildable piece

Stage 4 in `plans/hi-bubbly-kitten.md` is a **small phone control centre**: ask, status, commit,
approve. It is days of work and it closes the "laptop is off" problem he has actually been living
with.

What he described today is **the 5D universe app**, which is Stage 5 and Stage 7 territory: months,
and it needs the interview first (iota 7.0a) because a face built before the interview is a guess.

**They are not the same build and merging them kills the small one.** The phone control centre
should ship as the plain, fast utility it was specced as; the universe is what it grows into once
the backend he is writing now is finished. Recorded so a future session does not quietly widen
Stage 4 into something that never lands.
