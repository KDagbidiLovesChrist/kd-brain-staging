## THE NAME, settled 2026-08-26

**LLLLLM = LARGE LANGUAGE LOCAL LAYMAN LOGOS MACHINE.** Five Ls. His words, verbatim.

Earlier drafts in the record, superseded and kept only so an old note is recognisable:
"Layman Local Language Logos Model" (25 Aug), then "Layman Local Large **Logic** Model"
(25 Aug, when he corrected Language to Logic). **The five L version above is the current one.**

Each word is doing work:

    LARGE      the intelligence is rented and it is frontier grade
    LANGUAGE   the interface is plain speech, not a console
    LOCAL      the governance, the data and the guards are on his machine
    LAYMAN     the reader sees their own language, never the maths
    LOGOS      from the Word, John 1:1. The naming is his and it is deliberate
    MACHINE    it does work and leaves proof, rather than answering and forgetting

## THE PROMPT LAYERING, his words 2026-08-26

> "FROM KING'S LLLLLM WILL BE TRANSLATED TO THEIR LANGUAGE FOR THEIR PROMPT WILL BE ADDED ON
> KING'S PROMPT OF LOGOS ENGINE"

So a client instance is built in layers, and the order is the whole point:

    KING'S MASTER PROMPT      the constitution. the base. NEVER shipped, never seen.
         + CLIENT'S PROMPT    their principles, their business, sitting ON TOP
         + THEIR DATA         SOPs, runbooks, whatever they hand over
         = THEIR WORLD

**The base is what they are paying for and what they never receive.** It is why he said Klarnow
need him working on the code: the master prompt is his and the client prompt only makes sense
sitting on it. That is the commercial lock and it is structural rather than contractual.

And it is already architecturally true: `_ops/LOGOS_0_KING_PROMPT.md` (36KB) and
`_ops/LOGOS_MASTER_PROMPT.md` (17KB) are in his brain, guarded, and referenced NOWHERE in the
engine source. They have never shipped. Verified 2026-08-25.

## THE TWO RENDERINGS, built 2026-08-26

One decision, two readers:

    tools/logos_policy.receipt()   THE OPERATOR. The derivation: which rule fired, on what
                                   evidence, at what ceiling, cleared to leave or not.
    tools/logos_plain.full()       THE LAYMAN. The same verdict in their own language, ending
                                   in CHOICES rather than a verdict.

**The layman view is not a simplification.** It is a higher bar than the maths, because a number
cannot be misread and a sentence can.

## KING'S CONFIDENCE RULE, added the same day

> "aim for 100% as much as possible. If the maths is showing lower than 50% at each stage the
> user should be warned of consequences for FAILURE AND SUCCESS. But it is all probable, as
> anything is actually possible."

Bands, with the engine's own bars (`BAR_BY_TIER` in `sovereign.ts`) plus his new floor:

    >= 90   sovereign   as settled as this system gets, and still not proof
    >= 80   strong      reasonable to act on
    >= 50   mixed       read it properly, do not accept it
    <  50   WEAK        warn on BOTH outcomes

**THE HALF PEOPLE MISS, and he got it right:** below the line, a SUCCESS is more dangerous than
a failure. A failure tells you it was wrong. A lucky success tells you nothing, and you build on
it, and you trust the next weak answer more than you should.

Nothing in the wording ever says "impossible" or "certain". He is right that anything is
possible, and a system that overclaims certainty in either direction has stopped being honest.

## THE OVERRIDE LOOP, his design

The default routes on probability. The user is the only one who knows they are in the 10 percent
where the default is wrong. So: the receipt ends in choices, the override is recorded WITH the
reason, and after enough of them **the log shows where the rule is wrong and it gets corrected
from real evidence rather than from a guess.** The engine already does exactly this for a RED
tier publish: override allowed, reason required, kept permanently in the approver's name.
