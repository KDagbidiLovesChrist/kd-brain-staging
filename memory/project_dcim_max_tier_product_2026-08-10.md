# DCIM as the max tier, opened 2026-08-10

**King's words:** the DCIM capability should be a skill you only get at the **maximum paying tier**. It shows a
client how to build their own data centre, how to run the operations effectively, and helps them build their
own cloud services and their own LLM or software products on that LLM.

## Why this is the strongest vertical on the list

King is a **DCEO at Amazon**. Data centre engineering operations is his daily work. Compared with the
healthcare and stem cell vertical opened the same morning ([[project-healthcare-stemcell-vertical]]), which
sits in a field he has never worked in under the heaviest regulation there is, this is:

- **Real, non-fakeable domain expertise.** A moat no competitor copies and no model invents.
- **The natural next question** for any enterprise just sold "own your own LLM": where does it run.
- **Consistent with §0b**, the tier changes the door and never the effort.

## 🔴 The flag that moves from pending to urgent

**The Amazon employment contract review.** Already flagged twice in the brain and still unreviewed. This
product is where it bites, for three specific reasons:

1. Outside-work, non-compete and IP-assignment clauses are standard in that role
2. **AWS is a cloud provider.** Helping companies build their own cloud is arguably competitive activity
3. Anything learned inside Amazon that is confidential cannot be sold at any price

None of this makes the product impossible. People consult in their field constantly. It means **a solicitor
reads the contract before this becomes a product line**, not after the first client signs.

## Two claims to state precisely, or be held to the wrong one

**"Build your own LLM" is three products at three prices.** Running an open model on their own hardware
(what King already does with Ollama) is very achievable. Fine tuning an open model on their data is
achievable. Training a foundation model from scratch costs tens of millions plus a GPU cluster and almost
nobody does it. **A buyer who hears the third and receives the first has a dispute, even if King was right.**
Name which one, every time.

**"The smart machine will cover the cost" needs a number before it is spoken.** A data centre is capital
expenditure in the millions. AI can genuinely cut operating cost and improve efficiency; it cannot fund
capex. If the meaning is that service revenue funds the build, that is a real argument and it needs a model
behind it. Same class as the "80 percent increase" King caught himself on.
See [[feedback-hold-king-to-literal-accuracy]].

## Tier rule that attaches automatically

§0b: **the tier changes the door, never the effort.** Max tier gets *more doors*, never better-quality work on
the same door. Tier limits are **published, never silent**, because undisclosed degradation of a paying
client's output is misrepresentation.
