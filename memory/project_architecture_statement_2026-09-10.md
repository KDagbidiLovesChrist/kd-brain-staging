## TWO CONFLATIONS, corrected on the spot

### 1. A game engine is not an LLM engine

He asked to add "other engines like Google and Fortnite gaming or GTA or gaming and other engines
from GitHub" to make Logic A and B stronger. Unreal, Unity and the GTA engine render graphics and
simulate physics. **Bolting one onto Logic A does not make the model think better.** Different
machines, different jobs.

**His instinct underneath it is sound, and it splits cleanly into two things he can genuinely have:**

- **More minds, for the mind.** Adding MODELS does make both hearts stronger: more Ollama models,
  Google's, open ones from GitHub and Hugging Face. That is the router and the lanes, already built.
  Real, cheap, and the right reading of what he wants.
- **A graphics engine, for the FACE.** For the command centre, GUI and landing page a real 3D engine
  is exactly right, and the base is already found: **AUREO**, his own finished Three.js work,
  researched for Stage 7.

**Game engines for the face, more models for the mind. Both are already in his plan. They are not
the same upgrade.**

### 2. Hermes does not run on a phone

He again placed Hermes on the phone. Hermes is a **Windows agent**. If Hermes runs the phone, the
phone dies when the lid shuts, which is precisely what Stage 4 exists to prevent. **His own ruling
of 9 Sep already solves it: Hermes as a RUNG, not a door.** Oracle serves the phone, Hermes answers
while the laptop is awake, and the walk steps past that rung when it is not.

### And once more, lightly: he is not training the local models

He said he is "training His local models with automations". He is giving them **memory**, not
training: the master prompt and the recipe book. Proven on his own disk on 9 Sep, both models
pointing at one identical weights blob. It matters only because it tells him **where the compounding
actually happens: in the recipes, never in the weights.**
