## The shared recipe (the 4 fast ones)
1. **~1 second per cut** (40-53 cuts in the first minute).
2. **A recurring MASCOT** = a simple face/icon **motion-tracked onto real people's heads** in stock b-roll, expressions changing to match. THE signature device, keeps it faceless but warm/branded/funny.
3. **Real-people stock b-roll = ~half the video** (offices, shopping, trades, beaches). This is the visual VARIETY.
4. **Big bold centered captions** (Montserrat/Impact, white + black stroke), change every 2-4 words, **keywords colored** (yellow/green/red), pop-in synced to voice.
5. **Money/idea motion-graphics**: falling cash stacks, hand-drawn $ scribbles, red circles, arrows, counters, graphs, zoom-punches, screen shake.
6. **A SOUND on every movement** ("sonic anchoring"): whoosh on every cut/pan, ding/pop on text, click on UI, cash-register on money.
7. **Transitions** = hard cuts masked by zoom-punches + whip-pans + flash frames. Vibrant saturated grade, occasional film grain/letterbox.

## Why my promos kept missing ("doesn't look like the videos")
- **No mascot**, mine had no character at all.
- **Website-flashes instead of real people**, same KIND of visual every cut = flat & same-y. Refs use varied real-human b-roll.
- **Not enough sound + caption density**, refs hit a sound + a new 2-4 word colored caption on almost every cut.
- It was a **materials** problem, not an editing-engine problem. Reskinning an engine could never fix it.
