# King's ruling: the first verticals are MEDICAL and EDUCATIONAL · 10 September 2026

His words, ~01:00: *"through klarnow wod vps it will help businesses for medical and educational
itll b a[n app]"*.

So the Klarnow instance, running on its own VPS, serves **medical and educational businesses**, and
the thing a customer touches is an **app**. That is 6D and 7D getting their first named markets, and
it is the first time the empire has had a stated industry rather than "companies".

---

## Why his architecture is unusually well suited to this

Most AI companies cannot sell into a clinic or a school at all, because their product ships the
customer's data to the vendor's own cloud. That is the wall the whole sector hits, and it is why
serious healthcare AI is thin on the ground.

**He already ruled the thing that clears it, on 8 September, for a completely different reason:**
Klarnow gets a sealed copy, their own box, their own keys, their own bill, never access to his
engine. He decided that to protect his source. It turns out to be the single most valuable property
a medical buyer could ask for.

Underneath it sits the stronger claim: **his floor is a model running on the customer's own machine
that cannot run out and cannot be repriced.** For a clinic, "the patient data never left this
building" is not a feature on a list. It is the purchasing decision.

**And Stage T, built the night before this ruling, is the control that demonstrates it.** Retrieval
is travel-aware: local yes, cloud no, enforced on the way OUT rather than at index time, failing
closed when a caller does not say where it stands. That is exactly the mechanism an auditor asks a
processor to show. It was built to protect his own records; it doubles as the compliance evidence.

---

## The two hard requirements, neither optional

**1. Medical data is special-category under GDPR.** Not ordinary business data. Before a single
patient record touches anything it needs a written DPA, a lawful basis, and almost certainly a
DPIA. His brain already carries the shape of this from 10 August and it is still unresolved:
*the moment the engine processes a client's data, client = controller, engine = processor.*

**2. Any cloud model that sees patient data becomes a named sub-processor**, which must be disclosed
and contracted. His own record states ZDR requires a Commercial Anthropic org and he is on consumer
terms, so that route is closed to him today.

**Both dissolve the same way, and the way already exists: run the medical vertical on the FLOOR
ONLY.** No cloud rung in the chain at all. `LOGOS_LOCAL=1`, the lane constraints, and the travel
gate are already built. A clinic instance is simply a world in which the paid rungs do not exist.

**Education adds a third if it touches under-18s**, which is a further regime again on top of GDPR.
Worth establishing before a school is approached, not after.

---

## What this changes about the road

Nothing needs rebuilding. It sharpens what Stage 8 and 9 have to prove:

- **A no-cloud instance must be provable, not promised.** The boundary test that already exists for
  tenancy needs a sibling: a Klarnow-shaped instance where a cloud call is impossible by
  construction, and a prover that fails if one ever becomes possible.
- **The app is a new surface.** Everything so far is a deck, a phone page and a terminal. "It'll be
  an app" means a real product front end, and that belongs in Stage 5 (the face) rather than being
  discovered late in Stage 8.
- **The legal work is now on the critical path, not beside it.** It was deferrable while the buyer
  was hypothetical. A named vertical of medical makes the DPA and the lawful basis a gate on the
  first sale.

---

## Open, and King's to decide

- **Does the medical instance run floor-only from the start?** The honest recommendation is yes: it
  removes both hard requirements at a stroke, costs him nothing he has, and is the thing that lets
  him sell where competitors cannot.
- **Which comes first, medical or educational?** They have different regimes and different buying
  cycles; picking one to prove the pattern is cheaper than chasing both.
- **The legal instrument.** This needs a solicitor, not a brain. Same conclusion as the 9 August
  cap-table work: the family and a professional, in his own time.

**Nothing here is built or committed to. This is his direction recorded on the day he stated it, so
the next session starts from it rather than rediscovering it.**

Their instance needs admin-level credentials so **their devs and King** can work in it. This is a

- **The sealed copy stands.** Admin keys for THEIR instance, on THEIR box, never keys into his. The

### THE CORRECTION THAT AFFECTS WHAT HE MAY CLAIM

He described agents that **"learn and keep skills and intelligence from other AI models"**. An agent
cannot absorb another model's intelligence. Nothing transfers between them. What his system
genuinely does is **record a working method once and reuse it free forever**: the recipe book, the
return path.

**That is not a lesser claim, it is a stronger one for this buyer.** "Our AI learns from GPT and
Claude and gets smarter" is unverifiable and fails the first due-diligence question. "The method is
recorded, auditable, and runs deterministically on your own machine afterwards" is true, provable,
and is what a hospital IT lead actually wants to hear.

**Say the second thing. Never the first.** It is also the one his own architecture already does.
