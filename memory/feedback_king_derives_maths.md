# King derives the maths. Claude prepares and records.

**King's instruction, 2026-08-09:** the apologetics can be *"linked from the bible translated to
maths and derived from bible and church fathers, and king will derive the maths and help it with
encryption like how uncle tony said."*

## The direction
**Scripture and the Fathers (verified) → the structure they already stated → the maths King derives
from it → the language layer that encrypts it (with Tony) → the same layer pointed outward as the
seed.**

The arrow runs outward from Scripture, never inward onto it. Finding the shape that is already there
is honest. Inventing a shape and then decorating it with verses is not, and it is the version that
gets taken apart.

## The hands, which are not interchangeable
- **King derives.** His, by his own hand. This is not modesty on Claude's part, it is Rule 23, and it
  is also what the invention record needs while the IP position is unsettled and the patent is
  unfiled. A record showing King deriving it is worth more than one showing an AI produced it.
- **Claude prepares and records.** Assemble the verified sources, lay out the candidate structures,
  then write down what King actually derived. Claude is the study aid, never the author of the maths.
- **Uncle Tony encrypts.** He formalised the operator with King and holds the equation already.

## Two guardrails that must travel with the work
1. **The maths never claims to prove the faith.** It expresses a structure the Fathers already
   stated. St Vincent's canon was a three axis filter before anyone wrote it as an intersection;
   Philippians 2 had the descent before anyone named Tesler. Translation of a shape, never a proof
   of God. The moment it reads as proof it hands every critic a free win.
2. **Godel is used only for "a system cannot certify itself from inside", never stretched to
   "therefore God".** That stretch is the single most abused move in religious argument.

## Source discipline (non negotiable, from `rules\faith-sacred.md`)
Never fabricate. Verify and cite every verse, quote and historical claim. **Scripture is NKJV for
the New Testament and the OSB Septuagint for the Old**, differences flagged. Anything quoted in KJV
in earlier work gets re-quoted to NKJV. Any Father without a traceable citation is marked
`NEEDS VERIFIED LESSON` and described rather than quoted.

## Attribution, learned the hard way this session
The constraint monotonicity law (nothing downstream may loosen anything upstream) was **King's own**,
said to Uncle Tony on 2026-08-08 as *"sub-agents can never violate the master prompt, clients cannot
override the seed."* Claude formalised it in notation the following night without knowing it was
already his. **Check the record before crediting anything to this session.** Most of it is already
his, said earlier, in his own words.
