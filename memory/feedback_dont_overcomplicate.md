King's words (2026-06-10, mid kd-site-v3 build): **"why are you changing and over complicating."**

**Why:** During the gold-background work I kept flip-flopping (background in → out → far back → flagship →
back in) and adding clever detours instead of doing the simple thing he asked. It cost trust and time.

**How to apply:**
- When King states a simple want, do **that one thing**, the simplest way. Don't bolt on extra structure,
  reverts, or "while I'm here" changes.
- If a change must be undone, undo it cleanly and quietly, don't narrate a saga.
- Still **confirm each step when he asks** ([[feedback-discovery-intake-before-building]]), but the step
  itself should be minimal and on-point.
- Two technical corollaries from the same build: **use real-time 3D particles, never a flat image, for
  "living" gold** (flat pics don't read as moving on his phone, iOS Low Power Mode freezes rAF); and
  **never put competing visuals in the KD particle hero's space, showcase them as their own flagship.**
