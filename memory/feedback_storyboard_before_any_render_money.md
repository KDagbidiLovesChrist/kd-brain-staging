# Storyboard FIRST, approve, THEN build (never both at once) · money + control

**King, his words (2026-06-28):** "I need to see the storyboard before anything. You can't be doing the
storyboard and the video at the same time, because you're wasting money. Plan exactly how I want it, so
I'm not wasting money and it goes how I want it to."

**What happened:** I decoded the reference, then went straight to building a fully rendered MOVING test
(paid image-to-video + image gens) to "prove the style" BEFORE King had approved a storyboard. It matched
the reference recipe but was "not my standard of quality / not what I was looking for." Building before
the visual plan was approved = wasted spend AND wrong direction.

**Why:** A render is expensive (paid gen) and locks in choices. A storyboard is free and is where King
shapes the concept, script, look, character, captions, pacing, and quality bar to EXACTLY what he wants.
Approving the plan first means we build the right thing ONCE, no wasted money, and he stays in control.
This is the whole point of Rule #18 (visual storyboard before any visual build).

**How to apply (every video, no exceptions):**
1. Build the storyboard with FREE tools only (HTML/chrome mockups, existing assets, no paid gen, no i2v).
2. Make it a DEEP VISUAL board (see [[reference-deep-storyboard-standard]]): Brief/Design Sheet +
   Character Model Sheet + shot-by-shot frame mockups with the per-shot fields (emotion, VO line, caption,
   on-screen content, the motion technique, SFX, transition), start to end.
3. Deliver it, invite his redlines, and **do NOT build, render, or spend a cent until he approves it.**
4. Do not run the storyboard and the production build in the same pass. One gate at a time.
5. When approved, build it once to spec, then REFERENCE-QA it ([[reference-reference-qa-standard]]).

**Also:** King holds a high quality bar (his other work is top-tier). A faceless TikTok still must feel
premium and be HIS taste, not just "matches the reference." The storyboard is where that bar gets set.
