# Standard: Everything Top-Tier ($1M / Apple-grade)

**The rule (King David, 2026-05-31):** from now on, every website, online store, demo, and the
portfolio itself must look top-tier, "$1 million, Apple-style." Premium, richly interactive,
complex-but-functional, deployable and production-ready. Never ship basic or template-feeling work.

**Why:** King David charges €99 to €10k+ for builds that are often the same hours. What the client
actually pays for is the *feeling* of premium. The design quality IS the sales pitch and the pricing
justification. Cheap-looking work caps his rates; top-tier work unlocks them. This is a money rule,
not a taste rule.

## What "top-tier / Apple-grade" means concretely (the build checklist)
- **Typography first:** large, tight, confident headlines; a refined type system (e.g. Inter / SF-like
  for clean Apple feel, Fraunces / serif for luxe). Real hierarchy, generous line-height.
- **Space + restraint:** generous whitespace, clear grid, nothing cramped. Apple = clarity + calm.
- **Deliberate motion:** smooth scroll-reveal entrances, parallax, pinned/horizontal showcases,
  number counters, magnetic buttons, custom cursor (desktop), frosted-glass sticky nav, page-load
  reveal. Motion should feel intentional, not flashy.
- **Craft details:** soft shadows, rounded corners (~16-22px), grain/gradient texture, hover states
  on everything interactive, dark feature sections for drama against light.
- **Robust / production-ready:** progressive enhancement (content visible if JS/CDN fails), fast,
  mobile-perfect, accessible, deploys clean to Vercel (use the CLEAN public alias, team-scoped = 401).
- **Honest:** demos use fictional brands or the client's own assets; footer credits King David; never
  impersonate a real business or claim unwon work as a delivered client.

## Positioning strategy (King David, 2026-05-31)
Every customer should feel they're "coming to the best." The play: **premium quality at low intro
("founder") pricing** to establish himself + win the first clients and reviews, then raise prices and
scale. So the WORK must look €10k while the intro price is €99, that gap (premium look, founder price)
is the whole hook. The top-tier standard exists to make that gap believable. As proof/reviews stack,
prices climb.

## Template + pricing tiers (productized, 2026-05-31)
Two-tier productized system, every client feels VIP, KD's framing: "the nice car everyone can have."
- **Tier 1, 3D Flagship:** real-time WebGL/3D, interactive, the "hard to say no." Refs: kd-3d.vercel.app,
  vanta-store-six.vercel.app (store), flagship-demo-dusky.vercel.app. Premium/custom price. In the pitch + portfolio.
- **Tier 2, Premium Standard:** clean, fast, mobile-light premium (lighter motion, no heavy WebGL).
  Everyday small-biz clients + mobile conversion. Founder price (sites €99 + €39/mo, stores €249 + €59/mo).
- Pitch shows BOTH tiers with prices; customer picks. Heavy WebGL isn't right for every niche/cheap phone.

## CRITICAL: every demo is UNIQUE to its brand (not a shared template) · KD 2026-05-31
The constant is the QUALITY LEVEL + the 3D/interactive CAPABILITY, NOT the look. Each store/site gets a
bespoke design: its own layout, palette, typography, and a 3D/interactive treatment chosen to fit that brand
(fragrance = glass/liquid essence · streetwear = dark mono distortion · architecture = horizontal pinned
gallery · gym = kinetic energy · etc.). NEVER reuse one identical template across demos, that repeats the old
"146 demos = 2 layouts" weakness KD called out. When KD says "every demo has this template," he means the
LEVEL/engine, applied uniquely every time. Variety + bespoke fit per brand is a hard rule.

## CINEMATIC + VIDEO extension (KD 2026-05-31, session 5)
The top-tier standard now **explicitly includes "cinematic"** and applies to **every visual KD makes, including
video.** Videos (ad reels, gig videos, social shorts) must be cinematic: dramatic over-the-top narration, deep
cinematic colour grade, slow push-ins + punchy on-beat cuts, bold clean text + amber/gold subtitle captions,
epic building music, hook in the first 2 seconds. Reference style = KD's 6 chosen TikToks (`reel\refs\`) blended:
cinematic-dramatic (diditee) + bold clean text cards (sebastien) + motion graphics. Format = vertical 9:16.
