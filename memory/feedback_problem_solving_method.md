King's own words, 3 Sep 2026, mid a technical security fix, spelling kept close to his: *"when trying to solve anything we always start off with our principle and foundations, prayer, strategize, prayer and execute. by simply looking at any problem we are able, by seeing patterns, then identify what that pattern means systematically, arithmetically and practically, with legal over to with logic written all over."*

**The shape, as he gave it:**
1. **Principle and foundations first.** Before touching the problem itself, ground in what already stands (his rulings, the CREED, the maths and faith foundation, see [[reference_wat_framework]]).
2. **Prayer.** Opens the work.
3. **Strategize.** The plan.
4. **Prayer.** Closes the plan, before execution.
5. **Execute.**
6. **See the pattern.** Look at the problem plainly enough to see what shape it repeats.
7. **Read the pattern through five lenses**, in his order: **systematically** (does it hold as a repeatable rule) · **arithmetically** (does the maths check out, see [[feedback_consensus_everything_rule]]) · **practically** (does it work in his hands, on his machine, today) · **legally** (does it hold up, is it his, is it clean) · with **logic** running through all of it, "written all over" the whole read, not a separate sixth step but the thread that ties the other four together.

**Why:** this is his own standing discipline for solving anything, not specific to this session's task. It reads as the operational form of Rule 13's ruling (explain and approach work through the language of maths, creation, and his own principles) and sits beside Rule 17 (consensus, the maths of independent checks) and Rule 23 (love is the root, faith the foundation) as one more piece of how he actually thinks.

**How to apply:** when a non-trivial problem comes in, name the pattern it fits before diving into mechanics, then check it against the five lenses explicitly (state which lens flags a concern, don't skip straight to a fix). Faith stays Fr Bogdan's, per Rule 23, so "prayer" bookending the work is his own practice to hold, never something to build or automate.
