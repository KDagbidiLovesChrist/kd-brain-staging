# kingdavidagbidi.com · the brand look to MATCH (viewed live 2026-06-06)

## Visual language
- **Background:** a living GOLD NEBULA / cosmic galaxy on black (breathes, drifts). A gold ringed PLANET appears. NOT flat black, NOT a grid.
- **Type:** elegant **SERIF** for the big lines (looks like Cormorant/Playfair) with gold accent words. Small labels in caps/letterspaced. (My earlier promo used Inter sans + flat black = WRONG.)
- **Colour:** black + warm gold (#C9A84C / #F4C94B), off-white serif text.
- **Logo:** KD coin (gold ring + KD) top-left, matches the mascot I built.
- **CTAs:** "Book a call" (gold pill), "Free sample first", "SEE SAMPLE / OPEN DEMO".

## Brand lines (use these in the video)
- "Leverage AI to grow your business" (hero)
- "Online and on the tools. I build your whole presence with AI, so you save time, money and effort and win more customers. Free sample first."
- "I build worlds, not just websites." (with the gold planet)
- "AI that does the work for you." (AI/automation section)
- Services eyebrow: WEBSITES · STORES · AI · APPS · VIDEO

## How gigs are presented (copy this structure; NO pricing)
Each gig = a LIVE DEMO preview card + gig name + one-line benefit + "OPEN DEMO/SEE SAMPLE". Examples seen:
- **Nova**, "AI · intelligence distilled" (enterprise AI / SaaS)
- **Meridian**, "Architecture · where light meets form" (website)
- **KIN**, "Built for the streets" (streetwear shop)
- **ATLAS**, "Spaces that…" (interior design)
- **AI Automation (NEXA)**, "The repetitive work, handled, day and night"
- **Custom AI Assistants (Rivella)**, "A chatbot that answers, qualifies and books for you"

## Demos to FEATURE in the video (realistic only)
Nova, Meridian, Veloce, KIN, Lume, Roast & Co, Atlas, Orbit (SaaS), + AI assistant (Rivella). URLs in [[reference-king-services-audience]] / portfolio. EXCLUDE the 3 "Immersive 3D Worlds" (Levi 3D, VEYRA 3D, FLUX 3D) = the "PS2 3D look" King rejects.
