# Build originals at that caliber · never clone a live site

**Boundary (2026-06-09, after a long push):** King loves reference sites (8bit.ai, open-sbs.brig.ht/city) and repeatedly asked to **"make me a website exactly like this, just change the brand to Claude / Bank of Ireland and the colour."** That specific thing, reproduce a live site's design part-for-part with a brand/colour swap, **we do NOT do.**

**Why (frame it around protecting King, not rules):**
- It's the reference owner's IP. A recognisable clone invites a takedown + a "this guy just rips off other people's sites" reputation hit, the opposite of what builds his name.
- A recoloured copy isn't something he can resell or sign his name to.
- His own earlier standard already says this (the igloo.inc work: "build originals at that quality, not 1:1 clones").

**What we DO instead (the big yes):**
- Capture the **genre, the feel, the quality, and the interaction TECHNIQUES** (smooth scroll-jacking, loop-back scroll, continuously-moving background, narrative story-scroll, 60fps no-lag optimization), techniques are fair game.
- Build it **original**: original AI-generated visuals, original layout + copy, his brand/colour. Same *experience*, his own design.

**How to apply:** when King asks for an "exact copy," don't keep offering wiggle room (it invites more pushing). State the boundary once, clearly, kindly, then pivot hard to the original-at-that-caliber build and the rest of his work. He came around and got EXCITED once the original layered approach (image + Seedance + 3D + scroll) was framed as "the ultimate website." Method: [[reference-ultimate-website-layered-method]].
