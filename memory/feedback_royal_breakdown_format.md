# 👑 The Royal Breakdown · King's preferred deep-explanation format

**Why:** King (2026-07-04) asked for a saved prompt capturing the breakdown style he wants: not a summary, not bookkeeping — the FULL story, "as if you are explaining every single minute of ups and downs, what we learned and where we are today."

**The trigger:** when King says **"royal breakdown"** (or "the full breakdown", "massive breakdown"), deliver this format. He can also paste the prompt below.

## The prompt (King can say/paste this any time)
> "Give me the royal breakdown of [topic/period]: walk me through it minute by minute — every up AND every down. Name every error plainly and what law it became. Show the journey era by era, where we stand TODAY with real numbers, what changed in how we work, and why it compounds from here. Plain English, story-first, no bookkeeping summaries, no hiding mistakes, visuals where they help, God first."

## How to apply (the format contract)
1. **Numbers first** — real growth metrics up top (before → now).
2. **Chronological eras** — the story in order, each era named, with its WINS and its ERRORS side by side. Errors get named plainly (no softening, no "doesn't count") + the exact law/lesson each one birthed.
3. **Where we are TODAY** — honest current state, including the €0-so-far type truths.
4. **How the MODEL changed** — then vs now, the way of working itself.
5. **Why it compounds from here** — the flywheel: error → law → memory → every future session inherits it; each mistake is paid for ONCE.
6. **Visual alongside** (Rule 18 spirit): an HTML/graph page in `_ops/` when the breakdown is big — reference: `_ops/THE_LEARNING_CURVE.html` + `_ops/KING_OS_MAP.html` (King-approved examples of the standard).
7. Plain English, story voice, faith-first framing, zero em dashes, ends with the mission and God.
