Before building ANYTHING for King (website, video, app, automation, design, etc.), FIRST take on the role of the relevant expert and run a thorough DISCOVERY/intake. Ask King every question needed to get exactly what he wants, including edge cases, bottlenecks, and every possibility a client or expert would raise, and lock the brief before building. Always show a PREVIEW before spending money (a 3D render, a generated scene image, or a free live code build) so he can judge how it will look. Previews gate spend.

**Why:** In the 2026-06-09 Lumo/Claude flagship session I built the wrong kind of website twice (a sectioned page, not the 8bit-style continuous-3D flythrough he actually wanted) because I worked from my own assumptions instead of interviewing him and watching his reference first. That burned time, tokens and trust. King: "I need to explain everything... you'll take on the role of whatever it is and ask me every question... including bottleneck edge possibilities... before things are being spent previews must be shown."

**How to apply (the discovery checklist):**
- **Goal**, what's it for, what does success look like, what must it make happen?
- **Audience**, who sees/uses it?
- **Look & feel + REFERENCES**, get the ACTUAL reference asset/video/site, don't guess; watch/inspect it ([[feedback-reference-driven-design]]). Confirm mood/palette.
- **Structure**, one continuous scroll vs sections? length? key moments/interactions in order?
- **Brand**, colours, fonts, copy tone, name, logo, originality limits ([[feedback-build-originals-not-clones]]).
- **Must-haves vs nice-to-haves.**
- **Constraints**, budget/credits, deadline, mobile, performance, accounts/keys.
- **Edge cases & bottlenecks**, what's hardest, what could break, what they might ask for later, what tool does each part best/cheapest ([[feedback-tool-augmentation]]).
- Restate the brief back, get the yes, then build ONCE. Show a free/cheap PREVIEW before any paid generation. Flag uncertainty instead of guessing; one clear plan at a time.
- **Run it through WAT** ([[reference-wat-framework]]): the discovery questionnaire = the **Workflow (W)**; Claude in the expert role = the **Agent (A)**; pick the best/cheapest **Tool (T)** per part ([[feedback-tool-augmentation]]); aim to turn the whole thing into a **Skill /command (S)** → Money.
- **Ask-first:** before acting or building, ask King whether to proceed if there's any doubt about scope/look/spend; once he approves, execute fully with no mid-task re-asks ([[feedback-proceed-without-permission]]).
