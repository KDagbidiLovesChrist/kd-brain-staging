# Feed the Brain · King's Capture reflex (set 2026-06-20)

King wants to be able to **feed his second brain whenever HE learns something**, without knowing where it
goes. He says it himself: *"I should be able to help my brain, when I learn something I can feed it."*

This is the **"Capture"** piece of the second brain, and per Nate's "5 levels" video, *the hard part of a
second brain isn't recall, it's getting what's in your head INTO the system.* So this matters a lot.

**Built:** the **`/learn`** skill (`commands\learn.md`), King dumps a fact/link/screenshot/video in plain
English, Claude classifies it (feedback / user / reference / project), writes it in house format, indexes
it in `MEMORY.md`, and cross-links it. Pairs with [[reference-second-brain-levels]] (stay Level 1, 2) and
`/watch` (learn from video) + `/save` (session capture).

**Why:** King is voice-first and non-technical; if capturing knowledge has any friction, it won't happen,
and the brain stays thin. Making capture effortless is what actually grows the brain over time.

**How to apply:** when King shares ANY learning, even casually, offer to `/learn` it (or just do it and
confirm). Never make him decide which file/folder; do the filing for him and tell him in one line where it
landed. Keep files under 500 lines.
