# Upwork search playbook (where King's demos win)

King can't be auto-fed Upwork jobs (login wall). He searches, forwards the good ones, Claude does A+B ([[feedback-demo-first-AB-standard]]). These are the searches that land on jobs his live demos already prove.

## Set filters once
Sort: **Newest** · Proposals: **Less than 5** · Client info: **Payment verified**.

## Search terms by tier (type one at a time)
**Tier 1, n8n / AI automation (hottest, strongest demos):**
`n8n` · `n8n developer` · `n8n automation` · `n8n AI agent` · `n8n RAG` · `AI agent` · `AI automation`
- Proof: Contractor Engine (kd-n8n-proof) for RAG/agent; Studio Flow (kd-studio-flow) for service-biz automation.

**Tier 2, GoHighLevel + service-business automation:**
`GoHighLevel` · `GHL automation` · `Meta lead ads` · `lead automation` · `booking automation` · `CRM automation`
- Proof: GHL DSCR engine (kd-ghl-engine), Studio Flow.

**Tier 3, AI tools / chatbots / SaaS:**
`AI chatbot` · `RAG chatbot` · `customer support AI` · `AI content generator` · `OpenAI developer` · `Supabase` · `Next.js` · `SaaS dashboard`
- Proof: CopyForge (kd-copyforge, live AI content tool), Relay CRM (relay-crm-one), Contractor Engine.

**Tier 4, websites (web work):**
`landing page` · `website developer` · `Webflow` · `Framer` · `recruitment website`
- Proof: OceanHire (kd-oceanhire), portfolio (kd-demos.vercel.app/portfolio), cinematic flagships.

## The green-light bar (apply if most hit)
<5 proposals · posted last 24-48h · **payment verified** · in a tier above · a demo proves it · budget realistic.

## DON'T type these / SKIP on sight (Connect-burners + traps proven this session)
- `Make.com` (mandatory-Make gate; King builds n8n) · `Remotion`/`video editor` · `Odoo` · `lead generation specialist` (SDR/outreach, not a build) · `CAD`/`3D modeling`/`Blender`/`Unreal`.
- **$10 / micro-budget jobs even with a PERFECT skill match**, e.g. the $10 "n8n RAG agent" from a client with $60 total spent across 18 hires. Pay-vs-skill mismatch + underpay pattern = skip. Wait for the same job with a real budget.
- **Payment NOT verified** (e.g. the $300 "Ops Systems Architect") = below bar.
- Full-time/on-site/timezone-gated; off-platform (Telegram/WhatsApp/crypto); free "test"; gambling/alcohol/adult/occult.
