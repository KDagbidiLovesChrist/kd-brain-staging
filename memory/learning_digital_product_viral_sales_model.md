## The model the creator teaches (4 steps, as King described it)

1. **Create a digital product**, built in **Canva** (King said "canvas" = Canva).
   Digital products = templates, ebooks, planners, guides, presets, etc., make
   once, sell infinitely, near-zero cost per sale.

2. **Create a store**, somewhere to sell/deliver the product (platform not named
   in the recap, confirm on watch; likely Stan / Whop / Gumroad / Shopify type).

3. **Go viral to get customers**, using the **"TSV framework"**.
   **TSV = TITLE · SCRIPT · VIDEO** *(confirmed by King 2026-06-25)*.

4. **TSV in practice = copy what already works.** Take a video that already went
   viral and copy its **exact Title, exact Script, and exact Video**, use that
   proven combination to ride the same wave and pull the same attention. The
   attention is then funnelled to the offer (the store / digital product).
   - **T, Title:** reuse the proven, high-performing title (the hook that won).
   - **S, Script:** reuse the proven script/structure word-for-word.
   - **V, Video:** reuse the proven visual format/edit.

## The core idea (why it works)

- **Don't gamble on original content, copy what already proved it works.** A video
  that already went viral has a proven format; replicating it (the model + the
  context) borrows its proven pull instead of guessing.
- **Views = attention = customers.** The viral video isn't the product, it's the
  top of the funnel. It grabs attention, and attention gets routed to the store /
  the digital product. Organic reach replaces paid ads.

## The underlying principle (King's distillation, 2026-06-25)

> **"It's just about finding an award-winning product, then copying what already
> works to get attention and customers."**

The whole strategy = **remove the guesswork on BOTH ends by copying proven winners:**

1. **Winning PRODUCT**, don't invent a product and hope. **Find one that's already
   selling / already proven** (an "award-winning" / best-seller). Product research
   first = de-risk the offer. (Same logic dropshippers use to find "winning
   products", but for digital products.)
2. **Winning CONTENT**, don't gamble on original videos. Use **TSV** (copy the
   proven Title + Script + Video) = de-risk the attention.

**Proven product × proven content = attention + customers, with the guesswork
taken out of both halves.** That's the engine. Marketing here isn't creativity, it's *finding what already works and copying it well*.

- **FRONT half = the ENGINE (get attention), IDENTICAL for all three.**
  Find what already works → copy it with **TSV** (Title/Script/Video) → get attention.
- **BACK half = the OFFER (the money), this is what DIFFERS** per person: what you
  actually sell once you have the attention.

Analogy: **same fishing net (viral content), different catch (what you sell).**
