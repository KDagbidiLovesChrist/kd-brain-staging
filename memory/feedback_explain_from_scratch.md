Always explain concepts from scratch in plain English, even when King David clearly already knows the topic. He may say "I know this" or reference prior knowledge, but he still wants the full explanation, hearing it clearly explained is how he locks things in and teaches others.

**Why:** King David is a visual and auditory learner who processes through explanation, not through reading technical summaries. Even familiar concepts benefit from a fresh, clean narration.

**How to apply:** Never say "as you know" or skip a concept because it appeared earlier in the session. Treat every explanation as if it is the first time. Use plain English throughout. Analogies over jargon.

Also: use descriptive names for all files and screenshots. The name should tell you what the file contains before you open it, e.g., `client_acquisition_funnel.png` not `screenshot_1.png`. King David specifically called this out as something he likes.
