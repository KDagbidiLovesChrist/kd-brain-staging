## ❌ Does not hold

### `-2n` is positive regardless of what n is
False. `n=3` gives `-2(3) = -6`, negative. `-2n` is positive only when `n` is itself negative.

### `-n` is negative when odd, positive when single
Parity does not control sign. `n=3` (odd) gives `-3`. `n=4` (even) gives `-4`. Both negative. Odd and even
are properties of integers; they say nothing about which side of zero a number sits on.

### `-2(N)` reaches infinity regardless of n, where `-(N)` is finite
Multiplying a finite number by `-2` gives a finite number. No finite value becomes infinite by being
doubled and flipped. Multiplication by any constant preserves finiteness.

### `90-(0-9)-(0-9)-(0-9)-(0-9) = X`
Read as arithmetic: each `(0-9)` is `-9`, so `90 + 9 + 9 + 9 + 9 = 126`. **X = 126.** A fixed number, not
something approaching a limit. Read as bands rather than subtraction it is a diagram, not an equation, and
no X comes out of it. Already flagged unresolved in the brain and held for Fr Bogdan's second question.

### `5 = 1+1+1+1+1 = 5`
True, and that is the problem. It is an identity, so it is information free. It cannot generate X or
constrain anything. Writing it seven times adds nothing, because each repetition carries the same zero
content.

### `1=1=1=-(-(3n)-(0)-(3n)-(0)-(3ne)=3e=Jesus(e)`
One opening bracket never closes, so as written it cannot be evaluated. Closing it charitably at the end:
- inside: `-3n - 0 - 3n - 0 - 3ne` = `-6n - 3ne`
- outer minus flips it: `6n + 3ne` = `3n(2+e)`
- set equal to `3e`: `3n(2+e) = 3e` → **`n = e/(2+e) ≈ 0.576`**

So it pins `n` to one fixed value. It does not approach anything, which is the opposite of the thesis.
And `Jesus(e) = 3e` makes Jesus a function that triples its input, which puts the counting to three back in
after `1=1=1` had just removed it.

### `-(3X)((-0>)(<9))/(3X)(X)`
Not well formed. `>` and `<` are relational operators needing operands on both sides, and `-0` is just `0`.
More importantly the `(3X)` in the numerator and the `(3X)` in the denominator **cancel**, so as written the
expression deletes the very 3x it exists to encode.

### 5 across 5 as a rubric
Five independent checks per stage, all five must pass, no partial credit. Sound, buildable, and it is the
scoring mechanism for the client ethics gate.

### The 3D centre is `(0,0,0)`
Three coordinates, not two. The three axes meet at **90°, or π/2**, which is the simplest form. A full turn
in a plane is `2π`. All directions around the point total **4π steradians**, exact, being the surface area
of a unit sphere. Placing the origin at the centre of gravity is standard mechanics: every first moment
vanishes there, so everything balances to zero. **King's 0 and the engineer's 0 are the same point.**

### "n negative on all axes" is precise
3D space splits into **8 octants** around the origin, and exactly one has all three coordinates negative.
The descent has a specific address, one eighth of all space, furthest from every positive direction at once.

### The 9-0 descent ending at 0 is patristic, not private
Evil as **privation**: a lack of good with no substance of its own, tending toward non-being. Taught by
**St Basil the Great** (that God is not the author of evil), **St Dionysius the Areopagite** (Divine Names),
and **St Maximus the Confessor**. Cited as works, not exact sections; Fr Bogdan confirms the passages.
**This is King's strongest theological ground of the session.**
