## ⚠️ THE COST READ, and it is the one thing to change

**Capability is not the only axis. VOLUME is the other, and it is the one that decides the bill.**

Looked up 10 September, now in `tools/logos_lanes.py`:

```
gpt-6-astra          10.00 in   50.00 out     <- his design, coding, structure AND organising
claude-fable-5-1     10.00 in   50.00 out     <- his planning
claude-opus-5         5.00 in   25.00 out     <- his planning
claude-sonnet-4-5     3.00 in   15.00 out
claude-haiku-4-5      1.00 in    5.00 out
gemini-3.8-flash      0.75 in    3.75 out
logos-qwen3:8b        FREE, cannot run out
```

**As ruled, his two highest-volume roles sit on the two most expensive models.**

- **Planning is low volume.** A plan is written once and read many times. Fable or Opus there is
  correct and cheap in practice, because you rarely do it.
- **Coding, structure and organising are HIGH volume.** They run all day, every day, over and over.
  **Astra at 10 and 50 for that is the single most expensive configuration available**, and it is
  60 times the cost of Haiku on output and 13 times Gemini's.

**The efficient version of his own rule:**

| job | volume | the right rung |
|---|---|---|
| judgement, rulings, planning | **low** | Fable, Opus. Correct as ruled. |
| 3D and 2D mockups, vision | medium | **Gemini** is strong here and 13x cheaper than Astra |
| coding, hard architecture | medium | Astra earns it on the hard parts only |
| **structure, organising, refactoring** | **high** | **Haiku or Sonnet**, not Astra |
| bulk, repetitive, overnight | **very high** | **the floor. Free, and slow does not matter overnight.** |

**And above all of it: the recipe book.** A job solved once runs free forever. For anything
repetitive, that beats every cheap model, because it costs nothing rather than less.
