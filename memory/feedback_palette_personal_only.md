**Black + gold is Kingdavid's PERSONAL brand, used ONLY on his own portfolio hub
(kingdavidagbidi.com / kd-hub).** It must NOT be applied to client work or demos.

Every client site, store, or capability demo gets **its own world**, the client's (or the
demo brand's) own colour, tone, font, motion and 3D idea. This is the long-standing rule
(pink was LUMÉ-only; never one shared template), see [[feedback-top-tier-design-standard]]
and [[project-premium-3d-venture]].

**Also:** KD's logo = a clean **"KD" monogram in type** (gold gradient + thin gold rule), minimal and realistic.
A sphere/orb is NOT his logo. ⛔ **The old gold-RINGED KD badge is KILLED (2026-06-06): King said it looks
cartoonish/unrealistic, do NOT use the ring monogram anymore.** Use the minimal type-based KD mark
(see `KdMark` in `Built With AI\remotion\styles\MubaPromo.jsx`); roll it onto his sites when the website
work resumes (currently deferred).

**Why:** mixing his personal palette into client demos makes everything look same-y and
undercuts the "bespoke, built-for-you" pitch. **How to apply:** when building any demo, pick
a palette/style that fits THAT brand's niche, not KD's black+gold. Corrected 2026-05-31 after
LUMINA was wrongly built in black+gold (rebuilt in its own teal/coral world).
