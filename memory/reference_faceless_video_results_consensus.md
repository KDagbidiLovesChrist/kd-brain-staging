# Faceless short-form that gets results · the consensus method (2026-06-26)

Built from a 3-lane multi-source consensus scan ([[feedback-multi-source-consensus-research]]): creator
tactics (video tutorials), retention DATA, and the free production workflow. All three converged.

## The chain (in order of what actually drives results)
1. **Completion rate = the #1 driver** (~40-50% of algorithm weight). Target **~70% watched**. Everything
   else just feeds this. A 45s video at 70% beats a 15s at 40%.
2. **First 3 seconds decide everything**, ~71% leave by second 3. Hook in **~2.5s** (~10-14 words),
   open mid-action, no intro. Proven hook types: pattern-interrupt, concrete numbered promise, pain-point
   question.
3. **Animated burned-in captions are non-negotiable** (the FACELESS multiplier, no face to hold them).
   ~69% watch muted; captions lift completion ~66% -> ~91%, +12-25% watch time. 3-5 words, high contrast,
   keyword highlighted.
4. **Keep it moving:** cut every **2-4s** (micro-cuts <1.2s in the opening), **slow zoom on every clip**
   (100% -> ~108-110%, drift not zoom = biggest single watch-time lever), pattern interrupt every 5-7s,
   **SFX on every cut**, music ducked low under voice, no dead air.
5. **15-30s for most ideas** (80%+ retention zone; 40s only for real tutorials). Length is a lever ON
   completion, not a driver. Shortest length that delivers the payload.
6. **Engineer for shares + saves** (3-5x the weight of likes; Mosseri: "sends per reach" = top Reels
   signal; how-to/reference content gets SAVED) and **loops/rewatches** (20-30% rewatch is strong; loop
   the ending so end frame flows into the open).
7. **Post 3-5x/wk, ONE niche, consistency > bursts** (vidIQ 5M-channel study: 12+/mo = 53% faster view
   growth). **BATCH 5 at a time** (2-hour sprint = a week), the universal pro secret.
8. **Keywords > hashtags now** (algos parse spoken audio + on-screen text + captions). Say + show + write
   the search phrase identically. 3-5 specific tags + 1 broad.

## The proven FREE tool stack (cited by 3+ independent sources)
ChatGPT/Claude (script) -> ElevenLabs/TTSMaker or **King's real voice** (voice) -> **Pexels + Pixabay**
(b-roll, CC0, no watermark) + **Canva** (graphics/screen-record) -> **CapCut** (edit + free auto-captions)
-> export 1080p 9:16 watermark-off -> post. ⚠️ **2026 honesty:** CapCut added a paywall (free auto-captions
capped ~10 min/mo) but free still works for shorts; free caption fallbacks = **CaptionX Studio**,
**Reel Video Captions** (no watermark).

## Honest flags (don't overstate)
- Exact %s are from analytics blogs, **directional not lab-proven**. Strongest hard numbers: the **vidIQ
  5M-channel cadence study** + the **Verizon/Publicis captions study**.
- **"AI avatars get +40% watch time" = ONE vendor selling avatars. Ignored** (also conflicts with King's
  faceless decision, real voice, no avatar).
- Best b-roll source (stock vs AI vs screen-record) is **niche-dependent**, no clear winner, match the
  visual type to the topic.
