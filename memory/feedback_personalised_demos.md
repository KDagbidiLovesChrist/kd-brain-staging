When doing demo-first outreach, each prospect must get a **real, personalised, transformative rebuild of their own site**, not a link to one of King's existing showcase demos. King rejected a batch (2026-06-02) where prospects #2-#11 just pointed at generic flagship demos (lume-clinic, axion, ambre, etc.), *"NONE OF THE DEMOS ARE PERSONALISED! ALL OF THEM NEED TO BE A REBRAND AND TRANSFORMATIVE!"*

Each demo must:
- **Recreate THAT business's own style, tone and preferences**, pull their real name, colours, fonts feel, voice and content from their live site, then rebuild it *elevated*. It should read as their brand leveled up, not a different premium brand pasted on. ("stack them in everything and try recreate that")
- **Build in the specific thing their current site is missing** (e.g. before/after gallery + online booking for clinics, working cart for stores, immersive gallery for builders, 3D showroom for kitchen makers).
- **Be right-sized to the budget they can honestly afford**, most small local businesses get a clean, premium, simple build (Signature tier), NOT a forced €4k 3D flagship. Save heavy 3D for genuinely high-ticket visual trades (kitchens, luxury homes). ("they may not afford it")
- **Be RICH**, multiple custom sections, premium feel, even for smaller businesses.
- **Each its OWN world**, never reuse one palette/template look across businesses [[feedback-palette-personal-only]].

**Workflow rule:** King reviews EVERY demo before he sends anything. Claude preps + deploys + QAs, King approves, King sends from his own accounts. ("show me before send")

**Honesty:** do not fabricate awards, named testimonials, credentials or photos. Use clearly-labelled placeholders ("your real photos/reviews here"). If a prospect already has a strong modern site, say so and do not fake a teardown.

**How to apply:** For each prospect, WebFetch their site for brand + content, clone the closest existing proof as a structural base, re-theme fully to their brand, build in the missing feature, deploy to its own Vercel project ([slug].vercel.app), verify HTTP 200 + zero console errors + screenshot, then present to King for review. Use parallel agents to build many at once. Related: [[project-website-sales]], [[feedback-top-tier-design-standard]].
