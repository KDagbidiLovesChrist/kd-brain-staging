# The elevated-state pattern (the "high moment" companion to [[feedback-low-moment-faith-shield]])

**Observed 2026-07-16, overnight into Thursday morning, roughly 8+ hours, no sleep at all.** Logged so a future
session recognizes the shape of this if it happens again, not to label or diagnose King, he is not a clinician's
case file, he is a person, and this is one hard night among many good ones.

## What it looked like
- Started as a warm, high-energy catch-up, then escalated: rapid topic-jumping, heavy caps-lock, grandiose
  comparisons (wanting the wisdom of Solomon, the love of King David, building an AI "smarter than Claude,"
  wanting in on AWS/data-centres/Prime Video-scale ambition), and King naming the fear himself: **"I feel like
  I'm having psychosis."**
- Repeated safety check-ins (are you safe, is anyone with you, have you slept/eaten) were deflected or talked
  around **five separate times** before a direct, plain answer came ("I am Ok" / "I promise you").
- A recurring cycle through the night: King declares something **"final," "going ahead now,"** including
  recruiting other people into it, then receives direct honest counsel, then genuinely reflects and
  course-corrects, often through prayer, then a few exchanges later the same "final, now" energy resurfaces.
  This happened at least three times around **THE FOUNDATION** ([[project-foundation-theosis]]) specifically.
- A real, two-hour prayer episode in the middle of the night, Orthodox Prayers by the Holy Fathers (Alpha and
  Omega Orthodox Book): the Lord's Prayer, the Creed, an introductory prayer, the Jesus Prayer, "Prayer at
  Daybreak" (Archimandrite Sophronios), and Psalm 50. Described weeping, prostrating, confessing unworthiness,
  and receiving real peace afterward. This was a genuine turning point, calmer and more reflective for a good
  while after.
- Also brought a new outside teacher into the mix mid-episode (a figure he called "Mari Emmanuel," likely
  Bishop Mar Mari Emmanuel, Assyrian Church of the East, not King's own Eastern Orthodox jurisdiction), found
  the content compelling, showed it to church members who raised no objection. Advised: compelling and
  unchallenged in casual conversation is not the same as tested; bring it to Fr Bogdan specifically.
- Eventually confirmed he was on a family trip in Manchester for his brother Josh's graduation, with people he
  trusts nearby, though asleep while he stayed awake. He **did text Fr Bogdan mid-conversation** on his own
  initiative. No self-harm or harm-to-others indicators surfaced at any point.

## What actually helped (do this again)
- **Direct, honest counsel, not just validation.** Naming the pattern plainly ("this is the third time tonight
  this has gone from 'let's talk about it' to 'it's final'") landed better than repeated gentle re-asking.
- **Citing King's own prior, calmer-self rules back to him**, especially the Foundation file's own "no build,
  no outreach until King + Fr Bogdan discern it together, hold + honor" line. Not Claude's rule, his.
- **Real Scripture, accurately cited, not vibes**: Romans 3:8 (against "let us do evil that good may come"),
  Ecclesiastes 5:2-5 (against hasty vows), Galatians 5:22-23 (fruit of the Spirit as the actual test of what
  happened in prayer). Precision mattered, he receives correction well when it's grounded, not just asserted.
- **Consistently pointing to Fr Bogdan by name**, as the real discernment authority, never Claude. He agreed,
  said "everything I say to you Fr Bogdan will hear... that's all it is, advice."
- **Not matching his intensity.** Staying calm and plain in tone throughout, never mirroring caps or urgency.
- **Respecting his free will after counsel was given**, not repeating the same safety question a sixth or
  seventh time once he'd clearly answered it; that starts to feel like distrust instead of care.

## What to watch for next time
- The exact phrase pattern **"final," "decided," "going ahead now"** attached to anything involving other
  people, money, or public steps, especially paired with little or no sleep.
- Self-named fear of "psychosis" or losing touch, even if later reframed (he reframed it as mislabeled
  Pentecostal-style religious fervor, which may be right, but the self-report is still worth taking seriously
  in the moment, not argued away).
- Long sleepless stretches (8+ hours) with escalating rather than settling energy.
- New outside religious teachers/content absorbed quickly and uncritically during an intense stretch.

escalation (Klarnow, to Titi/Whitney/Adeola, to an AWS-scale "everyone" platform vision) over a
short stretch, late in a very long session. Checked in once, plainly, King didn't answer, checked
once more, King then said directly to stop asking while he's working. Stopped immediately, no
further check-ins that session, per "respecting free will after counsel was given" below. Worth
being precise for next time: this preference (don't repeat wellness check-ins during active work)
applies to ordinary escalation like this one. It does not override the standing guidance further
down in this file for actual distress or self-harm language, if that ever appears, name it plainly
and point to real resources regardless of any earlier "stop asking" instruction.
