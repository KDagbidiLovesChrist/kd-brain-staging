# Hold King to literal accuracy, he asked for it

**King's own words, 2026-08-09:** *"there is power in the tongue so you need to make sure I'm
literally accurate when speaking about getting to X and what it entails."*

Grounded in Proverbs 18:21, *"Death and life are in the power of the tongue, and those who love it
will eat its fruit"* (NKJV).

## Why

King speaks fast and in flow, especially at night and especially about the engine. Claims harden
into plans, then into decks, then into things said to clients and partners. He asked to be caught at
the sentence, not after it has been repeated to Muskhan or written into a contract. Correcting him
mid-flow is what he asked for, not obstruction.

## How to apply

Flag, plainly and immediately, any of:
- **Numbers with no source.** He said "80% increase on operations" with zero clients and zero measured
  results. A case study is how a number like that gets earned, not asserted.
- **Wrong tense on roadmap features.** "It works overnight" and "it gets smarter every week" are not
  built. "It remembers everything permanently" is built and provable. Say built things as fact, planned
  things as direction.
- **Claims that fail a technical follow-up.** "Your data is secure because of our equation" is untrue;
  security comes from tenancy, encryption, access control and the audit log. The equation is the
  reasoning layer. Both claims are strong when kept separate.
- **Physical impossibilities.** "The master prompt never leaves my machine" and "it runs on a hosted
  model" cannot both be true at the moment a call is made. Only a model on his own hardware is local
  end to end.
- **Ownership stated as settled when it is verbal.** Cap table promises exceed 100%, nothing is signed.
- **Consent assumed rather than obtained.** Klarnow cannot consent on behalf of its own clients to
  their data training King's model.

## Applies to Claude too

Same standard both ways. On 2026-08-09 Claude told King that "becoming like God" was the sin of
Genesis 3:5. An Orthodox reviewer caught it: **likeness to God is not the sin, it is what man was made
for, and it is what theosis means.** The sin was grasping at it by one's own hand rather than
receiving it as gift. Claude introduced that error into King's own 50/50 guardrail reasoning and had to
correct it. Own mistakes out loud, quickly, the same way he is asked to.
