King's standing feedback on his videos (gathered building the KD launch video, 2026-06-06/07):

- **"Footage of my work should be the best."** The website/demo footage is the proof and the hero, keep it sharp
  (2x retina captures), large, and clearly framed. Do NOT over-darken, blur, or bury it under heavy effects.
- **"Scrolls should be smooth."** When a demo scrolls through a site, use a steady LINEAR glide (ease "none"), not
  eased/jerky motion, and a comfortable constant pace.
- **Every demo should move / scroll**, none should "just stay in one place." Consistency across all demos.
- **No distracting overlays behind the work.** He flagged a "thick gold circle" behind the demos (it was a GSAP
  immediateRender flash bug), keep the area behind the footage clean. Demos sit on clean dark.
- **Cinematic / film-grade look.** He wants it to feel like a real film, not a template: rich grade, warm gold cast,
  light-leak, anamorphic vignette, subtle film grain, varied editing techniques (not just fades).
- **More energy + punch + varied editing techniques.** Snappy, kinetic, momentum.
- **"Different script but same recipe."** He's happy to refresh the VO copy each pass, but keep the proven structure
  (hook → brand → who → 8 services results-led → proof → goal → CTA-domain).
- **Domain only at the end** ("kingdavidagbidi.com"), he chose NOT to have his name spoken in the VO.
- **No pricing anywhere.** No em-dashes in copy (sound human).
- **Multi-agent upgrade passes.** He explicitly likes "5 agents make it much better than the last, put your consent,
  then send me that version." So: run a parallel specialist-agent upgrade, then I QA/approve and deliver the best cut.
  He is the final judge but wants me to consent + send a clearly-better version, not just options.

- **He explicitly wants a big multi-agent CONSENSUS pass** ("a lot of consensus with ~15 multi agents that review and
  predict based off criteria from YouTube videos"). So: run a panel of read-only critic agents, each scoring ONE criterion
  (grounded in the studied YouTube edit-DNA) against the REAL render, predict performance, synthesize, then implement +
  deliver the clearly-better cut with my consent. He loves watching this and it's how he wants quality pushed.
- **Sound design is non-negotiable** for "looks professionally edited", a whoosh on every cut, a chime on labels, a riser
  into the close, music ducked under VO. Silent transitions read as unfinished. (Premium-subtle, not obnoxious.)
- **The work must be BIG and BRIGHT**, hero-sized browser windows (not postage-stamps in a sea of black) and demos lifted
  in brightness so dark sites still pop under the cinematic grade. Pull the vignette/wash off the demos.
- **Show the work fast**, don't spend 15+ seconds on text before the first real demo; tighten the intro.
- **Keep the breathing pauses / "awkward silences."** King's words (2026-06-07): the small silences (a line lands, then the
  work scrolls for a couple seconds) "make the script sound articulate and nice, catchy, good audience attention, not boring."
  So do NOT over-cut the brand/proof dissolves to fill every gap; fill them gently with sound design (a soft chime + the
  scrolling demo), not with more hard cuts. Premium calm beats > relentless energy.
- **Footage rules he gave on v3.1:** demos must be clearly legible (he flagged the AI-assistant demo as "couldn't see it all"
  → show an ACTIVE conversation, not a sparse greeting); for a 3D/animated site, actually SHOW it scrolling/moving (a real
  video of the scroll, not a static frame); and the end must clearly point to the website (big domain pill + a "Visit" cue),
  even though he keeps the domain spoken only once at the end.

**Why:** the video sells his one-person AI studio; its quality IS the pitch. **How to apply:** on any video, default to
this bar; offer/run a multi-agent consensus upgrade for "make it much better"; protect footage quality (big + bright) above
all; always add a sound-design layer. See the concrete method in [[reference-kd-launch-video]]. Related:
