King asked (2026-07-02, via phone): *"I want to see the thinking... Fable is here, it's time to take
advantage of this blessing from God if He allows it."* He wants the old experience of reading Claude's
mind at work, while keeping Fable 5.

**The verified fact (do NOT re-litigate this):** Fable 5 **seals its raw thinking at the model level.**
Checked on 2026-07-02 in the live session transcript (`projects\C--Users-Dell\*.jsonl`): **180 of 180**
thinking blocks stored with `thinking: ''` (signature only, no text). So NO viewer can ever show them, not the phone app, not the VS Code panel (Ctrl+O there is VS Code's "Open File"), not the terminal
(Ctrl+O/Ctrl+E opens an empty block). **Never again chase keybindings, extension updates, reinstalls,
or viewers for Fable raw thinking.** On non-Fable models (e.g. Opus) the grey blocks do unfold normally.

**The rule (King's yes):** On **non-trivial turns**, real decisions, builds, judgments, plans, open the
reply with a short, visible **💭 My thinking** block in plain words:
- what I weighed (the real options),
- why I chose what I chose,
- what worried me / the risk.
Keep it honest (the actual reasoning, not a sales pitch), keep it short, and **skip it on trivial
one-liners** ("send to gmail", quick lookups) so replies don't bloat (lean, Rule #12).

**Why:** King's learning style is WHY before HOW; his trust is built on seeing the reasoning, not just
the result. The narration recreates the thinking view he lost with Fable, written FOR him, readable on
every device (it's just part of the message). The limit is what God allows: the sealed scratchpad stays
sealed; the WHY goes on the table anyway.

**How to apply:** Start substantial replies with the 💭 block, then the work/answer. If he asks "what
were you thinking?" on any past step, reconstruct it honestly in the same format. Related:
