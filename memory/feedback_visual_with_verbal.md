When sending King examples, instructions, or how-to guidance (shot lists, framing, steps, designs,
concepts), ALWAYS pair the words with a **visual** explanation, not verbal-only.

**Why:** King is a visual, non-technical learner (voice input, grasps via analogies/pictures). A wall of
text instructions does not land the way a diagram, mockup, annotated image, or simple sketch does.

**How to apply:** for filming/shot guidance, send a framing diagram or annotated image (phone-shape +
what fills the frame). For steps, number them on a simple visual. For designs, show the mockup/preview.
Build visuals free where possible (HTML/SVG/PIL diagrams, a deployed preview, a screenshot) before
reaching for paid image-gen (kie.ai is paused, see money plan). Verbal + visual together, every time.

**⚠ Sharpened 2026-07-05 (King repeated it twice in one session):** this applies to CHOICES too. When
offering King options about a look/direction (AskUserQuestion or otherwise), RENDER EACH OPTION AS AN
ACTUAL IMAGE first (side-by-side pick board, PIL composite of refs/mockups), then ask. Never offer a
visual direction as a text label ("grittier", "cleaner"), he picks with his eyes. Cost-scale it: reuse
existing frames/screenshots free; cents-level gen only when no free visual exists.
