# Social SEO + distribution layer (TSV machine, part 3)

**King, 2026-06-26:** still need the SEO/distribution piece, research + analytics tools, posting
times, audience, account setup, SEO across ALL platforms, **built + opted-in platform by platform
at King's own pace.** This is the third part of the machine after TSV (attention) and the
storyboard pipeline (produce). Social platforms are now SEARCH engines, so discoverability is a
skill, not luck. Ties to [[learning-digital-product-viral-sales-model]] +
[[reference-visual-production-pipeline]]. Lean-spend: free tools first ([[feedback-lean-spend-llama-routing]]).

## The 7 parts of the distribution layer
**A. Accounts & profiles (per platform, at his pace).** Pick platforms per project/niche (NOT all
at once). Set: consistent handle, logo/pic, **keyword-rich bio** (what you do + for whom + the
search terms), **link-in-bio to the offer/store** (the funnel), and switch to **Business/Creator
account** (unlocks analytics + scheduling). ONE niche per account (algorithm rule).

**B. Keyword/SEO research (what people actually type).** Free tools: TikTok search autocomplete +
**TikTok Creative Center** (trends/keywords/top ads), YouTube search autocomplete + **YouTube
Studio "Research" tab**, **Google Trends**, AnswerThePublic (a few free/day), IG search bar,
Keywords Everywhere (freemium). Method: build a **hook + keyword bank** per niche from the phrases
the audience searches. The winning Title (TSV "T") doubles as the top keyword.

**C. SEO on every post (make each one discoverable).** Same phrase **said + shown + written** (TSV
rule): spoken keyword in the first 3s, on-screen text keyword, caption keyword + a question/CTA,
**hashtags = 3-5 specific + 1 broad**, keyword in the title/filename/alt where supported.

**D. Analytics (measure + learn).** Native: TikTok Analytics, IG Insights, YouTube Studio. Free
cross-platform aggregator: **Metricool (free tier)**. Track: views, **retention/watch-time**,
follows-per-view, profile visits, and **link clicks = the real money KPI**. Judge after **30 vids /
30 days**, not per video (TSV cadence rule).

**E. Timing & cadence.** **3-5 quality vids/wk, ONE niche, 30 days before judging** (NOT 1-2x/day).
Best times: use native "when followers are active" once you have data; Metricool/Buffer suggest
windows before that. **Batch-produce + schedule ahead** free via **Buffer (free) / self-host Postiz
/ Metricool**.

**F. Funnel hookup.** Every profile link-in-bio -> the offer/store; every video CTA points there;
track link clicks as the KPI that proves attention became money.

**G. Compliance.** **EU AI Act Art.50 binding 2 Aug 2026** = disclose + label AI content; **vary
every video** (templated AI = "inauthentic" = throttled). Carry this into every post.

## Paced rollout (King's speed, one platform at a time)
1. Pick **ONE project + ONE primary platform** (where its audience + the winners already live).
   Set the account up properly (Business acct, keyword bio, link-in-bio).
2. Build the **keyword + hook bank + content calendar** for that one niche.
3. **Produce** via the storyboard pipeline (TSV-modeled) + apply on-post SEO (part C).
4. Hook up **Metricool (free)**; post on the cadence; **measure after 30/30**.
5. Add a **SECOND platform** (repurpose the same content, platform-native SEO). Expand at his pace.

## Distribution is PLATFORM-SPECIFIC (King's insight 2026-06-26)
TSV as taught (Tony @tonymindol, TikTok) is **TikTok-tuned**. PRODUCE once, then **re-optimise per
platform** (same video, different levers). Model targets King flagged: **Tony @tonymindol** (TSV
source) + **Erika @erickamillann9** (UGC creator), pull their top vids into the swipe file.
| Platform | Rewards (2026) | The lever | Funnel role |
|---|---|---|---|
| **TikTok** | completion + replays + watch-time; pushes to **cold strangers** (followers matter least) | strongest HOOK, high completion, trending sound, keyword caption | **COLD REACH / go viral** |
| **Instagram Reels** | **sends-via-DM = most weighted** + saves > likes; shows to people who already engage first | make it DM-shareable + saveable + trending audio + clean look; caption keywords | **COMMUNITY / warm + capture** |
| **YouTube Shorts** | retention + **indexed for SEARCH** (title/desc/tags); shelf life of months/years | keyword title/desc/tags; funnel to a longer walkthrough + subscribe | **SEARCH-DRIVEN SALES / evergreen** |
| **Facebook Reels** | shares in groups; older audience | local + community angle | local (Aunty) |
| **Pinterest** | pure search, evergreen, buyer-intent | keyword pins | evergreen digital-product sales |
Engagement 2026: TikTok ~3.15% vs YT Shorts ~0.40% -> **TikTok = reach, YouTube = search/evergreen
sales, Instagram = community/capture.** Pick platforms by funnel role, not all-at-once.
