# Keep the hub in sync · proactively, without being reminded

**The rule:** King's **main hub** (`Website Builder\kd-hub\` → kd-hub-bay.vercel.app → kingdavidagbidi.com)
is his front door. Whenever we ship a new flagship asset, a video (e.g. the hub ad), a new demo, new
positioning/copy, a new gig, **immediately update the hub AND redeploy it as part of "done."** Don't wait to
be told.

**Why:** On 2026-06-02 King had to ask *"is the hub updated with the new video and context? do I always have to
remind you about that?"*, I'd updated the hub's hero *copy* but never embedded the new hub-ad *video*. He
shouldn't have to chase that. The hub going stale = the front door not showing our best work to buyers.

**How to apply:**
- New flagship video → embed it on the hub (centerpiece) + redeploy. (Hub ad embedded `kd-hub\index.html` `#overview`, mp4 web-optimized into `kd-hub\`.)
- New positioning/angle (e.g. "Leverage AI to grow your business online") → update hub hero + meta + redeploy.
- New demo/gig → add a tile/link on the hub.
- Treat the hub as a **living front door**; "shipped" isn't done until the hub reflects it.
- Same applies to the **video gig page** (video-samples) and any other public surface that should show the latest.
